The former command center stacked non-additive views (five-distributor replenishment €, 18-market GPS opportunity, PMS capture rates, price-list guardrails) without keeping their denominators separate. Those charts and tables are removed from this page. Use the suite links below.
Scope — Replenishment pages cover five distributors with dealer sales files (ITUK · UTI · IRELAND · IUK · GREECE). That is not the full 18-market network. UTI≠UMI · IUK≠ITUK · IRELAND≠IUK.
Non-additive — Reference-price exposure €, target-spend opportunity €, capture % and price-list ambition use different bases. Do not sum figures across suite pages.
Reference dealer price — Where replenishment pages value a qty gap: ISZE list × 1.40 to the dealer × 1.275 to the customer price we use as reference dealer price (PR00 × 1.785 in the data).
Period — Replenishment subset is CY2025 unless a linked page states otherwise.
