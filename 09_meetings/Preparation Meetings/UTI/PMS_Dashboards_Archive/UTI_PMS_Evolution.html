Methodology — not IML. UTI cover uses PRISM N75: 0.5 replacement events/vehicle/yr × 70% addressable workshop share, plus category factors (oil/fuel 1.0×, air 0.5×, brake pad 0.5×, clutch 0.1–0.2×, etc.). IML v7 (1.25× filters, 100% addressable) is the ITUK headline only — do not apply to UTI. All cover % charts are capped at 100%; hover for true rate when billing overshoots the normative ceiling (+).
