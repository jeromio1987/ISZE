## 3. Summary of discussion

### 3.1 Campaign scope and timeline
- Phase 1: N75 fleet (dealers with workshops / repeated touch points).
- Phase 2: N35 (and further external / out-of-network opportunity) — later; not the immediate launch focus.
- Target launch: notify / drive from September, go-live October 2026, with pilot / scale through late 2026 into 2027.
- Communication tools discussed: one-page bulletin, monthly league table, follow-up via network development managers.
- Management headline target referenced by ISZE: +1% PMS share by value (standard distributor target). ISZJ noted that, depending on focus parts, a higher stretch may be appropriate once baselines are known.

### 3.2 ITUK operating model (Duncan)
- Core focus categories: oil and fuel filters; brakes (condition-based); clutch (VIN / application specific); air filter as incremental / inspection-based.
- Dealer flow: confirm contract maintenance; prepare parts for scheduled service; service advisor authorisation; technician check sheet; no unnecessary parts fitted only for cycle.
- Baselines: per-dealership baselines from ITUK 2025 parts sales / UIO for selected part numbers; incentivise uplift from baseline.
- KPIs: selected PMS part quantity & value vs baseline; air-filter inclusion (if kept); workshop retention / reminders; contract-maintenance penetration; parts availability / back orders; VIN part selection accuracy.
- Support requested from ISZE / ISZJ: commercial / SPA / BVP support, especially N75 brakes.

### 3.3 Maintenance packages (Tim) — context
- Package evolution (Connected / Essential / Complete) and five-year warranty / connected pathway explained as ITUK commercial context.
- Essential covers PMS-type activity (filters, safety inspections) but not brakes; Complete is full R&M.
- This was background to explain why TCO and service intervals matter; it is not the PMS parts campaign itself.

### 3.4 ISZE coverage view (Jerome)
- N75 park figure used: 9,359 units (DVLA / Retax 2024). ITUK challenged full-park vs ~10-year operable park; Jerome acknowledged and proposed possibly showing both figures.
- Theoretical service rate discussed at 40,000 km (~1.25 services/year) for filter math — with caveats on air filter.
- Indicative coverage: oil filter ~40–45%; fuel filter higher at ITUK→dealer (~63%) than ISZE→ITUK (~31%) — likely timing / production-order effect; air filter ~20%.
- Genuine / BVP mix: oil and fuel already have BVP share; air filter showed no BVP sales in the period reviewed.
- Brake pads: condition-based (operator-licence safety inspections, commonly ~every 8 weeks). SPA preferred while waiting for BVP.
- Clutch: manual share likely ~20% (not ~47%); Duncan to confirm. Potential lower priority vs air / brakes.

### 3.5 Air filter — agreed understanding
- Forcing air filter replacement at every 40,000 km is not realistic for UK R&M / TCO.
- ITUK practice: check and replace when necessary (aligned with maintenance planner / indicator logic for P700).
- Air filter should not be pushed as a fixed every-service item in Phase 1.
- If a filter “pack” is used: supply as grouped parts to the dealer, with dealer confirming need — not a forced kit.

### 3.6 AdBlue filter
- Demand skewed by recent AdBlue rework (~6,000 vehicles). Leave AdBlue quantities out of the PMS campaign metrics for now.

### 3.7 Brakes — main commercial focus
- Brake price competitiveness is the #1 dealer complaint and a barrier to truck sales and aftersales perception.
- ITUK margin on N75 brake pads already ~18% after logistics — no further ITUK cut available; needs ISZE / ISZJ support.
- BVP brake parts: production / launch from Japan around December 2026; realistic availability toward end of Q1 2027.
- Until BVP: proceed with SPA on genuine as a temporary bridge.
- Acceptable gap vs aftermarket discussed as roughly 15–20%; current gap is larger.
- SPA may protect existing volume (stop decline), not only force large volume/profit jumps — case-by-case with ISZE management; ISZJ can support ISZE if required.

### 3.8 Clutch
- ISZJ suggested lower focus (limited potential vs filters / brakes).
- Kept as secondary / annual promo track (see Decision 5).
