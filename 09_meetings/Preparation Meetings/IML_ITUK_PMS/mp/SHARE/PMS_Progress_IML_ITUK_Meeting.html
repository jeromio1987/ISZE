0b How PMS is being run (shared picture)

Same programme, two roles — so the meeting stays aligned on method.

IML — study & promotion design

1Demand = UIO × km/yr × replacement frequency by FIG-KEY / category
2Cover % = actual sales ÷ that expected demand (IML service schedule)
3Select promotion basket → sales promotion activities
4Face-to-face to finalise (UK visit · EU conference · Japan Aug)

ITUK — data & execution

1Supply UIO / R&M / dealer sell-out (practical UK formats)
2Review replacement frequencies → target items → promotion plan
3Phase-one scope: N75 · brake + air first · clutch deferred
4PMS as one lever; also higher-value categories + dealer engagement
