The incentive is then paid only on sales above that baseline.

2030 Growth Target Incentive
Where the opportunity sits
 
Isuzu’s IX 2030 plan targets ¥6tn sales and a transformation to a solutions company. In 
the UK, the corresponding lever is converting our parc (UIO) into incremental parts 
revenue.
 
The 2026 plan moves ITUK parts sales from £12.87m (2025) to £13.13m, a 77% share 
of UIO opportunity. The stretch idea pushes that share to 80% by adding £508,758 of 
incremental parts sales.
 
Half of the gross profit on that stretch, £62,052 is offered back by ISZE as a kickback 
we can deploy as a dealer incentive.
