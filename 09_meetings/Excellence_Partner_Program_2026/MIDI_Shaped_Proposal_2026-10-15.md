# A MIDI-shaped offer for ISZE distributors

**One fixed extra on a short list of service parts, paid to the distributor, only while the file names the dealer** · Internal draft · 15 October 2026

Prepared by the ISZE parts-sales team with AI assistance. For a decision with Bart before anything goes to management. This draft does not replace the two papers of 9 and 10 October. The discount paper is *Isuzu Excellence Partner Program* (9 October). The other route is *Isuzu Universe without a discount* (9 October, figures corrected 10 October).

A figure with no label in this draft is an error. **[M]** is counted in a named file. **[I]** is a conclusion from those figures. **[P]** is a proposal. **[U]** is unknown.

---

## 1. The decision

MIDI already runs this. Their letter to the network is dated 23 May 2024 and reached us on 14 October 2026. The programme is open in Italy, France and Spain. The current count is 22 ambassadors in Italy, 8 in France and 4 in Spain. The criteria and the incentives have not changed. IDS is reviewed once a year. Data and training are reviewed every quarter. **[M]** (the letter and the covering note)

MIDI invoices the dealer. The extra 2% therefore lands on the company that holds the stock, the workshop and the sales. ISZE invoices the distributor. A discount on the distributor's whole stock order pays the distributor, and a file with no dealer name still qualifies. That is the shape of the 9 October discount paper: about 2.5% or 5% on all stock orders, or a smaller hybrid. On the numbers in that paper the full version costs about €0.93 million a year if all 19 distributors are in, and the hybrid about €0.29 million, against €0.43 million of rebate credits booked in FY2026. **[M]**

This draft keeps MIDI's shape and changes who is paid.

**The offer.** One level. A distributor that qualifies gets 2% off its stock orders on a short list of service families, on top of the kickback it already has. The rate does not rise with volume. The file is checked every quarter. IDS is checked once a year. A distributor that does not qualify does not get the 2%. The kickback stays as it is until the contract text is in the workspace. **[P]**

**What it costs, if everyone who buys these parts qualified.** In FY2026 the named families were €2,364,871 of stock orders across the 19 distributors. Two percent of that is **€47,297** a year. The first seven distributors account for €1,636,157, and 2% of that is **€32,723**. **[M]**

That is smaller than the €57,600 already attached to the data condition in the kickback, and about a tenth of the rebate credits booked in FY2026. It is the ceiling. There is no second ceiling of €150,000 to €400,000 in this draft. **[I]**

## 2. Why the dealer version does not transfer

MIDI's letter gives a qualifying dealer three things: an extra 2% on spare parts in categories 11, 16 and 17 from 2025, a look at stock with a once-a-year right to return parts bought within two years at the purchase price, and automatic recording of servicing. In return the dealer sends three files: stock, workshop orders, and spare parts sales. The sales file is the part code, the date it left stock, the quantity, and whether the sale was over the counter or on a workshop job. There is no price in the file. The programme is voluntary. **[M]**

The letter does not say what categories 11, 16 and 17 are. They are not mapped here. **[U]**

ISZE cannot put 2% on a dealer invoice. The dealer is the distributor's customer. If a distributor wants its own scheme for its dealers, that scheme is paid from the distributor's margin. Dealers are approached only through their distributor, as the Universe paper already says.

Three pieces of MIDI's letter stay out of this draft.

- Workshop job files and chassis numbers stay out. The Universe paper ruled them out on 10 October, and ISZE does not run the workshop.
- A standing right to return parts at the purchase price stays out. MIDI can offer it because MIDI sold the part to the dealer. For ISZE it is a separate cost.
- Stock on hand stays out of the first file. It can be a later file. The first file is sales.

France, Spain and Italy stay out. MIDI already runs the dealer programme there. Ukraine, Armenia and Kazakhstan stay out, as in the October papers. UTI and UMI are separate. Isuzu UK pickup and Isuzu Truck UK are separate.

## 3. What qualifies

The file is one a month, within ten working days of month-end, the same timing as the October papers. It names the distributor, the dealer, the Isuzu part number and the quantity, and it states the month. Quantity is negative for returns. There is no price in the file. **[P]**

A network total does not qualify. Isuzu Truck UK sent a per-dealer file for 2025 and a network total for 2026. Germany is recorded as sending part number, quantity and date every month since May 2025, with no dealer name. Neither file qualifies until it names the dealer. Isuzu UK pickup's monthly feed is part number and quantity, with no dealer. That file does not qualify either. **[M]** (the October papers, from the August 2026 sales-review minutes and the files in the workspace)

The quarterly check is a quality check: on time, complete, per dealer, and the declaration of what the file covers. It is not a count of rows. IDS once a year is the standards check, as MIDI uses the green cards and the audit score. This draft does not invent a new dealer score. It uses the IDS round the distributor already runs. **[P]**

The 2% is set after the quarter, on the stock orders of the named families in that quarter. It comes on top of the kickback. It does not replace the 20% data condition inside the kickback. That condition stays until the contract is read. **[P]**

## 6. What the distributor gets back

In the same quarter as the file, the distributor gets a reading of its own business: what ISZE billed, what the file says was sold on, in euros, by these families, with warranty on its own line. The reading is a document. It is not a login to the pricing tool. The tool is not ready for a distributor to open, as the Universe paper recorded on 10 October. **[P]**

The reading does not rank distributors and does not name another distributor's dealers. Dealer standards stay the distributor's own IDS answers.

The test is the one the Universe paper adopted. If the 2% stopped, would the distributor keep sending the file because the quarterly reading would get worse without it? A file that exists only to collect the 2% fails that test. The 2% is small enough that this question is real. **[P]**

---

## 7. What we recommend

1. Use this offer as the discount route, in place of 2.5% and 5% on all stock orders. The cost at full qualification is €47,297 a year on FY2026 buying, not €0.29 million or €0.93 million. **[P]**
2. Pay it only for a file that names the dealer. Start with UTI, Isuzu Truck UK, Portugal, Germany, Ireland, Isuzu UK pickup and Greece, and pay each one only from the quarter the file qualifies. **[P]**
3. Leave the kickback contract alone until the text is in the workspace. **[P]**
4. Keep the Universe route as the document the distributor receives. Do not wait for a hosted login before the first quarterly reading. **[P]**
5. Leave workshop files, chassis numbers, stock on hand and a standing returns right out of the first version. **[P]**
