## Verdict (read this first)

1. **2025 was not “BVP failed to save genuine.”** CY wallet −€64K is **101% genuine-only**. BVP only +€2.3K. Genuine with BVP alternative + BVP (the substitutable arena) flat (~€47K).
2. **97% of that genuine drop is outside the PMS service basket.** PMS genuine −€1.7K; non-PMS genuine ≈ −€62K. Inside PMS, BVP +€5.8K and basket **total grew**.
3. **The FY24→FY25 killers are lumpy / sensor / assembly lines** — not oil filters walking to IAM: ENGINE ASM (−€15K), two PM sensors (−€19K combined), ENGINE PARTIAL (−€6K), STEERING UNIT (−€5K), TRANSMISSION ASM (−€3.5K), DPD ASMs (−€4K). Electrical sensors group −34%.
4. **GPS peaked CY2024 at ~42%, then 35.4% in CY2025** (SPU €105 → €88). Fleet flat. Numerator failure.
5. **FY2026 headline GPS 37.1% is soft-pedalled commercial truth.** Fiscal warranty €102K = **30% of FY26 billing**. Ex-warranty GPS ≈ **26%** on CY25 wallet.
6. **AIM is not the open.** High-risk AIM 2.1% of sales (€7K). Quadrant stays *Priority target (low GPS × low PMS)*. Street undercut is catalog exposure (€86K Eucon), not observed walk-out.
7. **Brake pads are the local exception:** genuine qty 53→33, BVP 7→70, BVP € share 9%→63%, total pad volume **up**. Category substitution ≠ wallet story.
