Dealer questions

Same list at Rolmax, Auto Reno Paços, Socarros. Ask in plain words. Write notes on paper if you need.

1. Where sales leave Isuzu

  1. Where is the biggest loss of sales for Isuzu in your area — which jobs leave the network, and why?
  2. On which part families do customers most often ask for something cheaper than genuine (filters, brake pads, clutch, body, oils…)?
  3. When a customer refuses genuine, what do they buy instead — Chinese, European aftermarket, or another OEM?
  4. Roughly what share of your workshop jobs still use Isuzu genuine parts end-to-end?
  5. Is the leak more on price, on delivery time, or on “the customer already has a preferred brand”?

2. What Isuzu (OEM) and I-Motors can do better

  1. What can Isuzu, as the manufacturer, do better for you on parts?
  2. What can I-Motors do better — stock, delivery, returns, pricing support, training, communication?
  3. If you could change one thing next quarter that would grow genuine parts sales here, what would it be?
  4. Are list prices to the customer competitive on the families that matter most to you? Where are you under pressure?
  5. Do you get enough technical / parts support when a difficult job comes in?

3. Parts — stock, order, substitute

  1. When a part is not on the shelf at I-Motors, what do you do — wait, buy outside, or substitute? How often last month?
  2. Which parts leave you waiting the longest (name the family or job type)?
  3. On brake pads and discs for D-Max / NLR — can you still win the job on genuine, or does the customer walk?
  4. Same for clutch work — kit vs separate pieces; who usually wins the price conversation?
  5. Filters and oils — do fleets bring their own, or do you sell from stock?
  6. Body / accident parts — how often do you go outside the Isuzu channel?
  7. Do you hold stock here, or pull from another site / I-Motors central warehouse?

4. Service, workshop, R&M

  1. How are parts and service sales this year versus last year? What moved?
  2. Do you sell repair & maintenance contracts or extended warranty, or only when the customer asks?
  3. When Allianz (or another roadside) sends a truck, do you get the job — or does it go elsewhere?
  4. What stops more customers from taking an R&M / service contract with you?
  5. Do you offer 24/7 roadside or after-hours parts access? If not, what would it take?
  6. Do you report workshop volume / maintenances back to I-Motors today?

5. DPD / DPF — refurbished or exchange

  1. When a D-Max DPD fails, what do you do today — new genuine, aftermarket, or off-car cleaning?
  2. What about refurbished / cleaned DPDs — would that be something you would participate in?
  3. What do you (or the customer) typically pay all-in for cleaning or exchange today, if you use it?
  4. Would you send a sealed can to a hub in France (about 2–3 days) if collection and return were arranged?
  5. What downtime can a workshop accept before they just fit a new unit?
  6. Who cleans DPFs in Portugal or near the Spanish border today, if anyone?

6. Maintenance data, fleet, planning

  1. Are you willing to share maintenance / workshop data with Isuzu or I-Motors in exchange for something useful (stock priority, pricing support, training, reports)? What would that exchange need to be?
  2. Do you know how many Isuzu vehicles you actively service in your area (your local park)? How do you keep that list?
  3. How do customers usually service — by km interval, by time, or only when something breaks?
  4. For pickups vs trucks, what annual kilometres do you typically see?
  5. Which fleets in your area matter most for parts — and are they loyal to the network?
  6. If we modelled “lost sales” from park × service interval, what would we get wrong for your market?

7. IT systems and ordering

  1. How do you order parts from I-Motors today — portal, EDI, phone, WhatsApp, email?
  2. What is frustrating in the ordering / VIN / catalogue tools you use?
  3. Can you see stock at I-Motors before you order? Is that reliable?
  4. Do returns and credit notes work cleanly, or do they block sales?
  5. What IT change would most help you sell more genuine parts?
  6. Do you use any aftermarket catalogue (TecDoc etc.) alongside Isuzu tools — and when does that win?

8. People, training, standards

  1. Do your technicians get enough Isuzu training on newer models / emissions?
  2. What would make the parts counter more confident recommending genuine?
  3. Looking at dealer standards (IDS) — which gaps are real for you, and which are paperwork?
  4. If we agree three improvements from this visit, what should they be — and who owns them?

Close
