Distributor questions — I-Motors / Astara

Wednesday Lisbon · Nuno + Luís. Force answers. Bring dealer-floor notes.

1. Where sales leave Isuzu in Portugal

  1. Where is the biggest loss of sales for Isuzu in Portugal — which part of the park, which jobs, which channel?
  2. Of the vehicles in service, roughly what share comes to the official network for maintenance — and what share never does?
  3. If we estimate demand from park × service interval, what do we get wrong for Portugal (km/year, DIY, Chinese parts, multi-brand workshops…)?
  4. On clutch, brake shoes/pads, filters — is the gap real leakage, campaign timing, or stock policy?
  5. Where do dealers buy when you are slow or expensive — and do you see that?
  6. Do you accept GPS capture ~63.3% as the working FY figure, or do you see mid-50%s once warranty is parked?

2. What Isuzu (OEM) can do better

  1. What can Isuzu, as the manufacturer, do better for I-Motors on parts commercial terms, SPA, and supply?
  2. What can Isuzu do better on R&M / warranty product shape so you can win tenders and fleets?
  3. What should we stop asking you for that does not help the business?
  4. If Japan / ISZE could unlock one pricing or programme lever this FY, what should it be?

3. Parts sales, fill rate, stock operations

Sep: fill 81.0% (Aug 85.4%), MOS 2.49, BO lines 495 (Aug 282), sales to dealers €417k (Aug €304k).

  1. What drove the Sep fill miss versus August — which families or part numbers?
  2. Are the 495 back-order lines temporary catch-up or structural under-stock?
  3. When does MOS get back to about 3.0 without killing fill rate again?
  4. Which parts do dealers complain about most — delivery or price?
  5. August order intake looked soft — temporary summer, or structural?
  6. How do you decide safety stock by family today?

4. Sales to dealer data

  1. Can we agree a go-live date for a monthly sales-to-dealer feed?
  2. Which columns can you extract — invoice date, part number, quantity, dealer code, optional stock snapshot?
  3. What blocks dealer-level grain if it is not available today?
  4. Who owns the file each month on your side?

5. SPA and competitive price

  1. Which part families will you propose for SPA volume (where a better price raises volume)?
  2. On which families are Portuguese workshops under strongest aftermarket / Chinese pressure?
  3. Can you table local dealer or workshop buy prices on the families you care about most?
  4. Which lines are already on SPA or special terms today?
  5. Clutch kits versus separate clutch parts — what is the real story on the ~20% drop you were checking?

6. R&M, tenders, roadside (Luís)

200+ tender units 2026: Lisbon 29 NLR, Madeira 4 NPR, IP 96 D-Max, REN 15 D-Max with service, CV year-3 by I-Motors, Arval developing. Allianz roadside during warranty.

  1. Which of REN / IP / Lisbon / Madeira / Arval still needs an ISZE parts decision before delivery or go-live?
  2. Can you table a named case for optional year-3 cover (product shape + euro ask) — not a blanket Japan 3-year?
  3. Who owns tender early-warning to ISZE so we are not guessing from public portals?
  4. What blocks R&M volume today — price, product, Allianz appetite, dealer capability?
  5. Do dealers actually receive Allianz roadside jobs, or does the network miss them?
  6. How many active CV and LCV R&M contracts now (update since July: then ~12 CV / ~29 LCV)?

7. Lubricants

  1. Status of central purchasing / Shell path — what is decided, what is open?
  2. Do dealers still buy oil outside because of price or logistics?

8. Dealer network and IDS

  1. From the three visits — which gaps should be an I-Motors network standard (workshop reporting to you, roadside, AED…), and which stay dealer coaching?
  2. You called Auto Reno “average” while IDS ranks it top of Portugal — what does “average” mean for you (volume, loyalty, genuine mix)?
  3. Multi-site dealers (central stock) — how should we read weak stock metrics on secondary sites?
  4. What would make dealers more loyal to genuine parts?

9. IT systems, EDI, data exchange

  1. What EDI / system capabilities do you already use with ISZE, and what is unused potential?
  2. What IT / parts-data requirements do you still need from us (PN ↔ series, superseded, maintenance vs extended…)?
  3. Are you willing to share richer maintenance / sell-through data if we give something concrete in return — what would that exchange be?
  4. Status of the aftersales hours questionnaire (due end July) — done or stuck?
  5. Obsolete / scrap-risk stock list for cross-distributor matching — can we have it?
  6. BCS F-series adjustable brackets — still needed?

10. DPD / refurbished (distributor view)

  1. Do your dealers already clean or exchange D-Max DPDs, and through whom?
  2. Would a France-hub exchange be usable for Portugal if collection / return and a cleaning price sat well under new?
  3. Would I-Motors want to participate commercially, or only observe for now?
