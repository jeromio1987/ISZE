1. Verdict

I was wrong earlier: Nuno did send a back-order file. It is not a single snapshot — it is eight filled month-ends (Jan–Aug 2026). The KPI “B/O lines” number is simply the row count of that extract. So when fill rate was fine in August (85.4%) with 282 BO lines, the file confirms it. When September jumped to 495 on the KPI, we still lack the Sep PN tab in the 29 Sep mail.

Inside the last filled month (31 Aug), the book is young in count (median age 6 days, 151 of 282 lines under 8 days) but old in euro: the 91+ day pocket is only 21 lines and holds €33,557 — almost half of August’s open euro. That pocket is mostly accident / body hardware that is not on our 6 Oct open sales-order book.

Against our open orders to sold-to 519 (6 Oct), only 52 part numbers sit in both lists. Most of their August euro (€60,479) is not on our open book now; most of our open euro (€137,518) was not on their August BO. That is expected with a five-week gap plus September’s intake — it does not mean the files are useless. It means Wednesday needs the Sep/Oct BO tab before anyone claims a clean supply match for the 495.
