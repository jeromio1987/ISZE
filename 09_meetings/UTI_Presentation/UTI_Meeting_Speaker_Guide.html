Fleet & R&M discovery — questions for UTI

Exploratory block (~10 min). Position as mutual learning — ISZE wants more distributors on structured R&M; their answers calibrate our models and programmes.

A. Fleet parc & R&M scope

  • Do you operate a UTI-owned fleet or R&M contract pool separate from dealer-retail parc? How many units?
  • What share of the Israeli Isuzu CV parc is under your R&M programme vs customer-maintained vs dealer-only?
  • Active vs registered parc — which do you use for planning? How often is parc data refreshed?
  • Are any major customers on full-service R&M contracts (parts + labour bundled)? Who holds the contract — UTI, the fleet customer, or a third party?
  • Typical fleet age profile — concentration in 3–5 yr or 5–8 yr bands? Any wave of replacements coming?

B. Service planning & intervals

  • Maintenance planning: km-based, calendar-based, or mixed? Typical km/year by segment (distribution, construction, municipal)?
  • Do workshop schedules follow Isuzu/IML intervals or local overrides?
  • How far ahead are service events planned — does that match the ~3-week parts lead time from IE?
  • Any telematics / predictive maintenance influencing parts demand?
  • Clutch and brake jobs — triggered at fixed km, inspection, or failure?

C. Parts sourcing & workshop behaviour

  • For fleet workshops: split of genuine Isuzu / BVP / UTI Parts / other by category (filters, brakes, clutch)?
  • What triggers non-Isuzu on wear items — price, lead time, contract policy, stock availability?
  • Scope of UTI Parts — which categories, dealer network only or fleet workshops too?
  • On oil filters specifically — why did FY26 volume drop while fuel/air grew? Fleet sourcing change?
  • Are R&M contracts priced with fixed parts allowances (absorbing ISZE price moves) or pass-through?

D. R&M programme & ISZE collaboration

  • Interest in expanding R&M coverage with Isuzu — more models, more customers, longer contract windows?
  • Would they share anonymised service volumes or intervals to calibrate PMS/GPS models for Israel?
  • Can fleet/R&M volumes explain FY26 clutch and oil-filter movement?
  • Appetite for SPA/programme pricing tied to fleet commitment on wear lines?
  • Who is the right counterpart for a follow-up fleet ops session (name/role)?

E. What we can offer back

  • Benchmark intervals from other CV markets (once calibrated — not ITUK assumptions verbatim).
  • Joint view of capture at workshop level if they share volumes.
  • Programme pricing on filters where genuine mix is the goal; BVP where price-sensitive.
