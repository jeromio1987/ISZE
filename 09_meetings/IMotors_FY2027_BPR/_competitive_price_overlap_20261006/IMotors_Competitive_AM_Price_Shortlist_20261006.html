1. Verdict

At basket level, Competitive parts sales to dealer (list €) is up, not down: FY Apr–Aug €263,192 → €324,107 (+23.1%). The contested AIM families move the same way. So the commercial story is not “competitive is collapsing” — it is a short list of part numbers that are both losing sales-to-dealer list € and sitting well above the aftermarket brand median on our reference dealer price.

Rule for a hit: sales to dealer list € dropping (FY Apr–Aug −15%+ or CY run-rate −20%+) and our reference dealer price more than 10% above the aftermarket brand median on that part (or its genuine/BVP pair). Ranked by impact = list € lost × overprice gap ratio. Combined list € lost on the 26 hits: €13,805.

Of those 26 hits, 9 also sit on supply stress we can name from our books: 4 still open on our sales orders to I-Motors (sold-to 519, EXPORT 6 Oct), and 5 only on Nuno’s BO extract earlier in 2026 (Jan–Aug tabs; Sep+ empty in the 29-Sep file). The other 17 have neither mark — price pressure without a current open-order / earlier-BO flag.

Coverage caveat: only 120 Competitive part numbers have an aftermarket brand price (31.7% of CY2025 competitive sales-to-dealer list € — €209,925 of €663,174). Most observed brand prices are DE / NO / UK listings, not Portuguese workshop purchase prices.

5. What this does not prove

  • That Portuguese workshops pay the Eucon/KS listing — those are foreign aftermarket observations.
  • That list € lost is all lost to aftermarket brands (stock, campaigns, and mix also move the line).
  • That every Competitive part is overpriced — most have no brand price on file.
  • OEM peer / Amayama grey as a substitute for aftermarket brand competition (kept in the full table as a secondary column only).
  • That an “Earlier BO 2026” mark equals a current fill-rate miss — Nuno’s Sep/Oct+ BO tabs were empty in the 29-Sep file; open-order marks use our 6 Oct EXPORT only.
Method note: Competitive = catalogue L1 “Competitive parts” (AIM remaps into Brakes / Suspension / …). Contested AIM L1 basket = Accident & Body, Brakes, Clutch, Maintenance Service, Suspension.
