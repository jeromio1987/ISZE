Story · 01

01 Why we built this

Public buyers across the ISZE footprint publish LCV / truck and fleet R&M notices every week. Until now there was no single Isuzu-relevant desk that turns that stream into actionable opportunities.

The problem

  • Tenders launch on TED (above threshold) and on national portals (most below-threshold volume).
  • Noise is high: passenger cars, SEND/route transport, tyres, postal panel vans.
  • GPS / MAP conversation needs a real buyer + geography + dealer path — not a scraped pile.

What the engine is

  • A rolling ~30-day scrape across TED + wired national feeds.
  • An Isuzu selection gate + product-fit gate so the desk shows CV that matters.
  • GPS € only when fleet is stated and the buy is pickup/truck-shaped with a real distributor map.
  • IDS dealer linkage for country → distributor → dealer (real rankings only).
SteerCo framing: this is a radar and a desk — not a forecast of network sales, and not a claim that stated fleet units equal addressable market.

Live working surface: Install v4 opportunity dashboard (six tabs). This pack is the narrative layer for decision-makers.

  • OUT — Pure tyres / towing / specials
