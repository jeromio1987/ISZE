Price revision simulator v2

Pick the weighted increase, choose which market conditions apply, then click any group, market or part to change its move. Everything recalculates at once on what the distributors bought from July 2025 to June 2026 (EUR 32.6M, 15,273 part numbers, 22 markets). It ends in a price file you can load. Nothing is published and no price list is touched.

Adjust by group or part

Click a name to open it. In the yellow box type 0 to hold today's price, a number such as 4 for exactly +4%, BP to follow the part's own base price step, BP+2 for two points more, or leave it empty for auto (the step is scaled so the whole book lands on your target). The part beats its group, and a group beats the one above it. With a price list selected above, your setting applies to that list only.
