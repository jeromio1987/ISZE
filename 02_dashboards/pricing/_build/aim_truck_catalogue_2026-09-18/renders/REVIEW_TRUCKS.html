What I can see, and what needs your call.
  • The front faces are blank. Headlights and grille are modelled but sit inside the body volume, so you see a plain panel where the face should be. This is a bug and it is the main reason the cab-overs read as generic box trucks.
  • The D-Max cab is too upright. The windscreen rake is nearly vertical and the bonnet is long and flat, so it reads as a light truck with a box on the back rather than a pickup. This is the weakest of the four.
  • The cab-overs have the right proportions but nothing identifies them: no grille shape, no badge, no windscreen contrast, flat cab sides.
  • Wheels are off. Rims too large and too bright; tyres read thin.
  • Angle. A three-quarter front view shows the face and reads far more like a truck, at the cost of hiding some clickable zone area on the far side.
