7 · Engine rules

  1. Benchmark = the highest-tier named IAM on the PN, not the cheapest listing.
  2. Ceilings: T4 +30% / T3 +60% / T2 +150% / T1 +250%. T1 never auto-cuts.
  3. Pads tighten T3/T4 only (T4 pad +15%, T3 pad +30%). Oil filters keep the full T4 +30%.
  4. Captive (no T3/T4, no Best Value in crossref) → hold. Incomplete crossref ≠ no Best Value in catalogue.
  5. Best Value in the crossref → push Best Value. Retention. Never a red cut.
  6. Proposed = quality IAM price × (1 + effective ceiling). Action = hold / review / SPA / cut-to-band / monitor.
  7. ISZE net = PR00. Projected DB / DLR / retail = net × 1.40 × 1.275 from the pricing-engine channel rule. IAM = trade or retail — flagged when not like-for-like.
  8. Eucon does not isolate the named brand — quality-end proxy is the survey maximum when T3/T4 is present.
  9. Assembly/kit vs mixed Eucon listing → auto-cut blocked (system completeness not confirmed).
  10. Mekonomen titles that are only a kronor figure, or a spring brand on a pad, are suspect.
  11. KS aftermarket at or below £2 is dropped. KS unnamed brands stay T1.
  12. Positive delta = cheaper than the comparison price. Hover every derived chip.
