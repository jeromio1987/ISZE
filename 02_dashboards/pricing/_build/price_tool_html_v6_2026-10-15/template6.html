PMS parts

Category, made in, weight band, part

PMS parts are the 484 part numbers of this base that sit in a PMS category: brake pads, shoes and rotors, clutch parts and five filter types. Click a name to open it: category, then where it is made (Japan IML or Thailand IMIT), then weight band, then the parts. A setting typed on a row works like on the price book tab: 0 holds today's price, 4 is exactly +4%, BP follows the part's own step, empty is auto. The part beats its group, the group beats its category, the category beats the AIM levels. The box in the second last column holds the category at today's price in every market where the distributor buys under 45% of what its trucks need.

If dealers buy less

Dealers react to a higher price by buying a little less. The response is the percent change in what they buy for +1% on our price. Click a case to use it in the tables below. Extra after the volume effect = billed euro x (1 + move) x (1 + response x move) minus billed euro, as in the revision workbook. No early buying before the date is assumed.

When it pays: the fiscal year

The figures on the tiles are a full year. A start date inside the year earns only the months from that date. Billing follows the monthly pattern of July 2025 to June 2026 in each market. Fiscal year 2027 runs 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. The effective date you pick is also the Valid From date of the SAP paste file.
