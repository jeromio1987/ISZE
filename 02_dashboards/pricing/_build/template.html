6 · Ten actions for next week

    Known caveats, stated up front.
    • ITUK has two published lanes: this page uses the network (PRISM) lane so all 18 markets are comparable. The official ITUK external numbers (IML v7 schedule: weighted cover 35.9%, €2.04M gap across 5 categories) use different intervals and UIO — directionally the same story, different absolutes. Don't mix the two in one document.
    • Air-filter capture rests on a provisional every-2nd-service interval assumption, not yet confirmed by IML.
    • Price samples are thin (Eucon 44 PNs, UK shop 39–42 PNs, scraped 9 May; UK/IL category scrapes 13 Jul). Premium medians are direction, not calibration.
    • Sweden: 5 of 6 aftermarket price hits from the Swedish chain scrape were page artifacts (identical price on unrelated parts) and were excluded; Sweden has no usable local price evidence this cycle.
