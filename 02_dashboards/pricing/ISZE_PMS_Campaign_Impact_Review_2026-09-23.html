PMS campaign impact review

Did deep under-list selling move volume — or was it stock stacking? Campaign tag = billed price ≥20% under your reference (ZPR1 for five customers, else list). PRD is colour only.
Built 2026-09-23 · Self-contained · Not a dealer elasticity study
Conclusions
  1. Campaign-shaped selling is real but not the whole PRD book. In 2025 about 8% of PMS euro was ≥20% under reference, while PRD was 28% of euro. Most PRD is replenishment; the deep price cut is the campaign slice.
  2. Stacking shows up clearly on IUK blower filters (and in spots on air filters): months with heavy quantity at ≥50% of euro under list, then a sharp drop in the following month. That fits “buy ahead because of the campaign,” not steady end demand.
  3. Sales impact is mixed by family. IUK air filters: total pieces still up hard in 2025 even after campaign months — growth plus cheaper mix. IUK blower filters: big campaign spikes then quiet months — more stock digestion than sustained sell-through.
