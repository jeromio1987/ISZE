01 · Full launch definition

Full launch = Layers 0–5 operational with: (1) every in-scope PMS category on oem_benchmark or iam_derived (no placeholder moving via base filler), (2) OEM peer street filled to gate where peers exist, (3) θ_parc + θ_interval wired, (4) eligibility excludes NO_LIST/SUPERSEDED from ISZE Δ, (5) PR00 + ZPR1/CIS rules as specified, (6) every line carries a reason code under the +3% weighted index.

Not required for full launch

  • Measured DB→DLR elasticity coefficient inside the solver (explicitly out of V1)
  • Volume response forecast (constant-units arithmetic until elasticity exists)

1 · Process contrast — crystal clear

How ISZE typically priced before

+3.0%
Revenue Δ €173,041 · constant units · uniform incidence
  • Uniform or near-uniform weighted list increase — the same % across much of the book when a +3% target was set.
  • Class factors set selling price from FOB (IML formula), but the revision itself was not rule-based by capture, street, or θ.
  • Little separation of IML buying-price pass-through versus the ISZE margin decision on the same list move.
  • Street reads often used the wrong pricing point historically (list vs PR00 × street factor 1.785) — Eucon lesson.
  • No brand-quality % ceilings (+30 / +60 / +150 / +250); no oem_benchmark gate; no logged allocation runs with reason codes.

What the engine does instead

+3.0000%
Revenue Δ €173,041 · constant units · differentiated incidence
  • Same +3.0000% weighted index — solver hits the target exactly under constant prior-year units.
  • Wallet zones (raise / contested / defend from PMS total capture) plus street evidence gates decide who funds vs who is cut.
  • Reason codes on every cell; caps (+8%, tighter on top-50) and floors; θ_parc with street veto.
  • Trusted premium basis required for position moves (oem_benchmark / iam_derived); hold-when-blind on NO_EVIDENCE.
  • Brand-quality ceiling ladder (+30/+60/+150/+250) in policy; run registry and alloc detail for every logged move.

4 · Blind weight — still partly the old world

What this means externally

  • Do not pitch “fully differentiated +3%” while ~23.8% of € is still on the unbenchmarked base.
  • Do pitch: evidenced lines are already ruled (raise / defend / contested); blind lines wait for street / OEM peers.
  • Filling near-gate OEM street (e.g. one genuine DAF quote) converts blind/near cats into oem_benchmark — that shrinks this slice.

9 · What you can tell a distributor

Short callouts — Mode A English, no internal nicknames.

Same index, fairer mix

We still deliver a +3% weighted list increase (€173,041 on €5,768,025). The engine does not invent a bigger bill — it changes which lines carry it.

Defend lines are not loaded like captive

Where genuine capture is already lost and street is above market, the engine cuts or holds (e.g. IUK brake pads/rotors). A flat +3% would have raised those the same as captive filters.

Blind weight is still partly the old world

23.8% of basket € still takes the unbenchmarked base lift (NO_EVIDENCE_HOLD, ~6.1% weighted). Differentiation is real where evidence exists — honest about where it does not.

Street on the right point

Street comparisons use PR00 × 1.785 (street factor), not raw list — correcting the Eucon-era misread. No oem_benchmark without enough priced peer OEs.

New models need mix, not only +3%

D-Max 2.2 (15k oil interval) is a θ / launch-book story: protect interval-linked wear with mix rules; do not claim volume from the list move alone.
