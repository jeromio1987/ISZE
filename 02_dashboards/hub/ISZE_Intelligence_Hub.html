How the areas connect

Price list→AIM level→Capture→Competitors→Network→Control room

The price list sets what a distributor pays. AIM says which parts the move touches. Capture says how much of the expected service spend we bill today. Competitor prices show how far the aftermarket sits from us. The network shows who buys and what is open. Use the filter chips above to follow one distributor or one part family through every area.

Pages marked "not receipt-checked" have no render check on disk. They may be fine; nobody has recorded it. The filter chips match the page title, or words shown on the page (8 or more mentions for a distributor, 40 for a part family). They do not look inside hidden data. Figures on the tiles are from 01_data/00_derived/metrics_snapshot.json (2026-09-24).
