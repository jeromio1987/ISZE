The size of the uncovered service opportunity

Live

A way to measure, per market, what the vehicles in service would spend on parts if every one bought at the planning rate, and what share of that we actually reach.

Why it mattered: It gave the aftermarket case a ceiling and a gap in euros, on a fleet denominator, so the opportunity could be sized instead of asserted.

  • 01_data/00_derived/pms_opportunity_matrix.csv

Warranty belongs on its own line

Live

Warranty claim euros sit inside parts billed, so billed and sales to dealer do not match until warranty is separated out.

Why it mattered: It explained why the UK pickup numbers looked like a shortfall, when part of it was warranty treatment, not lost sales.

2026
  • 01_data/00_derived/distributor_warranty_expense_fiscal.csv
  • 03_analytics/Isuzu/prism/pages/20_Warranty.py

BVP is not the aftermarket

Live

Best Value Parts is Isuzu's own budget range. Choosing it keeps the sale inside, at a lower margin; it is not the same as losing the sale to a true independent competitor.

Why it mattered: It stopped an internal mix question being reported as external competition, which would have sent the wrong response.

2026-07-01
  • 05_memory/projects/aftermarket-intelligence.md

UTI and UMI split by the right key; IUK is not ITUK

Live

Two Israel distributors and two UK entities share similar names. Splitting by the selling account (sold-to), not by payer name, keeps them apart.

Why it mattered: It corrected figures that had been quietly blended, and it is now a standing rule on every Israel and UK chart.

  • 05_memory/projects/aftermarket-intelligence.md
