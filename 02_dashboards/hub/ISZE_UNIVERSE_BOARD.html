The roadmap

The strategy paper's own six stages, in the order they have to happen.

  1. 1

    Agree the scope and the portfolio

    Done

    The strategy paper, the presentation and the showcase describe the same proposition, and routine services are written out of scope.

  2. 2

    Pin the classification release

    Done 2026-10-04

    One named release is approved: 2026-10-04, 164,943 rows, hash re-verified at build time. The newer candidate stays unapproved pending the three white-paper re-tests.

  3. 3

    Open two prepared views

    Partly ready

    The Atlas view exists (truck catalogue and parts atlas). The dealer-standard view exists (19 country pages of one comparison). Neither is yet prepared as an invitation, and neither has been used by a distributor for a real task.

  4. 4

    Run the dealer sample

    Not started

    Ten dealers across two markets, including one reluctant to add reporting work. Nothing recorded yet.

  5. 5

    Run the campaign line

    Not started

    At least two offers clearing the €150,000 eligible billing size filter, measured before, during and after. The campaign line has not run.

  6. 6

    Evaluate, then extend or narrow

    Not started

    Stops if, after two eligible rounds, no participant can name a decision the tool changed. It does not pause or add a report.

What it costs, and when it stops. The paper recommends starting from the existing €60,000 to €90,000 route inside the existing EUR 100,000 planning envelope, a minimum offer size of €150,000 eligible billing of eligible billing, and €15,000 to €25,000 of dealer enablement spent as accepted tasks rather than as an entitlement. Every amount here is read from the paper and printed in euros; the earlier board printed them in pounds. It stops if, after two eligible rounds, no participant can name a decision the tool changed - and it does not pause, add a report, or widen the group.
