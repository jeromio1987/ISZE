Key Observations FY2026

  • 🔴IUK structural decline — Largest single gap driver. −€1.6M vs BP. Brake parts and steering units not repeating; DPD/filter mix shift. Dealer sales healthy (112% vs LY) suggesting issue is at IUK wholesale level, not end-market.
  • 🔴UMI below expectations — −22% vs BP. SPA under management review. €167K lubricants BO pending — could partly offset in March.
  • 🟢ITUK strongest performance — +14% vs prior year. +6% vs BP. DLOD programme and ITUK year-end push both contributing. Feb dealer sales £1.18M vs plan £1.0M.
  • 🟢RSA / I Motors outperforming — Both above BP and prior year. RSA +15% YoY. I Motors +10%. Smaller base but positive trend.
  • 🟡ISD below BP — −17% vs BP but similar to prior year (−9%). Steering units one-off last year (€57K LY) distorting comparison.
  • 🟡IMG re-entry notable — Ranked #11 vs #24 prior year. Not included in top 10 table but signals improvement in Germany.
  • 🔴Q1 FY2026 (Jan-Feb) weak — €5,974K vs budget €8,814K = −32%. Strong Feb helped (€3.2M, +16% vs BP) but January was soft (€2.7M, −6%).
  • 🔵Competitive parts pressure growing — ITUK competitive share at 11.95% orders / 13.22% sales. IBX SOR-VOR ratio improving (75/25). DAF TRP Isuzu items now live in UK market.

FY2027 Action Plan Implications

  • 🎯IUK recovery is the #1 priority — FY2026 gap of −€1.6M vs BP is not recoverable in FY2027 without structural intervention. E-Commerce (due June 2026) and MDI project are the levers.
  • 🎯UMI SPA resolution — UTI awaiting management review. UMI below BP partly SPA-related. Resolution needed before FY2027 planning locks in.
  • 📋PMS pilot (ITUK) must activate — £91K gap to ITUK's own March target. PMS joint programme is FY2027 volume play. N-series capture is a genuine/BVP mix story, not a deficit — see FACTS.md §1 (do not quote 0.3%, obsolete).
  • 📋GSCM integration — Currently proof-of-concept. PRD orders disrupted (€93K fast movers placed manually). FY2027 efficiency risk if not resolved.
  • 💡Lubricants — time to land — IML go-ahead needed. UMI €167K BO in March shows existing latent demand. BC v2 submitted. FY2027 should see a live pilot or close the project.
  • 💡AIM application to distributor targeting — 93.5% classification live. Strategic Score should drive FY2027 customer prioritisation. Opportunity engine gap analysis to be shared with top-5 distributors.
