What these words mean
IDS dealers — the Isuzu Dealer Standard, an annual dealer evaluation. Each pin is coloured by how that dealer scores against its own national average.
Above · 3 points or more over its national average
Around · within 3 points either side
Below · 3 points or more under its national average
Solid ring · position from the MIDI network survey
Broken ring, pale centre · position is a town centre or coarser
160 of 564 dealers carry a surveyed coordinate. The other 404 sit on a town, a directory entry or, for nine of them, the country centre. Open a pin to see which note its own position came from.
