6. What this page knows and does not know

What it holds
  • Sections A to E for all 564 dealers in the assessment extract, with each market's average beside them.
  • The market average and the share of dealers counted in it, market by market.
  • The largest unfinished checklist items per dealer, with the market average on the same item.
  • Assessment dates where the extract carries one; 276 of 564 entries carry none and are shown as not supplied.
What it does not hold
  • No milestone history. There is none in this workspace, so every milestone on this page is typed by you. Nothing is invented to fill the table.
  • No actions, owners or dates. Where the workspace holds no minutes, the row stays blank.
  • No second assessment round. Section scores shown are the ones on file; a change in recorded answers appears only when a new assessment exists.
  • No saving anywhere. The page cannot write to a server or to a shared drive.
How your entries survive

This page keeps your entries only in the open browser tab. To keep them, press Save my plan to a file — it downloads a small comma-separated text file. Next time, press Load a saved plan and choose that file: your browser reads it locally, which is allowed, while a page that fetches a file for itself is not. Printing works whether or not you save.
