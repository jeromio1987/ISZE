Two truck warranty totals disagree, and this matters. On the canonical file (warranty_by_db_FY_canonical.csv) UTI's April 2025 to March 2026 warranty is EUR 260,152 and ITUK's EUR 375,552. On the bill-month truck claims used here the same twelve months are EUR 744,733 for UTI and EUR 509,345 for ITUK, 2.9 times and 1.4 times higher. The README of the commercial datasets says the two cover different workbooks and market sets, and I have not found which is right. My earlier note that UTI warranty is 8% of billing used the canonical file; on this basis it is 23%. Until the owner settles it, quote UTI warranty as a range.

What it says. ITUK warranty parts fell 16% year on year (April to August) while billing rose; UTI warranty rose 29% while billing fell. At UTI roughly a quarter of what we billed in the last five months was warranty parts on this basis, against under a fifth a year earlier. This does not yet say who pays for those claims: the files hold the part euros, not the funding.

2. UK oil-filter cover on the Japan mileage tables

With real mileage instead of an assumed 50,000 km, how much of the expected oil-filter demand did we bill?

Result. On Japan's tables the genuine oil-filter cover is between 43% and 59%, with 50% as the central case. The deck's 35% rests on 50,000 km a year, and only 14% of the 4,883 trucks in Japan's tables drive more than that. The old range of 24% to 60% was too wide to use; this narrows it to roughly 43% to 60%.

How the cases are built. Expected filters = N35 trucks (10,900) x km / 20,000 plus N75 trucks (10,684) x km / 40,000. Fleet counts from the UIO workbook (via 03_analytics/Isuzu/prism/data/uio_ld_n35_share.csv). Billed filters for 2025 from 01_data/00_derived/pms_capture_by_db_year.csv (genuine 10,231, genuine plus Best Value 13,599) and the deck's own genuine figure of 9,818 for the genuine column. Mileage from 01_data/90_INBOX/_shimauchi_mileage_20261006/shimauchi_mileage_quantiles.json.

What the Japan tables cannot settle. The files do not say which market they cover, there is no N35 and N75 split, they are modelled from warranty-claim odometers, and readings under 5,000 km were removed, which pushes the average up and so pushes cover down. The central case therefore leans conservative. The interval is a ceiling set by the dashboard indicator, not a fixed schedule, so real visits per truck can be lower than km divided by interval. Confirm the market with Shimauchi before any number leaves the building.

3. Cash, stock and receivables: what exists

What would a turnaround plan have, and what is still missing?

Not found. I looked for receivables, ageing, payment terms, ISZE's own stock value, payables, cash and balance sheet in file names across the data, docs, meetings and memory folders and in the finance report's sheets. None of it is there. The finance report has sales, cost of sales and provisions only. I read file names and sheet names, not every cell of every workbook, so a balance sheet could sit inside a file named for something else.

The closest thing that exists is stock held by the distributors, from the monthly parts-handling KPI. It is the distributors' working capital, not ISZE's, but it shows where billing runs ahead of what dealers sell.
