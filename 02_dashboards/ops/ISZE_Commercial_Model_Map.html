From PRISM Explainer · §1

One number needs three layers

A euro of parts revenue means nothing without the fleet. PRISM never reports a raw figure without anchoring it to surviving parc (UIO). GPS and PMS both divide against that denominator — at different resolution. They are siblings, not a chain.

LEVEL 0

The fleet

Source of truth: IML vehicle registration data. Surviving parc ≠ total registrations.

Rule: no sales figure is quoted without the surviving-parc denominator. A small fleet with strong €/truck can outrank ten times the sales.
Click to expand
LEVEL 1

UIO — Units in Operation

Surviving parc, IML, annual. The shared foundation under GPS and PMS.

Feeds every €/truck and every expected-demand calculation. Wrong UIO silently warps both siblings.
Click to expand

LEVEL 2A · GPS

Genuine Parts Spend / Share per Unit — Net Sales € ÷ UIO = € per truck per year. Macro view: distributor × year. Answers “how much of the parts wallet?”

Traffic light: >€150 healthy · €50–150 at risk · <€50 critical. CY2025: 15 green / 3 amber of 18 markets. Tender brief £580/veh/yr ≈ full-capture ceiling — GPS below that band is the same wallet story, measured after SaTy netting. Denominator trap: MIDI (~98,654 UIO, zero ISZE-direct sales) is excluded from the network GPS waterfall — its ~€15M theoretical wallet was dropped 2026-07-02 so CY2025 re-based 40.2% → 50.2% (later 51.1% with CR/RE). Never put non-customers back in the parc denominator.

LEVEL 2B · PMS

Preventive Maintenance Service coverage — Actual PNs sold ÷ Expected demand = Capture %. Micro view: distributor × model × PN. Answers “are we winning the service jobs?”

CY2025 wear-part capture 13%–64% — not one market clears two-thirds. Rankings do not move with GPS.

Why both layers: a distributor can look GPS-green while still losing most oil filters, brake pads and clutch jobs to the aftermarket. Collapsing them into one blended score would hide that — which is exactly why PRISM keeps them independent.

From PRISM Explainer · §4 · + BVP strategy

What “capture” actually counts

Every PMS figure adds genuine + BVP. Only aftermarket is treated as loss. A euro that moves genuine → BVP is still an ISZE euro.

Network PMS wear-part wallet · CY2025

Genuine 78.6% BVP 21.4%

€5,768,026 total captured (network) — Genuine €4,536,346 · BVP share of captured wallet 21.4%. 100% of this bar is ISZE revenue.

Only loss = aftermarket (Bosch, Febi, budget brands). Customer money leaves ISZE entirely. Rising BVP mix is a margin-quality signal — not leakage to “fix” by chasing customers back to full-price genuine when the alternative is €0.

From Tender Intelligence Strategy Brief v6 · + PRISM Tender Pipeline

Tenders — three actors, one pipeline

Public procurement is transparent and underused. The core problem is visibility: which public fleets have Isuzu, who maintains them, when R&M renews. Vehicle win without aftersales follow-through leaves parts to chance.

Actor 1

Vehicle Sales (IMIE)

Converts fleet vehicle purchase tenders. Early sight of PINs. New registrations fed back to ISZE as UIO immediately.

Actor 2

ISZE Aftersales

Runs tender monitor weekly. Qualifies leads. Routes to distributor. Owns fleet intelligence and SPA / genuine strategy.

Actor 3

Distributor (DB)

Local executor. Receives a pre-qualified brief — who to call, not tender jargon. Wins service / R&M, places parts with ISZE.

R&M contracts = highest-value tender type

Vehicle purchase places metal. Repair & Maintenance locks parts for 3–5 years — near-100% genuine capture on the covered fleet if an authorised DB wins; IAM lock-in if they don’t. No new vehicles required — existing UIO generates the revenue. Monitor auto-flags R&M with a parts-supply brief.

  • Benchmark: £580 / vehicle / year parts potential (UK True Sales) → 20 units ≈ £116k/yr · ~£928k over 8 years at full capture
  • Ford Pro: 17% of EBIT from aftermarket & services — vehicle is the door; aftermarket is the house
  • Kosha FY2031 €52.1M target needs ~€8.9M beyond market CAGR — new mechanisms required

From PRISM Explainer · §6

What the model admits it doesn’t know yet

The 106-check gate catches arithmetic drift. Judgment calls stay labelled — not smoothed over.

Air-filter interval — provisional Expected demand uses a 0.5× factor (roughly every second service). Doubled measured air-filter capture network-wide the day it was applied. Not IML-confirmed — every air-filter capture number carries that caveat.
CR / RE credit notes — reversible judgment 280 rows · €1,651,149 (2023–2026) with credit/return codes on positive-value lines. Excluded from revenue / PMS by choice, not ERP confirmation. If SAP clarifies, one definition reverts and everything recalculates.
SaTy is behaviour, not product VOR/RUSH → surcharge on net GPS. CR/RE → keep-set exclusion. Never treat SaTy as a category tree under BVP.
Tender → capture still unwired Monitor + strategy exist; automatic join into PMS/GPS opportunity € does not. Brief’s UK parc cross-check and Competitiveness link are next commercial wiring — not done.
Lubricants / BESCO — optional side lane Separate commercial programme (~€442K / ~13% GP in memory — verify before external use). Not in the GPS/PMS capture spine; do not fold into wallet % without an explicit join.
