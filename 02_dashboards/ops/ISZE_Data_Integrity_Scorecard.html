3. Which open items hurt a published number most?

Ranked by what they move, not by ID. Sizes are copied from the register of 24 Sep; the register does not size every item, and where it does not, this page says so.

A Moves a headline by more than a fifth, or makes a number unusable

Trucks-in-service forecast 2026-2030 UIO-FCAST

The 2030 fleet is 45% to 59% too high (it scraps 3% a year where history implies 10% for trucks, 12% for pickups). Poland pickups +102%, UK pickups +55%. Israel trucks are about 25% too low in every year.

What it moves Every spend-per-truck rate, capture vs maximum, and the FY27 growth plan for 2026 onward.

Next step Rebuild per V20 spec. Spec written, not built. Owner: UIO model owner

UK truck annual mileage ITUK-MILEAGE

Oil-filter cover reads 24% on the interval ratio, 60% on measured mileage: a 35 point swing. The deck assumes 50,000 km a year; warranty odometers say 12,891 (N35) and 35,478 (N75).

What it moves The UK truck headline cover. Only quotable as 'on an unconfirmed 50,000 km'.

Next step Mail to Duncan Young is drafted, not sent. Owner: Jerome

Three service-interval factors are guesses UNSOURCED-FACTORS

Air filter 0.5 (marked provisional since 2 Jul), brake pad and shoe 0.5 (the file says 'old unsourced guess'), brake rotor 0.25 (provisional). The register does not size them.

What it moves Every demand and gap figure for air, brake pad, shoe and rotor.

Next step Ask IML for the three numbers; make the check fail while any is unconfirmed. Owner: IML via Jerome

Warranty claims turned into euro three ways LCV-CURRENCY-RULE

Year averages (162 / 158 yen per euro), a flat 163.7 plus pounds at 1.12, and one file with dollars added up with no conversion. The register does not size the difference.

What it moves MIDI's euro and the fiscal-year and monthly warranty totals.

Next step Jerome picks one method before the fiscal-year file is refreshed. Owner: Jerome
