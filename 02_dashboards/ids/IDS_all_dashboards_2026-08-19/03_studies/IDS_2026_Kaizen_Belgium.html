2. In the room

Talking line

We are not here to argue a total score. We are here to pick the dealers who are below the country average, agree three actions, and come back next month to see what closed. Belgium as a market is close to the network; the work is the names on the hit list — start with the two in Needs improvement.

Do
  • Open the Belgium dealer-compare HTML. Map first, then this hit list.
  • Agree 3–5 actions from the largest checklist gaps. Owner, target date, proof — blank until they fill them.
  • Walk Parts and Service on the two Needs improvement dealers. Country Parts is not the headline.
  • Monthly pull: % visited, % actions closed, incomplete still open, escalations.
Do not
  • Promise that a higher IDS score will move GPS or fill rate. Those files stay separate.
  • Treat this list as a blacklist. It is the call list for the next 90 days.
  • Lead with a network correlation lecture. Stay on named dealers and named checklist items.
  • Drop Parts from the conversation because country D is near the network. Degroote and Fayt are still soft on D.
