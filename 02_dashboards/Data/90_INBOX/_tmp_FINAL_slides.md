## Slide 3

- PARTS SUPPORT  ·  SCHEDULED PMS
- The actions we will take
- 3
- Phase 1 covers the N75 fleet on the 40,000 km interval, scheduled PMS only. No additional visits are to be generated.
- Filter pack
- Oil, fuel and air
- All three filters go out as one set on the scheduled service. Oil and fuel are already established; the air filter is the one most often left off. Selling the three together is the primary action.
- BVP
- Where genuine is quiet
- A BVP equivalent exists for the oil, fuel and air filter. It is used where genuine demand is low, or where the BVP wins on price.
- SPA
- Where price is the barrier
- No BVP exists for the R90 brake pad, so an SPA on genuine is the only lever. Brakes stay condition-based and are supplied when the repair requires them.
- Clutch kit
- Manual transmissions only
- No broad campaign. Kit 5876102320 on identified manual units, until transmission data is available.
