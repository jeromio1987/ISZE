Staffing
Our project teams always consist of an internationally experienced project leader and a group of subject-matter-experts as 
demanded by the actual scope of work.
25
Project scope, methodology and deliverables
› Project Leader: up to 10 years experience in Automotive market 
research & analysis projects; responsible for the initiation, planning, 
execution, controlling, and closing of a Eucon project with the aim of 
achieving all of the project goals within the given constraints
› Technical Consultant / Data Editor: up to 15 years project experience 
and personal experience on workshop level, e.g. as workshop manager, 
qualified mechanic or warehousing staff; responsible for all parts data 
related topics such as parts basket qualification and “apples-to-apples” 
cross-referencing 
› Data Analyst: up to 10 years project experience as well as expertise in 
the field of system application in general and mass data management in 
particular (e.g. system supported data validation & transformation)
› Business Analyst: 5-10 years project experience and excellent skills in 
data interpretation and visualization; responsible for the processing of 
raw data survey results and creation of Management Summary 
documents as well as additional market insights; execution of 
secondary research
› Consultant: up to 10 years project experience and profound 
Automotive background (from OEM or IAM); responsible for certain 
market intelligence project modules and management presentations
