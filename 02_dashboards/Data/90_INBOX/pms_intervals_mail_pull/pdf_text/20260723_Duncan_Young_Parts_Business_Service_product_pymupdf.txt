Goal of Isuzu’s Parts Sales 

UIO of Isuzu vehicle in the world is more than 6 million, far exceeding the 1.3 million in Japan.

A large number of vehicle will be needed by 2050 to support rising living standards, especially in emerging economies.

With increasing UIO, we need a system to supply parts to all markets in a timely manner in order to maintain the Isuzu vehicles’ conditions 

and to ensure their 100% performance.

We have to properly manage DB/DLR’s various information that is required for “Moving the World – for you"

Environmental awareness / Background and Objectives

Mission

To support the operation/transportation of Isuzu vehicles by establishing a system that can deliver parts necessary for our customers’ vehicle 

maintenance, when they are needed, in the quantity needed, and at a reasonable price.

To manage and support the parts business of all companies and achieve parts revenue targets.

To raise ISUZU brand value to be chosen by customers through collaborative initiatives among vehicle sales, services and parts to provide 

service products for TCO reduction and profitable services to customers.

To build a business operation that takes the environmental issues into consideration by establishing an efficient parts distribution system.

Vision

Standardization of functions and mechanisms to maintain the same 

level of availability as in Japan

Provide answer to societal challenges and customer’s concern

Reduction of CO2 emissions,TCO and Waste

Value

No. 1 in parts supply rate

Maximization of revenue : 40% GP or higher

Achievement of No. 1 in global environmental friendliness

Achievement of No. 1 in CS and increase the brand value
