<h2>Leak 2 &mdash; parts billed that never reached a dealer</h2>
<p>The money splits into two problems that need opposite responses. Under-ordering means the parts
<b>are</b> selling through to dealers and the distributor is simply not reordering enough. Genuine
overstock means the parts are sitting still. Merging the two produces the wrong instruction.</p></div>
