Section 3 · the new logic

Six principles

1

Identity is the part number plus the figure key

A part number is unique and permanent. A description is free text and collides. The manufacturer's figure key groups parts by assembly. Identity = part_number + fig_no/key_no. No join on description, anywhere.

2

Two axes, two sets of columns, never mixed

The governed commercial tree lives in catalog_l1/l2/l3. The system vocabulary lives in workbook_l1/l2/l3. A hard flag marks every row so a chart cannot accidentally blend them.

3

The catalogue tree is the only governed axis

7 postures, 25 families, 172 types. It ties to PR00 at 100% on 163,014 checked part numbers. That is the axis management sees and the axis pricing is read on.

4

The EPC is the naming authority

The Isuzu Snapon EPC returns the manufacturer's own part name and figure by part number. It settles the ambiguous descriptions and names the residual. This is a new capability, found today — it was not available when the old file was built.

5

Figure key is structure, never competition

Two parts under figure 411-025 belong to the same assembly. That is a structural grouping signal. It is not proof that no competitor exists, and it may never produce a "Captive" or "Contested" label by itself. This is the exact mistake that labelled €12.96M as captive this morning.

6

Every figure carries its basis

Window and scope stated in the same breath as the number. Residual named, never hidden. 90.6% of billed euro sits on a governed node; the rest is declared.
