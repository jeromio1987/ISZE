7. How to read this

  • Lead with euros, not piece counts. A gasket and a can are not comparable in pieces.
  • Observed pcs / 1,000 UIO is not an IML service interval and not a capture %.
  • COLLECT means we do not have a dealer transaction — do not invent one from public listings.
  • UTI ≠ UMI. IUK ≠ ITUK. MIDI ES/IT claim geography is context, not an ISZE chase list.
  • FILTER ASM claim PNs are inserts — reman of the insert is not reman of the full can.
  • Click a bar, table row or worklist PN to open the drawer. Esc or the dimmed page closes it. Enter / Space on a focused row also opens.
  • Demand pipes: warranty first-claim, commercial billed, and fleet pcs/1,000 UIO are separate. Billed pcs ≠ market size. First-DPD warranty % cannot be computed. Cleaning / freight bands are ASSUMPTION until Optymha quotes land (COLLECT).
