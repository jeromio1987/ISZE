Methodology & Price Chain
PR00 Price Source

File: IE40 Price list as of 13 March 2026 (164,382 lines, EUR).
D-Max selection: Parts with ABC code A, B, or C (top volume movers) cross-referenced against pricing simulation qty-weights. Where multiple ABC:A variants exist, the most frequent PN is used.
N/F-Series: Higher JP-source price clusters in IE40, no direct PN→model mapping. Must be validated against EQ-HIT before external use.

Price Chain Logic

ASC (PR00): IE40 list price — what the Authorised Service Centre pays ISZE.
DB level: PR00 × 1.40 → distributor uplift +40%.
DLR / Fleet: DB price × 1.275 → dealer uplift +27.5%. Full chain PR00 → street = ×1.785.
Convention: this is the FACTS.md §7 apples-to-apples chain for benchmarking genuine against aftermarket. ISZE's own-EUR rule (35% margin = ÷0.65) is a different convention and is deliberately not used here.
Index: Each price level ÷ AM retail = competitiveness ratio. <1.0× = genuine cheaper than AM.

Aftermarket (AM) Prices — D-Max

Source: Autodoc.co.uk scraped 9 May 2026 — 300 PNs queried, 110 with prices (36.7% hit rate).
FX: GBP × 1.17 = EUR equivalent (updated from ×1.18 Feb–Mar 2026).
IAM tier: Budget/standard quality (Nipparts, Borg&Beck, NK) — mid of search range.
OEM-equiv: Premium OE-supplier brands (Bosch, Valeo, LuK, Brembo, Mann-Filter) — ~1.3–1.6× IAM.

Market Adjustments & N/F-Series AM

DE market: AM prices × 0.92 (more competitive IAM market).
FR market: AM prices × 1.05 (slightly higher retail).
N/F-Series AM: Estimated from European commercial truck parts market knowledge. Fewer brands = higher AM prices and smaller genuine premium gap. All LOW confidence.

⚠ Limitations to be aware of: N-Series and F-Series PR00 prices are estimated by price clustering in the IE40 file — no direct part-number to vehicle-model mapping exists without EQ-HIT data. AM prices for N/F-Series are estimates only and should be treated as directional. D-Max AM prices are based on a spot check (Feb–Mar 2026) and may not reflect seasonal promotions or bulk pricing available to workshops. Competitiveness index is vs retail AM — workshop/trade prices from Autodoc/GSF/EuroParts will be lower, making genuine parts look less competitive than shown.
