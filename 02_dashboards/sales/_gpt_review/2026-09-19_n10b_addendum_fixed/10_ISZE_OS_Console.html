01 · The loop, and where the sensors are blind

Three sensors feed one loop. A blind sensor is drawn as a break in the line, not as a footnote: the loop runs on what is measured and the break stays visible in the picture.

Break — no identity

Israel and the UK are excluded from the VIN register by design. The register holds 3,238 vehicles in 7 markets (PT 2,111 · IE 647 · DE 183 · MT 130 · CY 114 · BE 49 · GR 4). The UK mail extract of 29,935 VINs is not loaded.

break
Break — no weight class

The fleet bucket file carries one combined column of 36,793 trucks with no 3.5 t / 7.5 t split, so a measured per-class distance cannot reach the count.

Sensors and their breaks

  • Break — no vehicle count — Ukraine, Armenia, Kazakhstan and Palestine bill parts but have no target-spend basis. Any spend-per-vehicle figure for them would be invented.
  • Break — no identity — Israel and the UK are excluded from the VIN register by design. The register holds 3,238 vehicles in 7 markets (PT 2,111 · IE 647 · DE 183 · MT 130 · CY 114 · BE 49 · GR 4). The UK mail extract of 29,935 VINs is not loaded.
  • Break — no weight class — The fleet bucket file carries one combined column of 36,793 trucks with no 3.5 t / 7.5 t split, so a measured per-class distance cannot reach the count.
  • Break — provisional factor — The air-to-oil replacement ratio of 0.5 is provisional until the engine maker confirms it. Capture is comparable across categories, not locked.
  • Break — factor open — The brake wear factor sits at 0.4 with 0.8 undecided. UK brake capture is not quotable as locked.
  • Break — one global distance — Every category is computed on one global 20,000 km a year, against a measured 12,165 to 51,680 spread by truck class.
  • Break — count basis superseded — The 93.5% completeness figure of March 2026 is count-based, superseded, and is not used anywhere on this page.
  • Break — pricing-risk layer — The pricing-risk score is a null result (a fitted replacement failed its backtest). It is not a key figure and does not appear here.

05 · Honesty layer

Four states on every figure on this page, then the figures that are wrong elsewhere in the estate and what this page does instead.

The four states

Measured

Counted in a file, from a named extract, on a stated date.

Modelled

Built from an assumption that is stated on the figure. A placeholder is a model.

Excluded

Would be wrong to publish. Named on the page, left out of the arithmetic.

Needs a judgement

The data is not the blocker. A person has to choose.

Where this page's figures sit

Measured

Parts billed €30.76M · vehicles 163,459 · spend per vehicle €188.16 · the 18-market spend league · 54.4% sales-to-dealer visibility · 23,236 ledger rows.

Modelled

The €55.25M target-spend ceiling and everything derived from it (55.7% cover, €24.73M uncaptured) · the 996,890 expected service units · category gaps at average selling price.

Excluded

2026 rows of the spend-per-unit file · every 2026–2030 vehicle forecast · the AIM completeness percentage · the pricing-risk score · the service-schedule capture lane.

Needs a judgement

Which age basis goes on a tile · the sales-to-dealer denominator · the flat-target ceiling · the mileage default · which page is the aftersales front page · ledger triage.
