Competitive price-gap exposure Table view

Real Eucon-sourced benchmark (01_data/01_Pricing/, priced 2026-08-10/11) — replaces the earlier competitiveness_autodoc_risk.csv panel, which failed a sanity check (93% duplicate placeholder price, ~0 correlation to ISZE price). This one is genuine: 0.69 correlation, real IAM brands (Blue Print, Febi Bilstein, Ridex…) and real genuine-OEM peers (DAF, Toyota, Ford). But it's a narrow, actively-growing sample — 51 part numbers, mostly Brakes/Filters/Fuel/Intake — not a full catalog scan, and it's explicitly a internal / Jerome-only work-in-progress per its own status notes (some categories intentionally left blank rather than guessed at). Bars = % gap vs ISZE street-equivalent price; negative (red) = competitor undercuts ISZE.
