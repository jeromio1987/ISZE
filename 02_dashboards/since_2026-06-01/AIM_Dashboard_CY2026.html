Guardrails — wording for anything external

Do not say externally

  • BVP is not "aftermarket". BVP is Isuzu's own budget OEM range — internal mix/down-trade, revenue retained. Only third-party IAM defection is a lost sale. Never label genuine→BVP shift as aftermarket loss.
  • No distributor-level IAM %. Eucon/AutoDoc benchmark is network-level (44 PNs). Do not quote "distributor X loses Y% to IAM" — the data cannot support it.
  • Cust 2574 ≠ ITUK. 2574 = IUK, 2575 = ITUK. CY2026 file carries ITUK as sold-to 2575 only. Never merge them in UK statements.
  • Basis mismatch. CY2025 AIM workbook scoring = Qty × PR00 (planning price). CY2026 dashboard = actual billing. Do not quote them as one series without stating the basis change.
  • brake_pad / brake_shoe "defended" claims. Genuine↔BVP crossref pairs for these categories are TF* (D-Max/LCV) only — a CV distributor at "100% genuine" there has no BVP alternative, it is not a commercial win.
