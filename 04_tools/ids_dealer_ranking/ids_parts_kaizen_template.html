04 90-day Kaizen play (Parts)

One dealer, ≤3 countermeasures, one owner each, one verify metric. Do not open a museum of actions — close or escalate at day 90.

How to use this play: pick K1 for every Wave-1 dealer (RO attach). Add K2 where Excel shows weak Genuine/BVP promo or mix (e.g. D.1-8). Add K3 where D.2-10 / fill / MOS / inventory control is zero. Cap at three K’s per dealer.

K1 — PMS basket on every R&M repair order

Intent: every paid service visit offers the periodic-maintenance parts set, not only what the customer asked for.

Do:

  • Define the basket: oil filter + fuel filter + air filter (and cabin if fitted) + “next due” wear hint (brake/clutch) from km/age.
  • Advisor / DMS: PMS basket line is default on for R&M job types; customer decline = reason code on the RO.
  • Week 1: print a one-page counter card; train advisors + 1 workshop lead (30′).

Owner: dealer Service Manager (+ DB Aftersales coach). Verify: % of R&M ROs with ≥1 PMS filter line (sample 20 ROs / week). Target (90d): ≥70% of R&M ROs carry the basket or a logged decline reason.

K2 — Genuine default on PMS part numbers

Intent: stop silent non-genuine / wrong-brand substitution on the PMS basket; make Genuine (and approved BVP where policy allows) the path of least resistance.

Do:

  • DMS / parts counter: PMS PNs default to Genuine; non-genuine needs a reason code (price / availability / customer request).
  • Fix IDS gap D.1-8: Genuine + BVP promo visible at the parts counter (poster / screen / kit display).
  • Weekly: pull 10 PMS lines — count Genuine vs other; discuss the “other” reasons in the board.

Owner: dealer Parts Manager. Verify: Genuine share on PMS-family lines (or RO sample if no sell-out). Target (90d): +5–10 pp Genuine on sampled PMS lines or 100% of non-genuine lines have a reason code.

K3 — Min/max + first-fill recovery (top PMS stock)

Intent: close the Excel gaps on inventory control and fill measurement (D.1-5, D.2-8, D.2-9, D.2-10) so the workshop can sell what K1/K2 promise.

Do:

  • List top ~50 PMS movers (filters + high-runner wear); set min/max and review weekly.
  • Measure and post: first fill rate, turns, standing stock — even a simple spreadsheet counts if DMS cannot.
  • Monthly sales/order targets for parts (D.2-9): plan vs actual on one A4, reviewed in the board.
  • Escalate to DB when fill fails because of their supply (BO / lead time), not dealer process.

Owner: dealer Parts Manager + DB Parts. Verify: first-fill % on the top-50 list; zero critical stockouts on oil/fuel/air for >5 working days. Target (90d): fill on top-50 ≥ country DB average (or +5 pp vs baseline if already measured); D.2-10 re-score moves off zero.
