FILTRON FILTERS – ENGINEERED TO PROTECT

FILTRON filters are designed to provide protection throughout their entire service life – even under the most demanding driving conditions.

Working together as a powerful protection system for critical components, they help prevent costly wear and breakdowns by delivering superior protection against contaminants, gases, and allergens, while ensuring comfort and peace of mind for passengers.
