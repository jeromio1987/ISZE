# Partner intake, phase 0

File-based intake for the monthly sales-to-dealer file. Built from the internal film
`09_meetings/Excellence_Partner_Program_2026/_build/film_a.js` and Plan B
`Plan_B_Universe_White_Paper_2026-10-09.md`.

The specification and the nine prompts are in the same copy, under
`09_meetings/Excellence_Partner_Program_2026/` (`TECH_SPEC_Partner_Intake_Phase0_2026-10-09.md`
and `CURSOR_PROMPTS/`). Rules whose words are not in the film or the Plan B paper
stay registered and do not fire. See `rules.py`. Nine of them are still off.

Nothing here posts a credit, sends mail, or reads live billing. Tests use made-up rows.
Warranty stays its own line. What is left after that is stock, not lost sales.

Open, and left open: credit note versus invoice condition, whether warranty sits inside
stock-order lines, what order type PRD means, where the real drop lives, which
distributors already send files, where the KPI file lands, and the training register.
