What we billed, against what the distributor sold to its dealers

Both sales stages side by side, always, part number by part number, inside the AIM product tree. Billed is what ISZE invoiced to the distributor. Sold to dealer is what the distributor sold on to its dealer network, on its own report.
How the euros are read. Sales to dealer arrives with no price of its own, so its euro is modelled and the page says so everywhere it appears. The primary comparison figure is units × the ISZE price list of 10 Aug 2026, so the two stages are measured on the same list. Beside it the page also shows a modelled dealer price: the same figure plus 35% gross margin, which is the price-list euro divided by 0.65. Neither one is the distributor's turnover, its revenue, or an actual dealer purchase price. Only the billed side carries a real invoiced price, so the average actual price and the price index are shown on the billed side alone and no margin is added to them.
Drag on the strip to set the window, or pull either orange handle. One bar a month: billed above the line, sold to dealer below it.
