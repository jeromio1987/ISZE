1. Decide this week

Same rule as the weekly distributor list: a deadline on or after the build date, not marked Ignore by our own scoring, and either repair and maintenance or a pickup or truck purchase. Sorted by deadline. The same tender published twice (EU journal and national portal) is shown once.

1b. Check the vehicle list first

Repair, maintenance or parts contracts for an existing fleet where the notice does not name Isuzu. Whether this is our business depends on the vehicles in that fleet, and the notice does not list them: the vehicle list is in the tender documents. Open the notice, check for Isuzu, then decide. Not scored as pursue or pass until then.
