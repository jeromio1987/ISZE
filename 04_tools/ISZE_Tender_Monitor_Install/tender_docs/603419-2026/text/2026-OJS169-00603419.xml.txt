     The contracting authority has a total of 219 light vehicles of various makes and models. Of these, there are currently approx. 100 electric and 119 fossile fossile (four petrol hybrids). The share of electric vehicles will increase during the agreement period. The contracting authority has separate agreements for the purchase and hire of vehicles. In these contracts, service, or service and maintenance agreements, can be included or be able to be included for some of the Contracting Authority ́s vehicles. Currently approx. 50 of the Contracting Authority ́s vehicles have their own service, or a service and maintenance contract. The number may vary during the agreement period. This framework agreement will not be used in such cases. The contract does not include warranty deliveries as a result of a new vehicle guarantee, improvements due to insurance damage, tyres, tyre hotel or repairs of body damage and glass. The procurement ́s value is estimated to approx. NOK 1.8 million per annum (excluding VAT). The estimated extent over 4 years is approx. The absolute contract value is NOK 10 million. The absolute contract value is higher than the estimated scope per annum would suggest, in order to take into account unexpected large amounts that may be in question. Please note that the stated volume of the number of vehicles and the contract value are estimates. The figures may deviate from the actual volume of the contract. The stated figures are not binding for the Customer regarding the number of future bookings or the extent of the contract.
