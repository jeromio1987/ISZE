Reading the paper
What this edition does
This is a full product and participation proposal for Isuzu Motors Europe (ISZE), not an audit report and not a 
specification for a single dealer page. It incorporates the supplied Universe commercial papers, their revisions, 
the twenty-topic evidence consolidation, the ITUK pilot work and the latest IDS × AIM export documentation. It 
also examines external examples of connected industrial services, partner operations and data exchange. [I01–
I10]
The business owner has confirmed that Isuzu Dealer Standards information and sales-to-dealer information can 
be supplied for product development. The commercial-owner and data/access-owner arrangements have been 
addressed. This paper therefore treats them as foundations, not as questions to reopen. That does not mean 
every recurring connection is already operating, every identity has been validated, or a particular discount or 
expenditure has been authorised. [U01]
Unless a passage is explicitly described as existing evidence, all descriptions of the future Universe experience 
are proposed product requirements. The word “should” is used deliberately. External providers' descriptions 
establish what those providers say their services do; they do not establish that the same result will occur at 
Isuzu.

Reading routes
A decision-maker can begin with Executive position, The participation proposition, The economics of the 
exchange and The development sequence. A product designer should also read the chapters on the experience 
and the eight working services. A commercial or operational owner should read The response ISZE owes, 
Measuring success and the service records in the appendices. Data and technology teams should use 
Information architecture, Analytical rules and AI assistance as requirements, not as permission to replace 
existing systems.
The paper is deliberately broader than the current ITUK demonstration. A historical case is evidence that a 
particular interaction can work. It is not a reason to limit the entire product to the fields in that case.

Executive position
The product is the service, not the collection of data
Universe should become the shared place where a distributor or dealer can obtain a better commercial 
answer and see it acted on. Its strategic value to Isuzu is richer knowledge of aftersales activity, collected with 
the participant's understanding and in exchange for something useful. Its immediate value to the participant is 
not the information it gives Isuzu; it is the work Universe helps it complete.
The existing kickback-linked sales-to-dealer requirement provides a starting relationship. It cannot, by itself, 
create willingness to share explanations, stock constraints, competitive situations or service information. Those 
contributions will be sustained only if the participant receives a credible return and the effort remains 
proportionate. The original value-exchange papers correctly place self-interest and fair interpretation beside 
the formal reporting requirement. This paper turns those principles into operating services. [I02; I03]
The design should not maximise the number of fields collected. It should maximise the amount of decision-
useful, reliable and repeatable information obtained with a participant's informed agreement. An unused 
compulsory field is not progress. A short, accurate explanation attached to a real quotation can be more 
valuable than a large export whose commercial meaning is unknown.
The proposed promise
To a distributor, Universe should say: “Use your existing parts and dealer information to decide where to act, 
prepare stronger support requests and conduct better network reviews. We add parts knowledge, relevant 
market and vehicle context, an accountable response and a record of the result.”
To a dealer, it should say: “Get a parts, availability, application or commercial question answered without 
repeatedly reconstructing the case. See the evidence used about your business, add the missing context and 
receive the answer through your distributor.”
The two promises are related but not identical. A dealer does not participate merely because its distributor has 
a reporting obligation. It needs a return at the counter, in the workshop or in the management review. The 
distributor, in turn, should retain its national commercial role rather than feel that Universe is a route around 
it.
The recommended product
Universe should bring together eight working services: the connected dealer review; quotation and commercial 
support; parts availability and range; application guidance for Genuine and Best Value Parts (BVP); IDS 
improvement follow-up; fleet and service analysis; tender and fleet-opportunity preparation; and the 
participant's record of decisions and benefits.
These are not eight disconnected portals. The same selected distributor, account, site, period, part family and 
case should follow the user. A question that begins in a purchasing review can become an application request, 
a support case or a jointly agreed action without being typed again. A response should return to the place 
where the question arose.
The Aftermarket Intelligence Model, or AIM, supplies the common parts language. Isuzu Dealer Standards, or 
IDS, supplies the dealer's own assessment and the improvement conversation. PRISM supplies the relevant 
commercial calculations, vehicle context and clearly identified expectations. Universe connects these 
capabilities and the operational response. Market references and tenders are useful evidence and services 
within that experience; they need not become additional analytical pillars. [I02; I03; I07]

What makes participation accumulate
A participant should receive useful analysis from the core data before being asked for sensitive additions. Extra 
information should be requested at the moment it can improve an actual decision. A quotation may need the 
alternative product and required delivery date. An availability study may need selected stock and unfulfilled 
requests. A service study may need a defined vehicle population and appropriate usage evidence. Those are 
separate exchanges, not compulsory stages of a universal data ladder.
The service must also work when a participant declines an optional field. It may then answer a narrower 
question, leave a particular estimate unavailable or use an aggregate calculated locally by the participant. It 
should not remove unrelated core functions or erase the contributor's permitted history.
The resulting commercial record becomes more useful over time: which questions arose, what evidence was 
available, what was decided, what ISZE and the distributor delivered, and whether the participant regarded the 
return as worthwhile. This is accumulated usefulness, not coercive dependence.
The obligation on ISZE
A request for more data is not an answer. Universe must identify the role that will respond to a price, range, 
supply or technical issue and make that response visible. The software should not make an unlimited promise 
of fast technical decisions or automatic funding. It should show the next decision, the responsible role, the 
expected response arrangement and the state of any approved support.
Financial returns must be described from the beneficiary's position. Lower purchase cost, funding delivered, 
capacity released and additional gross profit are different measures. An Isuzu credit is not, by itself, proof that 
the network won incremental business. A modelled opportunity is not revenue. The proposed benefit record 
therefore separates estimates, approvals, delivery and participant-confirmed outcomes. [I03]
How to proceed
Develop a connected distributor product with complete end-to-end journeys, then deepen those journeys as 
participants use them. Start with the information the business owner can supply, not with another broad 
search for permission to begin. Use ITUK as a worked commercial example, not the architecture for every 
market. Keep the wider distributor and dealer experience in the design from the beginning.
The first development increment should let a user understand a purchasing pattern, open an appropriately 
scoped case, receive an owned response and record the result. The next increment should prove that the 
interaction is worth repeating. More operational data, more markets and more sophisticated analysis should 
follow demonstrated usefulness, not precede it as universal conditions.
The test is whether participants choose to complete their real work through Universe, correct its information 
and supply the next useful piece of context because doing so improves their own result.

The strategic opportunity
A better relationship with aftersales activity
ISZE sits between parts sources and national distributors. Distributors serve dealer networks, and dealers serve 
vehicle operators. Information seen at one point in that chain is not a complete account of the work at the next 
point. Parts billed to a distributor reflect its purchasing choices. Sales to dealer describe downstream 
purchasing. Workshop records describe selected recorded work. Customer transactions and vehicle 
populations answer other questions again. [I01]
Universe should reduce the effort of relating those partial views without pretending they are one 
measurement. The commercial opportunity is to understand a situation early enough to provide an effective 
response: a part that cannot be supplied, a quotation that needs an alternative, a dealer uncertain about an 
application, a service population with a documented requirement, or an improvement action that needs 
support.
The ambition is larger than better monthly reporting. A good reporting view helps a manager ask a question. A 
useful service helps somebody answer it, make a decision and finish the work. That final step is what can make 
the data exchange sustainable.
What Universe should be unusually good at
Universe should combine the manufacturer's parts knowledge with the distributor's commercial context and 
the dealer's local experience. None should be treated as a substitute for the others. An accurate parts 
classification does not know why a quotation was lost. A dealer's explanation does not certify 
interchangeability. A service expectation does not reveal the stock held at a branch.
The product should make these contributions complementary. A purchasing change should lead to the relevant 
parts and applications. A technical answer should remain connected to the question and the commercial 
situation it resolves. A support decision should retain the evidence that justified it. A later review should know 
which conclusion was provisional and which was confirmed.
This is where a common Isuzu experience can provide something different from a distributor simply exporting 
its own enterprise system. The extra value comes from cross-functional knowledge and response, not from 
decorating the same totals.
What Universe should not try to replace
Universe should not begin as a replacement for the distributor's accounting, stock, dealer-management, 
workshop or customer-management system. Those systems remain authoritative for the transactions they 
own. Requiring participants to abandon them would increase the adoption burden and make useful exchange 
dependent on a large technology decision.
The preferred design is a shared experience with selected integration. It should read the necessary information, 
preserve its source, return a usable decision and, where agreed, write a defined result back to the relevant 
working process. It should avoid maintaining a second authoritative order, invoice or workshop job when 
another system already owns it.
Nor should Universe imply that an analytical workshop extract is an official manufacturer service book. That 
would require a different proposition, provenance, completeness and manufacturer authority. It belongs in a 
separately governed future service, not in the wording of an early analysis feature. [I07]

The operator or fleet customer is an important eventual beneficiary, but should not automatically become a 
direct Universe user or an identifiable data subject in every case. The distributor and dealer relationship

The useful core and the optional exchanges
The core should provide a credible review using the price-free sales-to-dealer data, existing IDS evidence and 
ISZE knowledge. A participant should be able to see its history, understand parts composition, examine 
appropriate references, challenge an interpretation and record an agreed next step. No additional workshop 
file should be required merely to use those functions.
Optional exchanges deepen specific services. They should be explained in a simple form: what is needed; why 
it is needed; what the participant receives; who can see it; and what remains available without it. This is 
more useful than an abstract maturity score or a long list of desirable fields.
The internal design can retain the original levels for traceability. The external experience should offer services 
such as “Review this quotation”, “Check this application” or “Analyse this stock issue”. A distributor should not 
feel it has been classified as a low-level participant because it has chosen not to share unrelated sensitive 
information.
The incentive must not distort the evidence
Retain the existing reporting condition on its established basis. Do not invent its contractual percentage, 
broaden it through product wording or reward the sheer number of optional records submitted. Optional 
explanations should include “unknown”, “several factors” and “not worth investigating”. A system that rewards 
a completed reason field without regard to truth will acquire confident fiction.
Similarly, support should be based on the relevant commercial case and existing authorisation, not on a general 
score for how much data the participant has surrendered. Better evidence may make a request easier to 
assess. That is different from withholding ordinary support until a participant shares its entire inventory or 
customer list.
The product should recognise the effort of a correction even when it reduces the apparent opportunity. A 
dealer explaining that a family is not applicable has improved the record. A distributor declining a poorly 
matched competitor comparison has protected the quality of the decision. The service should make those 
contributions useful rather than treating them as resistance.

The participant should retain control of its history
The authorised record of the participant's own contributions, responses and outcomes should be portable. 
Stopping an optional feed should stop the associated analysis from refreshing, not retrospectively confiscate 
the history. Retention obligations and permitted access may limit particular exports, but those limits should be 
explicit.
There should also be a clear correction route. The contributor must be able to distinguish a source-data 
correction from disagreement with an interpretation. Correcting an account name should not erase an earlier 
decision. Updating an explanation should not silently rewrite what was known when support was approved.
What external examples teach
Research method and limits
The examples below are selected for mechanisms relevant to Universe: useful work, reciprocal service, partner 
participation, integration and control. They are not a ranking of vendors and not a recommendation to 
purchase their products. Public documentation was reviewed in September 2026. A described function is not 
independent evidence of adoption, financial improvement or suitability for ISZE.
Each example separates the documented practice from the proposed lesson. Product geography, subscriptions, 
enabled integrations and local service agreements may limit what an individual customer can actually use. 
Universe should copy a useful design principle only after considering those operating requirements.
John Deere: permission linked to a useful support relationship
Documented practice. John Deere's Operations Center feature description connects machine and operational 
information with planning and management tools. It also describes dealer use of connected-machine 
information, with customer permission, to support monitoring and proactive assistance. This is a product 
description, not a measured estimate of the benefit to every customer. [E01]
Lesson for Universe. Ask for deeper information within a recognisable support relationship. The participant 
should know which function will use it and what response becomes possible. A generic demand to share more 
is weaker than an explanation that a defined application or service analysis can now be completed.
Boundary. Universe does not need to begin with continuous telematics or claim a connected-vehicle 
entitlement. The transferable principle is permission tied to service. The initial information may be a short case 
entry or a selected existing export.
Caterpillar: data is accompanied by maintenance delivery
Documented practice. Caterpillar describes Customer Value Agreements as combinations of maintenance-
related support, parts and service arrangements. Its VisionLink materials describe equipment information and 
condition-related management functions. Particular agreement terms and services depend on the offering and 
dealer arrangement. [E02; E03]
Lesson for Universe. The return should extend beyond a diagnosis. A participant sharing an availability or 
service concern should receive an operational answer: an appropriate part, a planned action, a technical 
response or an explicitly assessed support request. The organisation providing the answer must be part of the 
product design.
Boundary. Isuzu should not inherit a competitor's service guarantee or compensation scheme from a brochure. 
A measurable commitment has to be matched to actual authority, stock, staffing and technical responsibility.

Volvo Trucks: make the information improve a service plan
Documented practice. Volvo Trucks describes connected monitoring that uses vehicle operation and 
component condition to support service planning, contact the customer and prepare workshop resources. Its 
service-planning description also connects truck specifications, use and annual mileage with an individual plan 
that is reviewed over time. [E04; E05]
Lesson for Universe. Fleet and workshop information becomes compelling when it changes a plan people can 
use. The offer should identify the service question, applicable vehicles and required evidence, then return a 
practical decision rather than another fleet chart.
Boundary. Observed consumption does not become the approved service requirement. Universe must use the 
appropriate Isuzu technical basis and distinguish a commercial analysis from instructions governing a vehicle's 
maintenance.
Decisiv and Prokeep: capture information where the work happens
Documented practice. The providers' May 2026 launch describes a connection between customer 
communications, service cases and parts requests. The operational documentation is more specific: enabled 
locations can attach communications to a case and exchange parts-request status. It also identifies separate 
credentials and location-level configuration. [E06; E07]
Lesson for Universe. A shared commercial record should follow the real request across teams. Do not require a 
parts counter to recreate the same question in a reporting screen after answering it in its normal process. 
Integrate selectively and make the case reference durable.
Boundary. “Connected” need not mean a single system or completely automatic information exchange. The 
practical integration details matter. Universe should promise only the information transfer and response that 
have actually been implemented.
Salesforce partner management: separate requests, decisions and claims
Documented practice. Salesforce's partner-management materials include partner experiences, deal-related 
coordination and market-development funding functions. Its documentation distinguishes fund requests and 
the approval information associated with them. These are described software functions; they do not prove that 
a funding programme generates incremental sales. [E08; E09]
Lesson for Universe. A commercial-support case should make requested, authorised and delivered assistance 
separate states. A distributor needs to know which state it has reached, what is missing and who will respond. 
The same record can later carry the outcome.
Boundary. Do not turn participation into a contest for funding or assume the software grants spending 
authority. Existing commercial approval remains with the authorised people, and individual support is not a 
general data-sharing reward.
SAP Business Network: begin with an actual transaction
Documented practice. SAP describes supplier participation around operational documents and account 
interaction. Its supplier documentation includes onboarding associated with invitations or existing business 
activity, and a standard-account proposition for core transactions. More extensive services have their own 
commercial terms. [E10; E11]
Lesson for Universe. An invitation should lead to a useful task already relevant to the recipient: a review to 
confirm, a question to answer or a case whose response is ready. “Register now because a portal exists” is not 
the strongest starting point.

Boundary. Universe is not being proposed as a new procurement network. Its no-data-access-fee direction is 
an Isuzu product choice, not a conclusion that every comparable platform is free.
TecCom: connect parts identification to the next commercial action
Documented practice. TecCom's order-management guidance describes inquiry and ordering steps, supplier 
responses and links from product selection to the working basket. It illustrates how product information and 
operational transactions can be connected instead of repeatedly transcribed. [E12]
Lesson for Universe. A part-family insight should lead directly to a scoped part question, an application check, 
an availability inquiry or a commercial case. The product loses much of its value when the user must copy a 
number out of a chart and start again elsewhere.
Boundary. A historical purchase is not current availability, and a catalogue relationship is not unconditional 
interchangeability. The transaction must retain those distinctions even when navigation becomes easier.
Catena-X: explicit purposes and lightweight participation
Documented practice. Catena-X's Minimal Data Provider Services standard describes an approach that 
supports data provision and the expression of access and usage conditions, including participation by 
organisations with limited technical capability. The standard is a technical ecosystem design, not evidence that 
a particular Isuzu participant needs that infrastructure. [E13]
Lesson for Universe. Participation should not require every dealer to become a data-integration specialist. 
Offer a simple route, retain the contribution's purpose and audience, and allow a more automated connection 
when the recurring value warrants it.
Boundary. Adopting these principles does not mean adopting an entire industrial data-space architecture. 
Technical access controls also cannot recall a copy already legitimately downloaded.
Standards as design aids, not substitutes for product judgement
Auto Care's ACES and PIES release material illustrates the separation of application-related data from product-
information standards. That distinction is useful for Universe even though those standards' principal market 
context is not the same as Isuzu Europe's. AIM classification, product content and approved vehicle 
applicability should not be collapsed into one label. [E14]
GOV.UK's service standard emphasises solving a user's complete problem rather than arranging the service 
around organisational boundaries. The proposed Universe journeys use that principle: the question begins with 
a participant's job and ends with an answer or a decision, not with an internal data handoff. [E15]
The combined lesson is not to acquire every feature seen elsewhere. It is to make a small number of complete 
services work across the boundaries that participants currently have to navigate themselves.

The experience: one place to complete the

Role-based entry, shared context
The distributor should enter Universe through its current commercial work: the review period, significant 
purchasing changes, open dealer questions, support awaiting a response and actions due for review. The dealer 
should enter through its own requests, applicable range information and agreed follow-ups. Neither should 
have to navigate an organisational diagram before doing the task that brought it there.
A network map and the parts portfolio are valuable ways to explore. They should be available throughout the 
experience, but neither should be a mandatory visual obstacle. The user must be able to reach the same dealer 
through a name, an account reference, an open case or a location. It should be possible to reach the same part 
through a number, a description, a family or a validated application.
The selected context should remain visible: distributor, purchasing account, physical site where established, 
reporting period, source coverage and relevant part family. Changing one should update the dependent 
observations deliberately. It should never quietly replace a dealer's period with a network default or combine a 
historical purchase with a current price without saying so.
Organise around intentions, not internal models
AIM, IDS and PRISM should remain identifiable capabilities, especially for expert users and explanation of a 
result. Everyday actions should be written in the participant's language. “Explain this purchasing change”, 
“Request support”, “Check an alternative”, “Review this dealer” and “Resolve an availability issue” are clearer 
entry points than requiring the user to choose an analytical model first.
The portfolio remains a broad view of the parts range. It should support exploration beyond the current top 
sellers and help a user understand where a range sits. The service experience then narrows that view to the 
parts, vehicles and cases that matter. Removing the old sales cube should not mean removing the ability to 
examine a concrete part's purchasing history. Those are different design decisions. [I07]
The common case record
The principal unit of useful exchange should be a case: a question with context, evidence, a responsible 
response and an outcome. A case is not necessarily a complaint or a discount application. It can be a request to 
explain a pattern, validate an application, investigate supply, pursue an opportunity or implement an agreed 
improvement.
The case should retain its identity as it moves between functions. A technical answer may resolve the 
application question while a commercial decision remains open. A supply response may explain timing without 
settling whether the customer purchased. Separate states prevent a case from appearing complete simply 
because one department has replied.

The connected dealer review
The job to be done
The distributor's review should explain the dealer's purchasing and agree the next useful action. It should not 
be a sequence of unrelated charts, an assessment inspection or an obligation to invent an opportunity every 
month.
Before the meeting, Universe should prepare a short review from the selected period and coverage. The 
preparation should identify changes worth discussing, the parts and families behind them, relevant existing 
cases and prior actions. It should make clear when an apparent change is caused by incomplete reporting, an 
account transfer or a classification revision.
During the review, the distributor and dealer should be able to follow the evidence from an overall movement 
to a family and the relevant parts. They should see available Genuine/BVP relationships, suitable market 
context and the dealer's relevant IDS responses without being required to infer that those sources explain one 
another.
Afterwards, the product should retain the agreed decision, responsible people or roles, expected next step and 
review date. “No action” can be correct. So can a technical check or a correction to the data. A compulsory 
improvement action would create activity without demonstrating value.
What the participant contributes
The core input is the existing sales-to-dealer information on an agreed basis. The useful additional contribution 
is often a small explanation: a contract moved, a branch changed its buying process, stock had already been 
acquired, a customer did not proceed, a range was unsuitable, or a supply problem interrupted purchasing.
Those explanations should not be demanded for every declining row. The product should help select the few 
issues that matter to the review. The dealer can confirm the scope of its statement and choose whether a 
sensitive detail is visible only to the distributor or is needed for an ISZE support request.
What Universe adds
AIM should supply a stable vocabulary for the families and parts involved. PRISM should supply the 
appropriately scoped commercial and fleet context. IDS should contribute the dealer's own statement where 
the question is relevant and the identity connection is established. Open support, technical and availability 
cases should show what has already been discussed so the next review does not repeat the same question.
The most useful comparison is often the dealer's own covered history. Peer purchasing patterns may add 
context, but should not imply equivalent service potential without the necessary vehicle and operating 
information. For a category mix comparison, the product should state the selection of peers and what the 
comparison does not measure.
A proposed review sequence
The review opens with the previous agreed actions and the current question, not a general ranking. The user 
then examines the material movement and whether its reporting basis is comparable. Relevant parts and 
applications follow. Local explanation is captured alongside the observation. The group chooses a response, 
confirms ownership and identifies what would demonstrate a useful result.
A meeting brief should be generated from that same record, with the permitted audience and selected period 
preserved. It should not create another manually maintained version of the facts. The dealer should be able to

Why this earns better data
A participant has a reason to correct a name, explain a purchasing change or confirm an action when that 
information changes the review it will actually attend. It has little reason to improve a remote report that 
returns no response. The review becomes the recurring context in which both source corrections and 
commercial explanations are useful.
Success is a better-prepared discussion, a justified decision and reduced repetition. A completed review is not 
proof of incremental revenue. It is a service outcome that can later be connected to the appropriate 
commercial result.
Quotation and commercial-support service
Begin with the business at risk or worth pursuing
The quotation service should help a distributor or dealer assemble a defensible answer to a specific 
opportunity. The trigger might be an alternative price, an unfamiliar application, a fleet tender, a delivery 
constraint or a request for a Special Price Agreement, abbreviated SPA. The participant should be able to open 
the case directly from the relevant part or purchasing history.
The service should reuse the existing commercial approval route. It should not establish a parallel discount 
programme simply because the interface is new. Where ITUK's Don't Lose the Deal programme is relevant, 
Universe should attach evidence and outcomes to the established process rather than treat historical rebate 
paperwork as a reconstructed set of successful cases. [I03; I07]
Ask only for the evidence that changes the decision
A basic case may need the part, quantity, application, required date and the reason ordinary terms do not solve 
the situation. A competitive case may additionally need a source-dated alternative, product-match status and a 
comparable transaction basis. The initial record need not include the end customer's personal details or the 
dealer's resale margin.
A participant should be able to state that a detail is unknown or commercially restricted. Universe can then 
identify the consequence: an application check is still possible, but a like-for-like price assessment is not; or a 
provisional response can be prepared, but final terms need one more confirmation. This is a narrower and 
more credible approach than requesting every conceivable field at entry.

The answer must be more than approved or rejected
The response should explain what has been assessed and which conditions apply. It may offer existing terms, a 
technically suitable BVP option, a scoped SPA, a delivery alternative, further technical investigation or no 
change. A rejection should identify the reason the evidence did not support the request or the option still 
available. It should not become an unexplained closed status.
When financial support is approved, the record should state eligible parts and quantities, effective period, 
applicable conditions, currency, approval reference and the party receiving it. These are the terms of the actual 
case, not a new mandatory field list for all distributor reporting.
The participant should be able to retrieve the response without searching several email chains. A later 
modification should be versioned. An expired quote should not continue to appear as the currently approved 
offer.
Capture the outcome at the appropriate time
The outcome request should be short and attached to the quotation's natural close: won, lost, withdrawn, 
postponed, partially supplied or still unresolved. A reason can be added when known. Where a participant can 
link the approved case to an order, that is stronger evidence than recollection, but it still does not establish the 
counterfactual that the business would otherwise have been lost.
The product should distinguish the approval decision from the customer outcome and from the actual support 
delivered. A favourable quotation may not convert; an approved quantity may not be purchased; a credit may 
be issued later. Each is a separate event in the same record.
Product-generated learning
Over repeated cases, Universe can learn which parts, applications and competitive situations create the most 
unresolved questions. It can identify repeated missing documentation or recurring requests for an alternative 
that the range does not provide. That learning should improve the product offer and ISZE response, not merely 
produce a league table of dealers who request support.
The useful metric is not the number of requests approved. It is the quality and timeliness of the decision, the 
participant effort, the known outcome and the appropriate economic result after costs and displacement. A 
technically correct no-discount answer can be valuable if it resolves a mistaken comparison.
Availability, range and selected stock analysis
Three questions that must remain separate
A purchasing history can show what was bought and when. An availability record can show what a supplier said 
it could provide at a particular time. A stock record can show what a participant held on a defined basis. None 
is a substitute for the others.
Universe should begin with a service that explains purchasing continuity and helps raise a focused availability 
question. With optional operational data, it can progress to selected stock and range analysis. It should not 
label the difference between upstream billing and downstream purchases as inventory movement. [I03; I08]

The first useful availability case
A dealer should be able to choose the affected part, required quantity, required date and application from its 
current work. It can record whether the request is for a planned job, a quotation or a vehicle already awaiting 
parts. The distributor should add the available fulfilment position it actually knows, and ISZE should contribute 
the relevant supplier-side status where the escalation requires it.
The answer may be a dated availability confirmation, an expected update, an approved alternative, a partial 
delivery plan or a technical reason that the requested part should not be used. The participant's effort should 
buy this response. It should not merely make the distributor's supply problem more visible to its supplier 
without reciprocity.
If the participant shares an outstanding-order extract, that establishes outstanding orders under its field 
definitions. It does not, on its own, prove a customer lost sale, the absence of another source or responsibility 
for every purchasing decline. The case should distinguish requested, available, allocated, dispatched, received 
and fitted wherever those states are actually observed.
Earn selected stock information through a specific study
A recurring full inventory file is not an entry condition. The first stock exchange might cover a few relevant part 
families or a selected list of repeated requests. Before asking, show what purchasing evidence can and cannot 
explain, identify the decision stock information would improve and agree the returned analysis.
A useful stock study may need on-hand quantity, committed quantity, available-to-promise basis, observation 
time, replenishment lead time and open requests. These should be requested only to the extent the participant 
already records them and the proposed analysis needs them. A simple statement about one part in one case is 
not permission to begin collecting the whole inventory.
The returned analysis should separate slow or intermittent purchasing from excess stock, and a stockout from a 
general lack of demand. Suggested changes should be presented as scenarios with the participant's constraints: 
space, service requirements, returnability, order rules, lead time and working capital. Historical movement 
alone does not determine the correct stock level.
Range development without forced substitution
AIM can show parts families purchased regularly, intermittently or not at all. Documented applications and 
Genuine/BVP relationships can help identify questions about range coverage. However, an unpurchased family 
is not automatically a lost opportunity. The dealer may serve a different fleet, obtain legitimate supply 
elsewhere, or have no relevant demand during the period.
The product should ask a commercial question rather than deliver a verdict: is this range relevant, is it 
available, does the participant know it, and is a local constraint preventing use? When the answer is no 
relevant application, the opportunity should close as explained. That is useful information for both the dealer 
and ISZE.
Application and Genuine/BVP guidance
The value proposition
A parts counter should be able to move from an uncertain part or application to a clear, traceable technical 
answer. This service can earn highly valuable information about the vehicle, the job, the requested component

and the reason an alternative is being considered. It should do so only when the information is needed for that 
answer.
AIM supports the family and commercial context. The authoritative application source determines whether a 
part is suitable. A Genuine/BVP relationship provides a candidate for review, not unconditional fitment 
approval. A supersession reference identifies a recorded replacement relationship, not necessarily equivalence 
in every circumstance. These distinctions should appear naturally in the experience. [I09]
The proposed interaction
The user begins with a part number, a product description or an application. Universe displays the known 
classification, original transaction identity, relevant replacement information and relationship status. It asks for 
the minimum additional application detail required by the technical question. Where necessary, a vehicle 
identifier can be handled through the appropriate restricted route rather than displayed in every commercial 
record.
The result should say what is confirmed, on which source and version, and under which conditions. It should 
distinguish an approved application, a documented catalogue relationship, a possible association and an 
unresolved match. The user should not have to infer confidence from the presence of a picture or from a 
percentage produced by an artificial intelligence (AI) model.
The next action should remain connected: availability inquiry, quotation, technical escalation or saved answer. 
If the part relationship is corrected later, the cases that used the earlier version should be identifiable without 
rewriting their history.
Learn from friction, not only from successful searches
Unsuccessful application searches are valuable when they are captured accurately. The reason may be 
incomplete vehicle information, unclear catalogue wording, a missing local range, an unavailable BVP 
relationship or a genuine technical exception. A structured unanswered question is a better input than an 
invented match.
The service should let the counter record an observed fitment problem without requiring it to determine the 
root cause. The report can include the application, part, relevant evidence and outcome. A qualified technical 
function should determine whether the issue concerns selection, installation, compatibility or a product defect. 
Commercial reporting should not turn a local observation into a manufacturer-wide quality conclusion.
BVP success is not a maximum share target
The objective is appropriate placement: retain a suitable offering for the customer's need without accidentally 
displacing a more appropriate product or misrepresenting quality. BVP share alone cannot show success. The 
relevant questions are whether the alternative was suitable, available, understood and used in a commercial 
situation where it created value.
The product should not imply that country of origin determines quality or that a lower-priced tier is the correct 
answer to every competitive quotation. It should support a documented choice and retain the participant's 
explanation. Aggregate learning can then distinguish an awareness issue from a range or application issue.

IDS as a living improvement conversation
Use the existing assessment, do not replace it
IDS records dealer self-assessment. Universe should make that existing work easier to use, relate it to relevant 
commercial questions and support the follow-through. It should not create a new distributor audit, recalculate 
scores from invented weights or establish an additional compliance league table. [I02; I07; I10]
The most useful unit is the dealer's answer to a specific, versioned question, together with its context. A 
summary score can aid navigation where its basis is known, but it should not conceal unanswered sections, 
inapplicable questions or missing source evidence.
Connect without claiming causation
A dealer may state that fast-moving parts are reviewed regularly. Its purchasing history can inform a discussion 
about that practice. It does not prove that the process did or did not operate. Similarly, a statement about 
availability is not a measured stock position, and an inspection-sign-off indicator is not a record of filter 
replacement.
The connection should therefore be framed as a review prompt. “This answer may be relevant to this 
purchasing pattern” is a useful starting point. “This score explains the decline” is an unsupported causal claim 
unless additional evidence establishes it.
The relationship between a question and an analysis should itself be maintained: why it is relevant, who 
reviewed the connection, which question version it applies to and what inference is prohibited. A code reused 
for a different question must not silently inherit the earlier analytical link.
Make follow-through useful to the dealer
After a review, an action should record the intended improvement, what support is required, the responsible 
role, timing and the evidence that will indicate completion. The dealer must be able to distinguish its own 
commitment from an ISZE or distributor commitment. An action that waits on parts training should show who 
owes the training, not only that the dealer is overdue.
The record should support decisions such as completed, changed, no longer relevant, deferred with reason or 
awaiting support. Closing an action should not automatically improve an assessment score. A later assessment 
remains a separate observation with its own date and method.
An empty action register means that actions are not recorded there. It does not establish that the dealer or 
distributor has done nothing. Universe should make the act of recording easier by generating the brief during 
the review and avoiding a second report afterwards.

Fleet, workshop and service-business support
A service that deserves operational data
The strongest reason to share operational information is a decision that cannot be answered reliably without it. 
Examples include planning a defined service programme, validating a replacement assumption, understanding 
the requirements of an identified contract population or investigating repeated parts usage. The return should 
be a usable plan or analysis, not simply a larger database for ISZE.
The initial distributor product should remain useful without workshop records. At the same time, the complete 
product design should show how a willing dealer can obtain a deeper service. Deferring every operational 
exchange until an arbitrary maturity level would hide the long-term value and make the dealer experience 
incomplete.
Define the population before the rate
A workshop-observed population is the set of vehicles represented in the records under a stated period and 
selection rule. It is not automatically the workshop's complete service responsibility or geographic market. A 
vehicle can appear at several workshops. A registered operator address may be a head office rather than the 
place where the vehicle operates. [I05; I08]
The product must name the population the participant intends to analyse: vehicles serviced, vehicles under an 
eligible contract, a confirmed fleet customer's vehicles, or an assigned service population where such an 
assignment actually exists. The numerator and exposure should refer to that same population.
A rate for observed vehicles may be useful without representing the whole market. It should not be described 
as a minimum market-share figure or as a dealer performance score. The service can return an answer scoped 
to the observed population and identify what would be required to answer the broader question.
Separate observation, benchmark and requirement
Recorded replacement events describe work found in the source. An observed interval describes the distance 
or time between those recorded events under a stated method. An approved maintenance requirement comes 
from the applicable technical schedule and conditions. A forecast or expected quantity combines that 
requirement with exposure and other assumptions.
The product should make those distinctions visible. It should not derive an interval from a population's own 
consumption and then present the resulting quantity as independent proof that the population consumed too 
little. It should also account for time-based requirements, whichever-comes-first rules, relevant duty cycle and 
component quantities where applicable.

Tender and fleet-opportunity service
Make an opportunity usable, not merely visible
A tender notice is useful only when the participant can decide whether it is relevant and what to do next. 
Universe should connect an eligible opportunity to the distributor's market, the relevant vehicle or service 
scope, applicable parts knowledge and the responsible commercial route. It should not turn a feed of notices 
into an assumed revenue pipeline.
The first experience can be deliberately small: a country-relevant notice, a clear deadline where known, a short 
relevance explanation, the source and a way to accept, decline or assign investigation. The participant should 
not have to visit a separate product to maintain a second habit. The opportunity should become a case in the 
same working environment.
The information the service can earn
A participant's response can improve the relevance of future notices. “Not our segment”, “already covered”, 
“no suitable range”, “deadline passed” and “pursuing with this role” are useful when they arise naturally from 
an opportunity review. They should not become mandatory reporting for every notice.

applicable vehicles, service scope, quantities or duration, technical conditions, required delivery and permitted

A tender can be won, lost, cancelled, not pursued or unresolved. A recorded award does not automatically

Explanations and commercial memory
The information that transactions do not contain
Transactions record that something occurred under a particular source definition. They rarely contain the 
complete reason. A family may decline because a customer moved, stock was purchased earlier, an application 
changed, supply failed or a local quotation was lost. Universe should earn these explanations in the context of 
the decision they inform.
The record should distinguish an observed fact, a participant-reported explanation, a corroborated explanation 
and an analytical hypothesis. These are not grades of the person contributing; they are different kinds of 
evidence. A credible report of a local constraint is useful even when it cannot support a network-wide causal 
conclusion.
Use a short, expandable reason structure
The first entry should be simple enough for a counter or parts manager to complete while the issue is current. 
A small set of reason families can guide it: price or commercial terms; supply and timing; application or 
technical suitability; customer requirement; local stock position; account or coverage change; and other or 
unknown. Several can apply to the same case.
Additional detail should be requested only when it matters. A price-support decision may need an evidenced 
comparison. A network analysis may need an attributable quantity. A resolved application question may need 
no customer name. The system should not infer a precise share of a sales change from the existence of a 
reason code.
A free-text explanation remains valuable, especially when the reason does not fit the initial structure. AI may 
propose a category and a concise summary, but the contributor should confirm it. The original wording should 
be retained under the appropriate access rules.

Make unresolved work visible to the responsible function
A repeated question is also evidence about ISZE's service. If several participants ask for the same clarification, 
the right action may be better documentation or a range decision rather than another request for local data. 
The product should help identify these systemic issues and assign an owner.
The reasons library should therefore support two directions of improvement: better interpretation of the 
distributor's situation and better performance by Isuzu in answering it. A platform that measures only dealer 
behaviour will miss much of the value it could create.

Support several practical contribution routes
A simple file upload may be the appropriate first route for a monthly extract. A short in-product form may be

agreed application interface can support a mature recurring exchange. A locally calculated aggregate may be 
preferable for sensitive financial or customer information.
These routes should lead to the same business definitions, not separate versions of the product. A participant 
using a file should receive the same relevant service as one using an automated connection. Automation 
reduces recurring effort; it should not be mistaken for superior willingness or quality by itself.
The product should remember approved mappings and explain import exceptions in business terms. “The 
dealer account is new” is more useful than an unexplained parser error. A correction should identify the 
affected period and records and make clear whether previous results have changed.
Be disciplined about requests
Every additional request should have a named use and a current beneficiary. Before asking, confirm that the 
information is not already held, not derivable safely from existing evidence, and not better calculated by the 
participant. Explain the smallest acceptable response and what happens if it is not provided.
Requests should be consolidated by case and recipient. A dealer should not receive separate questionnaires 
from the technical, pricing and network teams asking for the same vehicle and part information. The case 
owner should coordinate the request, while each specialist retains responsibility for its conclusion.
Mobile and low-friction participation
The parts counter may need a concise interaction rather than a full desktop analysis. A mobile-capable case 
view should allow the person to read the response, confirm an outcome and add the limited context needed. 
Any photo or document upload should be optional and purpose-specific, with review before sharing wider than 
intended.
The manager's desktop view can provide deeper comparison, portfolio exploration and review preparation. 
Both should refer to the same case and state. Notifications should take the user to the exact action rather than 
a general home page. A participant should be able to control routine digests and avoid alerts for matters it 
cannot act on.

The economics of the exchange
Identify the beneficiary and the value type
A benefit to ISZE is not automatically a benefit to the distributor. A dealer's workshop gain is not automatically 
distributor profit. A credit from ISZE is a transfer between parties before considering whether it creates new 
business. The economic record must name the beneficiary and distinguish financial flows from productive 
improvement.
The proposed model retains the revised value-exchange paper's separation of lower purchase cost, funding 
delivered, preparation capacity and participant-confirmed profit. It adds explicit treatment of programme cost, 
displacement, timing and double counting. [I03]
Lower purchase cost
For a defined eligible set of parts, compare the agreed baseline net purchase terms with the approved terms 
under comparable conditions. Multiply the difference by the actual eligible quantity on the appropriate basis. 
Include relevant charges, currency and effective period where they affect the comparison.
This establishes a purchase-cost difference, not necessarily immediate profit. Parts may remain in stock; some 
benefit may be passed onward; sales volumes and costs may change. A reference dealer price or an annual 
average billing price is not a substitute for the actual agreed baseline.
The record should also identify whether a later credit is already reflected in the net amount. Otherwise the 
same support could be counted once as a lower price and again as a credit.
Funding and assistance
Requested funding, authorised funding and funds paid or credited are separate states. A participant benefits 
from a clear decision even before money is delivered, but the financial measure should use the correct state. 
In-kind assistance should be described as assistance delivered, with its actual cost where known, rather than 
automatically equating ISZE's cost with the participant's value.
Support can also prevent an unnecessary concession. A corrected application comparison may show that a 
blanket price reduction was not justified. That is a possible commercial benefit, but it needs an explicit baseline 
and documented decision. It should not be inferred simply because a request was declined.

Capacity released
Measure the time required to complete a comparable task before and after the service, including data 
preparation, correction, review and follow-up. Net time released is the old effort less the new total effort. A 
faster review that omits the hard questions or mislabels values is not an improvement.
An agreed hourly cost can express the result as estimated capacity value. That is not automatically cash saved. 
A business may use the released time for other work without reducing expenditure. Realised cost reduction 
should be claimed only when the financial effect is evidenced.
Gross profit and incremental contribution
Gross profit is revenue less the defined cost of goods sold. A participant's operating costs and the programme's 
cost should be assessed separately when judging incremental contribution. The analysis should not describe a 
change after an intervention as caused by Universe without considering alternative explanations.
Relevant explanations include demand changes, ordering timing, stock, price, product mix, account transfers, 
supply, other campaigns and displacement between Genuine and BVP. Additional purchases by one dealer may 
be business transferred from another part of the network. Moving the same business to a different month is 
not new annual business.
A credible evaluation can begin with a documented decision and a measured subsequent result without 
claiming full causal attribution. Where stronger attribution is necessary, define a suitable comparison and 
method before assessing the outcome. Do not reconstruct an attractive counterfactual after seeing the data.

The business case for ISZE
The investment case should identify the recurring cost of delivering the service: product maintenance, source 
management, specialist response capacity, support operations, training, integration, security, analytics and any 
commercial assistance. A low marginal cost for software does not mean the response service is free.
Returns may include better-targeted support, improved range decisions, less duplicated commercial 
preparation, fewer unresolved technical questions and additional contribution where evidenced. The business 
case should keep these categories separate and avoid counting the same sales improvement in several 
modules.
A modelled market-spend ceiling can describe strategic context. It should not be multiplied by an arbitrary 
recovery percentage to invent Universe's revenue case. The programme must demonstrate its own route from 
participant activity to decision to result.
The response ISZE owes
Software cannot substitute for an operating commitment
Universe creates a reciprocal obligation. When the product invites a participant to supply evidence for an 
answer, the receiving organisation must have the capacity and authority to answer. Otherwise, better data 
collection simply makes unanswered requests easier to count.
The service should identify the function responsible for triage, the specialist responsible for the substantive 
answer and the case owner responsible for keeping the participant informed. Those may be different people. 
The product should not allow a case to lose ownership because it moves from pricing to technical support or 
supply.
Define response classes before promising speed
A simple classification can separate a routine information response, an application check, a commercial 
decision, a supply investigation and a more complex service analysis. Each requires an agreed operating 
arrangement. The paper does not invent response-time targets; those should be set using observed workload, 
urgency and actual capacity.
The participant should nevertheless know what happens next. An acknowledgement is not a substantive 
answer. “Awaiting technical review” should identify the next review arrangement and the information still 
needed. When a response date changes, the case should retain the reason and notify the affected participant 
appropriately.
Urgent vehicle-off-road work should follow the established operational route. Universe can retain the linked 
commercial context, but should not imply that a general analytical queue replaces emergency support.
Workload and escalation
Case volume should be monitored by type, age, required expertise and rework, not only by total count. 
Repeated requests for the same clarification may justify a shared approved answer. Repeated supply questions 
may reveal a communication gap. Repeated returns of incomplete cases may indicate that the request form is 
poorly designed.
Escalation should advance the decision rather than merely notify more people. It should identify the 
unresolved question, the impact and the authority required. The participant should not have to know ISZE's 
internal organisational structure to find the correct next person.

Trust, data use and commercial boundaries
Make the agreement understandable in the product
Participants should be able to see what information is used for, who receives it, what they receive in return and 
how to correct it. The product should not depend on a broad phrase such as “improve our services” to explain 
every future use.
The established ownership and access arrangements should be implemented through clear service-specific 
rules. This chapter is not a request to reopen those arrangements. It translates the intended relationship into 
operational controls and identifies where a new use would differ from the agreed one.
Personal data and confidentiality are related but different
European Commission guidance identifies purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation 
and security among the principles governing personal-data processing. The UK's Information Commissioner's 
data-sharing code provides a framework for considering sharing arrangements and responsibilities. These are 
relevant foundations, not a legal assessment of each Universe workflow. [E19; E20]
The proposed product rule is to request the least identifying information that answers the question. 
Commercially sensitive data also requires protection even when it is not personal data. A distributor's stock 
position, a pending offer or a dealer's commercial explanation may deserve a restricted audience for business 
reasons independent of personal-data law.
A participant should know whether an entry is private to its organisation, shared with its distributor, part of a 
joint ISZE case or eligible for a reviewed aggregate. These choices should be understandable and enforced, not 
merely indicated by colours.
Avoid turning price evidence into price coordination
The European Commission's vertical-restraints guidelines distinguish resale-price maintenance from ordinary 
price monitoring and discuss the conditions surrounding information exchange in distribution relationships. 
They do not provide blanket clearance for every shared pricing field. [E21]
The proposed Universe boundary is clear: support a specific legitimate commercial assessment without 
prescribing dealer resale prices or exposing one participant's sensitive future pricing to competitors. A support 
case should identify the needed evidence and appropriate bilateral audience. Public advertised prices should 
retain observation date, tax, quantity, product-match and transaction-level distinctions.
Cross-market learning should focus on reviewed product and service lessons or suitably controlled aggregates. 
It should not allow a participant to reconstruct another distributor's live bids, customer terms or individual 
commercial strategy. No threshold or anonymisation label should be treated as an automatic guarantee against 
reconstruction.
The Data Act is not a general right to every commercial record
The European Commission's Data Act material describes access and sharing rules for data generated through 
connected products and related services, with scope and safeguards. Its vehicle-data guidance addresses 
application in the automotive context. That is not a general entitlement to every distributor invoice, workshop 
commercial record or inferred analysis. [E22; E23]
Universe should treat any future connected-product data service as a defined use with the appropriate rights, 
recipient and purpose. It should not tell dealers that all operational data must be supplied because connected 
vehicles exist. The product proposition remains an exchange that is useful in its own right.

Retention, portability and changes of purpose
Each class of record should have an appropriate retention rule and a clear explanation of what happens when a

Portability should apply to the participant's permitted record of contributions, responses and outcomes. 
Revoking future access does not recall a previously downloaded file. The product should therefore minimise 
unnecessary exports and avoid embedding other participants' information in a file intended for one recipient.
A new use should be evaluated as a new use. Information provided to resolve a selected supply problem should 
not quietly become a general dealer penalty measure. Information shared for an application check should not 
automatically become a marketing contact list.

should have access to a human route when the system has misunderstood a case.
These controls are part of the service quality. They are not a separate legal screen that can be ignored once the 
user has logged in.

Versioned definitions and reversible corrections
Every material analytical result should be traceable to its source version, period, population and method. A 
later classification release should not silently change the historical basis of a previously approved case. The 
product may offer a restated analysis, but should preserve what was known when the decision was made.
Corrections to monthly aggregates should replace the declared submission version rather than append another 
copy. Line-level sources should use their genuine transaction or line identifiers where available. Identical-
looking rows are not automatically duplicate transactions; removal needs a supported rule.
A source correction, a method revision and a genuine change in the business should be distinguishable in the 
review. This is especially important when participants are asked to explain a change that was actually created 
by a revised classification or missing month.
Integration contracts and graceful degradation
An integration should state its source meaning, period, required fields, allowed optional fields, correction 
procedure, expected refresh and known limits. The product should preserve a last-known-good view where 
appropriate and clearly indicate that it is not current rather than silently replacing missing data with zero.
A failure in one optional source should not disable unrelated useful work. A competitor price feed can be 
unavailable while the purchasing review continues. A dealer assessment can be unlinked while the commercial 
account remains usable. A service estimate can be withheld while recorded workshop activity is still shown 
accurately.
The return to a participant's existing system should be equally narrow and explicit. A case reference, approved 
response or confirmed outcome may be written back through an agreed interface. Universe should not 
independently modify stock, post an invoice or change commercial terms unless that action is specifically 
designed and authorised in the appropriate system.
Analytical rules that keep the product credible
A useful finding needs a defined comparison
A purchasing movement should identify its period and coverage. A part-family opportunity should identify the 
relevant applications and the evidence of demand. A per-vehicle measure should identify the population and 
exposure. A price comparison should identify the commercial level and product match.
These rules should be embedded in calculation and interface behaviour, not left to a cautionary paragraph at 
the end of a report. The objective is to make valid analysis easier and unsupported comparisons harder.

Keep the commercial flows separate
Parts billed by ISZE, distributor sales to dealer, workshop parts recorded as used and dealer sales to end 
customers are different observations. They should not be added together as network revenue. Differences 
between them may have several explanations and cannot be converted automatically into stock, customer 
demand or business lost to competitors. [I03; I08]
A case can display several flows side by side when the labels make their relationship clear. The point is to 
inform a question, not imply that they describe the same vehicles, shipments or transactions. A correct identity 
link is necessary for some comparisons, but never sufficient to establish every commercial relationship.
Distinguish composition from service potential
A dealer's own purchasing composition can be compared with its covered history without a fleet denominator. 
A peer group can provide descriptive context if the selection is explainable. That does not make the peer 
average the dealer's expected requirement.
Service potential requires applicable vehicles, relevant conditions and a supported model. No national fleet 
should be allocated across dealers by their sales, visits or geographic proximity merely to manufacture a rate. 
Multi-workshop vehicles must not create multiple full-year obligations for the same vehicle.
Treat uncertainty as part of the decision
A modelled scenario can be useful even when it is not precise, provided the assumptions and sensitivity are 
clear. Different scenarios should not be presented as a statistical confidence interval unless that is what they 
are. Observed behaviour, approved requirement and forecast remain separate analytical objects.
The product should identify which uncertainty changes the decision. If component quantity or schedule 
applicability dominates the result, additional decimal places in mileage do not create credibility. If no 
supported estimate is available, show the measured activity and the next useful question instead.
Preserve technical relationships
A part classification provides a commercial grouping. A catalogue pairing, a replacement reference and an 
approved application each establish different relationships. The product should preserve original part numbers 
and avoid implying that a mapping itself validates fitment.
Shared applicability must remain shared. A part that fits several vehicle classes should not be apportioned to 
those classes by fleet share and then described as measured purchasing for each class. The same market actual 
repeated across class rows should not be summed or divided by each class expectation to create apparent 
service cover. [I07; I09]
Rates above expectations require interpretation, not cosmetic correction
A ratio above a modelled expectation may reflect scope differences, cross-application sales, timing, an 
incomplete denominator or genuine activity beyond the assumption. It should not be silently capped to make a 
chart look plausible, nor treated as proof that all possible demand was won.
The appropriate action is to inspect the definition and retain an explicit method-specific convention where one 
is deliberately used. Competing internal and external measures should be labelled for the question each 
answers, not averaged into a new undefined measure.

AI assistance within a controlled service
Where AI can add useful capability
AI can help participants find relevant evidence, summarise a case, draft a response, suggest questions, 
translate working text and identify records that deserve review. It can reduce the effort of navigating a large 
body of parts and commercial information. It should not become the authority for technical suitability, financial 
approval or access permission.
The US National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Generative AI Profile provides a voluntary risk-
management reference for examining such systems. The Open Worldwide Application Security Project 
(OWASP) prompt-injection guidance highlights risks from instructions embedded in material that a model 
processes. These sources support treating AI as a component requiring controls and evaluation, not as a 
replacement for source authority. [E17; E18]
The assistant should act on the same business objects
An assistant answering “why did this family change?” should use the selected distributor, account, period and 
source basis rather than constructing a fresh, disconnected analysis. It should identify whether the explanation 
comes from data, a participant's statement or an inference. It should be able to create a draft case from the 
current context, subject to user review.
For an application question, it should retrieve the approved technical source or identify the unresolved 
relationship. It should not convert a plausible language match into fitment approval. For pricing, it can 
assemble comparable evidence and draft a request, but should not authorise terms or prescribe resale 
behaviour.
For IDS, it can locate the relevant recorded answer and help prepare a review prompt. It should not invent 
missing answers, recompute scores without the actual method or infer a dealership's conduct from its score.
Control actions outside the language model
Permissions should be checked by the application for every retrieval and action. A model's instruction to “only 
show the right dealer” is not an access-control mechanism. Source documents, uploaded quotations and 
external pages should be treated as untrusted content, not as instructions to change access or send 
information elsewhere.
Privileged actions should require the appropriate deterministic checks and human approval. These include 
sharing externally, approving support, changing authoritative mappings or writing to a transaction system. The 
model should not receive broader access merely because a user asks a broad question.
Evaluate the behaviour that matters
Test the assistant on genuine difficult cases: a missing reporting month; conflicting part relationships; an 
ambiguous branch; a source with unknown currency; a prompt hidden in an attachment; a request for another 
distributor's pricing; and an unsupported claim that an IDS answer explains a purchasing decline.
The evaluation should examine whether the answer is correct, whether the source supports it, whether the 
intended audience is respected and whether the assistant asks for information that is actually necessary. A 
fluent explanation is not a pass if it hides uncertainty or invents a cause.
The service should also support a useful non-AI route. A participant must be able to search, inspect the 
evidence and use the case workflow without relying entirely on generated text. AI should accelerate the 
service, not become the only way to understand it.

Product quality and usability
Design for ordinary working conditions
A review may be conducted on a desktop, a parts question on a phone, and a response read between other 
tasks. The experience should work with clear text, keyboard navigation, reliable focus, readable tables and a list 
alternative to maps. It should not rely on animation, colour alone or complex drag interactions to complete an 
essential task.
The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 provide a recognised accessibility reference. An 
appropriate product target is conformance to its AA level, assessed in the actual implementation rather than 
claimed from design intent. Accessibility work should include representative users and real tasks, not only 
automated checks. [E16]
Make status and limitations understandable
The interface should distinguish “not supplied”, “not applicable”, “not yet matched”, “out of date”, “under 
review” and “confirmed no activity”. These states should not all appear as an empty box or zero. The wording 
should tell the user whether a correction, further evidence or no action is appropriate.
A technical or financial reference should state what it is. “Reference valuation” should not be styled as 
measured turnover. An assessment should show its round and available date, not a fabricated current 
timestamp. A saved case should make clear whether its evidence has been restated since the decision.
Give the user a recoverable path
Forms should save progress, preserve entered context and explain the specific field that prevents continuation. 
Imports should return actionable exceptions rather than reject an entire useful file because of an unrelated 
optional field. Significant changes should be reversible or versioned.
The user should also be able to stop a case or decline an optional request without losing unrelated work. Clear 
exits and understandable consequences support participation more effectively than a form that forces an 
answer to every question.
Control notifications and the cost of attention
The most important notification is the one that advances a decision the recipient owns. Routine summaries 
should be configurable. Escalations should explain the unresolved issue and the next action, not simply 
increase the number of people copied.
The product should measure whether alerts are acted on and whether participants consider them relevant. A 
large number of notifications can indicate a poorly prioritised service, not engagement. Case owners should be 
able to consolidate related questions so the participant is not asked the same thing by several functions.

The long-term product should be designed broadly, while each development increment should prove a 
complete useful interaction. That is different from building every data layer first or creating a collection of 
attractive but disconnected demonstrations.

Objections worth answering directly
“We already have the data in our own system.” The answer should not be that Universe also stores it. The 
value is the parts knowledge, relevant external context, cross-functional response and history of decisions that 
the participant's extract does not provide by itself.
“We do not want Isuzu to see our margins.” The core service does not require them. A particular outcome may 
be confirmed as an aggregate calculated locally, or remain unknown. The product should not use a price-
support request as a route to collect an entire commercial book.
“We are too busy to fill in reasons.” Do not ask for every row. Capture the explanation on the case being 
worked, reuse context and ask only when the answer changes the decision. Measure the actual effort and 
reduce it.
“The model does not understand our market.” Let the participant identify the missing circumstance, 
distinguish the model from measured activity and correct the relevant scope. A model that cannot represent a 
known local condition should not deliver a performance verdict.
“This will become an audit.” The design must make that concern falsifiable. It should show participant-visible 
evidence, correctable interpretations, optional sensitive contributions and the commitments ISZE owes. It 
should not rely on a reassuring slogan while the actual product creates a league table.
The long-term product and its limits
A service that improves through use
The strongest long-term asset is the connection between a parts question, its commercial circumstances, the 
response and the result. Over time, Universe can identify recurring range issues, improve technical guidance, 
target support more precisely and reduce repeated preparation. This value depends on quality and appropriate 
use, not on retaining every raw record indefinitely.

Appendix A — Detailed service blueprints
Connected review: from preparation to a returned decision
Trigger. A scheduled distributor–dealer review, a material purchasing question or a follow-up on an existing 
action. The trigger should identify the account and period before the analysis begins.
Preparation. Universe assembles the covered purchasing history, relevant parts families, previous explanations 
and open cases. It highlights changes in reporting or classification before proposing commercial interpretation. 
The distributor chooses the questions worth discussing rather than receiving a compulsory list of every 
exception.
Participant input. Confirm the selected identity, add local context on the chosen questions and correct source 
issues. A new statement should record the period it describes. The participant can withhold unrelated sensitive 
detail without losing the review.
Returned work. A source-aware explanation, a relevant technical or commercial question, an agreed response 
and a reusable review brief. The brief should distinguish what the dealer agreed to do from what ISZE or the 
distributor agreed to provide.
Completion. The review records a decision, including no action where justified. The next review begins from 
that record. The benefit test is whether the discussion became more accurate or less repetitive and whether 
the response was delivered.
Quotation support: from request to known outcome
Trigger. A participant has a defined quotation that ordinary information or terms do not resolve. The case 
begins from the part, application or opportunity already selected.
Preparation. Universe carries the historical purchasing context where relevant, the applicable relationships and 
any authorised market reference. It identifies missing evidence that actually prevents a decision. It should not 
require a customer name when a case reference is sufficient.
Participant input. Quantity, required date, relevant application, reason for the request and the evidence 
needed for the specific comparison. Future sensitive pricing remains restricted to the appropriate case 
audience.
Returned work. A response through the existing commercial route: approved support, ordinary terms, a 
suitable alternative, a technical check or a reasoned decline. The response states conditions and remains 
versioned.
Completion. Record the participant's outcome when known and the support actually delivered. A case can be 
lost or withdrawn and still produce useful learning. Approval is not counted as conversion, and conversion is 
not automatically incremental business.
Availability: from urgent question to a credible answer
Trigger. A part is required for a quotation, planned work or a vehicle awaiting service. Established urgent 
operational procedures remain in force.
Preparation. Carry part, application, quantity and required date. Retrieve only the available status that the 
source genuinely supports. State its observation time and whether it concerns on-hand, allocation or expected 
supply.
Participant input. The selected request and local constraint; optionally a linked order or relevant stock 
statement. A short statement about one case is not a recurring full-stock agreement.

Symbolic purchase-cost example
Let the baseline net purchase price for eligible part i be B_i, the approved comparable price be A_i and the 
actual eligible quantity be Q_i. The purchase-cost difference is the sum of (B_i minus A_i) multiplied by Q_i, 
adjusted for relevant charges and credits that are not already included.
This result should be labelled as a purchase-cost difference on the declared basis. It should not be described as 
additional distributor gross profit until the sold quantities, cost-of-sales treatment and relevant revenue effect 
support that interpretation. A later credit already reflected in A_i must not be added again.
Symbolic preparation-capacity example
Let old effort be the total time required for the comparable task before Universe. Let new effort include 
submission, correction, review, response and follow-up time after the service is used. Net capacity released is 
old effort minus new effort.
If the participant agrees an hourly cost, that time can be expressed as estimated capacity value. The financial 
result remains distinct from cash savings. A task that appears faster only because it omits unresolved accounts 
or a necessary technical check does not pass the comparability test.
Symbolic incremental-contribution example
Define the relevant change in gross profit using actual revenue and cost of goods sold on a matched basis. Then 
consider support and operating costs not already included, displacement, timing and other interventions. The 
result should state which effects are observed and which rely on a modelled counterfactual.
This is deliberately not a programme ROI estimate. No approved Universe budget, realised distributor margin 
improvement or causal uplift is established by the supplied material. The formulas define what later evidence 
must support rather than fill the gap with illustrative earnings.

Appendix E — Measurement definitions and 
experiment design
Useful repeat participation
Define the eligible task population before measuring adoption. A dealer that has no relevant quotation in a 
period should not be classified as disengaged because it opened no quotation case. A distributor whose 
monthly review is complete may not need daily visits.
A useful repeat measure is the proportion of participants with eligible work who use the service again and 
obtain a documented response. Report task type, observation period and coverage. Keep the formal reporting 
submission separate from voluntary use of optional services.
Purpose-specific information coverage
For a selected service, define which information is needed to assess an eligible case. Report the share of cases 
with usable, known information and separately the reasons for absence: not relevant, unavailable, declined, 
not yet due or awaiting confirmation.
Do not increase the apparent rate by removing difficult cases from the denominator after seeing their results. 
Do not treat “unknown” as a failed participant. It is a valid evidence state whose frequency may reveal a poor 
request design or a genuinely unobservable fact.
Response quality
Measure whether the response answers the submitted question, identifies conditions and is understandable to 
the participant. A technically correct but unusable answer can still fail the service. A fast acknowledgement 
should not be substituted for time to substantive response.
Review a sample of closed cases for evidence, unresolved assumptions, rework and participant outcome. 
Separate administrative closure from confirmed resolution. Include no-action decisions and cases with 
unknown outcomes rather than selecting only successful examples.
Evaluation designs
A before/after comparison can monitor operational change, but should identify changes in task difficulty, 
source coverage, staffing and other interventions. A matched comparison or staged introduction may support 
stronger conclusions where practical. The choice should be made before claiming the financial result, not after 
selecting favourable cases.
For usability testing, use the same underlying question through the existing process and the proposed service 
where feasible. Observe time, errors, repeated entry and the user's ability to distinguish a reference valuation 
from a transaction. Test both a complete case and a case with missing information.
For participation research, compare the recipient's stated reason for contributing with its subsequent 
behaviour. Willingness to share a selected stock snapshot after a useful availability review is evidence about 
that exchange; it is not permission to collect all inventory data forever.
