## The one-sentence job, in my own words

Write one internal paper that explains what the Isuzu Universe programme is, proves each of its five
pillars with the best material from every earlier edition, shows each pillar doing commercial work on a
real named example with real euros and a stated period, states plainly what the evidence will not carry,
and carries a contents page, a figure list and a table list whose page numbers are correct in the
rendered file — so that the business owner can see in twenty minutes what to approve, what to fund, and
what is still unproven.

If what follows does not satisfy that sentence, it is not the deliverable.
