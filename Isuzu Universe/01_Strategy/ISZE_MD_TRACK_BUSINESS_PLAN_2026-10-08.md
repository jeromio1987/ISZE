## 2. Ambition

**Network competitive defence with measurable return** — for Jerome's book first:

1. **Jobs kept** — workshop jobs that would have left to independent / used / internet quotes stay with the dealer (primary return).
2. **Parts families** — injectors, aftertreatment (DPF/NOx), driveline, turbos, DEF, sensors: the hard-part families in the ITUK register, not a vague "parts" bucket.
3. **%GM discipline** — list and SPA stay the structure; Don't Lose the Deal stays rare, evidenced, and capped. No silent erosion of dealer net into a second price list.
4. **Evidence habit** — competitor price or reason code, won/lost, order reference on every new case. Without that, there is no board story.

Ambition is **not** "spend 0.5% everywhere". Ambition is **prove save-rate in ITUK + IUK (+ one small market), then decide**.

## 4.5 Board measurement definition (Proposed)

**Save-rate** = supported cases marked *won* ÷ supported cases with a known outcome (won or lost). Cases left *unknown* stay disclosed separately — do not pretend they were wins.

**Return numerator (Proposed):** on won cases, ISZE billed euros on the linked order (or the supported lines if the full order cannot be tied), times ISZE margin on those lines when margin is known; otherwise report billed euros and label margin Unknown.

**Evidence harden date:** **8 October 2026**. Fill rate = new cases on or after that date with Competitor Price or a typed reason code ÷ all new cases on or after that date. Target for Q1 FY27 gate: **>80%**. Pre-harden backlog (217/218 blank) stays disclosed separately — do not wash it into the gate.

**What we will not claim:** a loyalty euro, a full-book ROI, or a promotion timeline.
