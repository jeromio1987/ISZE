# Design the Universe value-exchange model

**Design Universe as a progressive data-exchange proposition.**

Start from this principle:

**The existing distributor kickback requirement starts the data
exchange, but Universe must make the returned value useful enough that
distributors eventually contribute information because the exchange
helps their own business, not only because a KPI requires it.**

Do not assume a fixed number of pillars. AIM, PRISM and IDS may remain
core capabilities, but Universe may also contain shared evidence layers
such as distributor aftermarket intelligence, public competitor pricing,
tender intelligence or future operational data.

Design the exchange separately for:

-   ISZE
-   distributor management
-   distributor parts team
-   dealer principal / aftersales manager
-   dealer parts manager

For each participant answer:

**What do they contribute?**\
**What does Universe add to it?**\
**What do they receive back?**\
**What decision becomes better?**\
**Why would they contribute again?**

Develop a progressive participation model:

## Level 0 --- ISZE data only

What useful Universe experience can we already provide without asking
the distributor for anything new?

## Level 1 --- sales-to-dealer

What does the distributor unlock by providing dealer ID + part number +
quantity + reporting period, without prices?

## Level 2 --- dealer/network identity

What additional value is unlocked by validated account/site
relationships and IDS links?

## Level 3 --- commercial context

What can distributors/dealers add through structured explanations such
as competitor price, availability, tender, customer requirement,
existing stock or application mismatch?

## Level 4 --- optional operational information

Evaluate stock, backorders, dealer fleet/service population,
workshop/service activity, local competitor information and
tender/quotation information.

Do not recommend requesting a field unless you can complete:

> **"If the participant gives Universe X, Universe can return Y, which
> helps them decide Z."**

Include the concept of a **data dividend**: every additional
contribution should generate an identifiable return to the contributor.

Finish with the minimum viable exchange, the strongest long-term
exchange, and the data requests we should explicitly avoid for now.
