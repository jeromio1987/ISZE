## 2. What changed from version 0.1, and why

Four substantive additions, each of which was a specific finding in the review of v0.1.

**The response obligation is now a chapter of its own (B6).** v0.1 never says who answers a distributor's
question, in what time, or with what capacity. B6 gives the five response classes, states that an
acknowledgement is not an answer, refuses to invent response-time targets, states the capacity condition,
and explains why the failure is structural rather than accidental. It carries the two verifying facts: the
competitive-quote programme name returns no hits across all 538,346 billing rows, and the mileage question
to the ITUK Parts Director, drafted on 18 September 2026, is still unsent.

**The kickback position is stated once and honestly (D2).** v0.1 leans the recurring data supply on the
existing condition and then runs a footnote saying the standard is not a statement of existing terms.
D2 says what the condition buys — a file, and not a file anyone cares about — names the three reasons a
distributor contributes, and states that the commercial terms are not confirmed by any file held. It does
not hedge.

**The one-column ask is on the page (G1).** The dealer-name-to-account-number request now appears as a
decision row with its owner and the smallest response that closes it, and it is written out in full as it
could be sent, together with the acceptance test and the list of things the ask must not contain.

**The counted-versus-modelled distinction has its own chapter (C6).** v0.1 implies it. C6 states it, and
in doing so removes a sentence that was in the earlier material: "every figure here is a floor" is wrong,
because counts and observed monetary totals are floors while rates and expectations are not.

Three register changes: the chapter plan leads with the business rather than with an illustrative part
family; the writing is specification-shaped throughout (fields, owners, cadences, validity rules,
acceptance tests, screen states); and the banned house words are swept, including the one term that leaked
into v0.1.

## 5. Corrections made during the verification pass

Two, both on the banned-word list, both fixed in place and re-checked.

**"Downstream" appeared twice.** Once in the flow rule ("missing coverage in the second flow means unknown
downstream activity") and once in the wholesale rule. Both were rewritten to say the thing in words:
"activity beyond it is unknown", and "dealers further down the chain". The word now appears nowhere.

**"Fully verified" appeared once** — in the sentence that bans it. That is the one permitted use and it is
deliberate, because the paper has to name the phrase to forbid it. It was left as it stands.

**The sentence that would have looked wrong in public.** It was in the first draft of the flow rule: the
paper said a missing sales-to-dealer feed means "unknown downstream activity, not zero activity". Read
cold, that sentence can be taken as a claim that the flow itself is fine and only the naming is missing.
It was rewritten to say plainly that activity beyond the second flow is unknown, and that unknown is not
zero. That is the difference between a rule and a reassurance.

## 6. What is not verified, and what it would take

**The reader's test has not been run.** Section 8 of the master prompt asks that the draft be handed to
somebody who does not know the project and asked whether they can name what is built, what is blocked and
who owes the next answer. That has not happened, because it needs a second person. **This is the one check
on the list that is outstanding**, and it is the one most likely to find a chapter that is still a
principle rather than a specification.

**The paper has not been read as a printed document.** It exists as markdown. There is no office suite on
this machine, so no PDF was produced and no page-by-page visual check was possible. Table rendering, page
breaks and heading hierarchy should be checked wherever it is typeset.

**The two named documents are still not held.** The commercial and participation chapter is provisional
until the revised commercial model and the feedback disposition are supplied. No figure in this paper
depends on either of them, but the chapter's status does.

**The classification release is still undecided.** Appendix B forbids a family label being printed outside
ISZE until one release is pinned, and the paper itself therefore prints none. If the owner pins a release,
the paper can carry family names in a later edition, and until then it cannot.

**Two figures in the decision list are stated without a number and should stay that way.** The open
decisions are written so that each names what it blocks and the smallest response that would close it. No
invented estimate was put beside any of them.
