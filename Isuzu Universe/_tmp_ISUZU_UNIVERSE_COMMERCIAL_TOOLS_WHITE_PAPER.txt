A commercial edge, not another
support desk.
Universe should give distributors and dealers tools that change how they understand and
develop their parts business.
The programme is a portfolio of distinctive commercial tools built around Isuzu product knowledge, deep
purchasing analysis, dealer self-assessment and relevant market evidence. The user should be able to
discover a range issue, understand a commercial movement, compare alternatives or choose an
improvement directly in the tool. Opening a request for somebody else to answer is not the defining
interaction.
The proposed portfolio comprises the AIM Commercial Atlas, IDS Development Studio, Range & Campaign
Planner, Pricing & Margin Lab and PRISM Market Intelligence. The names identify proposed tool directions,
not a claim that every capability is operational. The tools connect when that improves a decision, and remain
useful independently when other evidence is unavailable.
The participant return comes first. A distributor receives a clearer basis for national and network priorities. A
dealer receives a useful view of its own supported business and development choices. Isuzu Motors Europe
(ISZE) receives better product and market understanding through an exchange that participants can judge
valuable in its own right. More purchases from ISZE alone do not establish that a dealer gained.
Standard availability enquiries, ordinary part and BVP lookups, routine quotation handling and day-to-day
support administration are outside this programme. They remain existing services, whether or not a
participant contributes information to Universe. Evidence from normal operations may inform an agreed
commercial analysis; their operating workflows do not become Universe modules.
The first proof is a useful commercial capability in the participant's hands: a better
decision, a relevant output and a credible reason to use the tool again.

PRODUCT / AIM
AIM Commercial Atlas
See what is really changing in the parts business.
The Aftermarket Intelligence Model (AIM) turns original part references into a navigable commercial range.
The Atlas lets the user move from distributor or purchasing-account activity into families, product forms and
exact references, while keeping the selected period and sales stage intact. It is both an exploration tool and a
way to isolate the products that deserve a decision.
A distributor should be able to explain which ranges strengthened, which weakened, which purchases
became concentrated and which recorded patterns changed. A dealer should be able to examine its own
supported purchasing profile and find the product detail behind an apparent change. Neither should need to
ask ISZE to prepare a new slide every time the selection changes.
The analytical depth comes from keeping several distinctions visible: value versus quantity where monetary
evidence exists; continuing versus newly active references; Genuine versus Best Value Parts (BVP); product
form such as element, housing or kit; and the contribution of individual references to a family movement. A
validated successor can be shown alongside the original transaction without erasing the code that was
actually purchased.
Recorded purchase continuity can be compared with the same account on a compatible period. A clearly
defined comparison group can add descriptive context, but it is not the account's required assortment or
service potential. A national-only source supports a national Atlas. Quantity-only information supports
quantities, composition and recurrence, not fabricated dealer revenue.
The result is a saved commercial selection: the source-defined product range, the observed pattern,
alternative explanations, missing evidence and a proposed choice. The user may retain the range,
investigate further, prepare a campaign, compare economics or take no action. The useful product is the
analytical capability; a support request is not the required ending.
A product-level explanation of the business, not another turnover total.

PRODUCT / IDS
IDS Development Studio
Turn the self-assessment into a practical development tool.
Isuzu Dealer Standards (IDS) should be a distinctive management tool rather than a questionnaire archive or
an action-chasing service. The user explores its actual assessment, sees the meaning behind each section
and selects a practical development priority. Suitable comparisons and commercial context add depth
without converting self-assessment into proof of business performance.
The first useful output is an understandable assessment profile: actual question wording, response, section,
questionnaire version and the available assessment period. Missing, unanswered and inapplicable items
remain distinct. An import date must not replace an absent assessment date. The tool should make strengths
as visible as weaknesses.
A comparison may show a dealer's position against an appropriate, permitted group only when the
questionnaire and scoring basis are compatible. The group and coverage must be explainable, and results
must not disclose another participant's identifiable position. There is no distributor audit score and no new
universal league table.
The commercial addition is an evidence-based development choice. A user can examine whether a
self-reported practice is relevant to a product-range or commercial-planning objective, identify the change it
wishes to make, compare effort and intended benefit, and retain the reason for the choice. A cross-source
link is a discussion aid, not a causal diagnosis.
Progress has separate meanings. Completing a development initiative shows that the agreed work occurred.
A later comparable assessment can show a change in recorded answers or results. A commercial
improvement requires its own measurement. None should update the others automatically. The tool
therefore remains useful before a second assessment exists, without pretending to measure historical
progress.
A usable view of strengths, priorities and development options.

Range & Campaign Planner
Build a more relevant commercial offer.
The planner takes an analytical selection and turns it into a proposed range or campaign decision. It helps a
distributor or dealer examine which products belong in the initiative, which participant or vehicle segment is
actually relevant, how the Genuine/BVP offer should be positioned and what would demonstrate that the
initiative was useful.
A product is not an opportunity merely because the participant has not bought it. The planner first
distinguishes no recorded purchase from a documented range gap, a coverage exclusion, a number change
or an irrelevant application. A candidate selection can be retained while those questions remain open; an
unsupported sales target cannot.
Genuine and BVP should be assessed together. The commercial task is not to maximise BVP share. It is to
develop an appropriate offer and understand the effect on the combined business. A campaign that moves
existing customers between Isuzu products is different from one that adds evidenced new activity.
Relationships, contents and technical applicability remain explicit.
The proposed output is a campaign brief the participant can use: chosen products, intended audience,
rationale, scope, commercial assumptions, resource commitment and evaluation plan. The system should
help compare alternatives such as retaining the current offer, targeting a narrower range, changing the
product mix or testing a scoped commercial condition.
Execution stays in the existing business. Universe does not become an email-marketing system, order
platform or routine quotation desk. It retains the analytical selection and baseline, then evaluates later
comparable activity. This connects product insight to business development without importing day-to-day
operations into the programme.
A targeted product selection and a testable commercial initiative.

Pricing & Margin Lab
Make the commercial trade-off explicit.
The lab combines supported competitive positioning with user-controlled commercial scenarios. It is a tool for
evaluating a selected offer or range, not a service for answering routine quotations. A calculation can be
useful before a pricing recommendation is justified, provided the assumptions and evidence status are
explicit.
Comparable evidence must establish product and kit contents, application, quantity unit, currency, tax,
market, date and commercial level. An online retail observation is not the same as ISZE net distributor terms.
Several offers from one seller do not become several independent market confirmations. Unverified
comparisons remain candidates, not price instructions.
The confirmed rule in the supplied AIM pricing component defines parts gross profit as revenue less the
parts purchase cost of the quantities sold. Freight, duty, overheads, distributor kickbacks and other operating
costs are excluded from that particular measure. A separate initiative-economics view can then consider
additional costs once, without silently changing the definition of parts gross profit.
Each option uses a stated customer or local-business perspective, effective period, eligible quantity and
unchanged-offer baseline. Show revenue and gross-profit changes together. A minimum-margin or
permitted-profit-sacrifice policy is an approval control, not an invented input. Missing cost prevents
gross-profit output; it does not have to prevent a valid revenue calculation.
The quantity needed to preserve revenue or gross profit is an arithmetic threshold, not a forecast of customer
response. A participant may test its own quantities or supported commitments without claiming estimated
elasticity. Sensitive downstream selling prices and margins are not mandatory contributions to ISZE: a
private calculation can return only a permitted aggregate or no shared financial result.
A transparent comparison of revenue, gross profit, margin and required volume.

PRISM Market Intelligence
Choose where to develop the business.
PRISM contributes the relevant market and fleet context to commercial planning. Combined with the product
detail in AIM and source-dated opportunities, it can help a distributor decide where to concentrate range
development, fleet engagement or tender effort. It should not become a live vehicle-support service.
The initial view separates measured parts billed, historical vehicles in service and modelled expected
demand. Spend per truck is a measured purchasing rate on the stated fleet basis. Target spend and service
expectations depend on assumptions and technical applicability. A modelled gap is not automatically
recoverable sales or a programme business case.
The product should connect a market segment to a relevant range and to the evidence needed for an offer. A
national vehicle total cannot be allocated to dealers by sales, visits or geography. A part that serves several
vehicle classes remains shared unless exclusive applicability establishes the attribution. A workshop sample
is not a dealer's complete fleet.
For tender intelligence, the useful output is a qualified opportunity shortlist: source and observation date,
actual scope, known deadline, relevance to the business and a decision about pursuit. The existing notice
source is a reuse candidate. No new tender collector or routine bid-support desk is required by this
proposition.
A tender's overall value is not expected Isuzu parts revenue; an award is not parts billed. Tender execution
and customer contact remain with the existing distributor and dealer organisation. Universe helps choose the
opportunities, connect the relevant product proposition and evaluate what became known. Future
service-economics or connected-vehicle tools require a separate source and benefit case.
A structured market-development choice tied to relevant products and evidence.
