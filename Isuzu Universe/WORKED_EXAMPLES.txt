AIM RANGE DECISION PROTOTYPE - WORKED EXAMPLES
Prepared 22 September 2026. All numbers are fabricated test inputs, not Isuzu commercial findings.

BASIS
One synthetic distributor; oil-filter service elements; 1,000 synthetic trucks in historical fleet context at 31 December 2025.
Reference quantities from calendar 2025 are replayed as an unchanged-offer assumption for 1 October 2026 to 30 September 2027.
Both alternatives use one fabricated forward cost basis valid for that entire period. Prices are ISZE-to-distributor net EUR excluding VAT.
The baseline is EUR 112,000 revenue and EUR 45,000 gross profit at that forward cost basis; it is not historical reported profit.
No assumed recovery or loss is presented as measured demand. The figures are not annual growth rates.

THE COMMERCIAL DISTINCTION
The two BVP examples both end with 600 BVP elements. Recovering all 400 added BVP filter sales produces EUR 6,000 more range profit.
If 200 of those filter sales instead replace Genuine purchases, range profit falls by EUR 2,000. The BVP headline is identical; the range result differs by EUR 8,000.
The second example needs 100 further assumed BVP recoveries to preserve range profit; that would also leave revenue EUR 6,500 above the unchanged offer.

THE PRICE-CUT TEST
A Genuine cut from EUR 100 to EUR 90 reduces profit by EUR 10,000 before any new recovery. With a unit cost of EUR 60, each further recovered unit contributes EUR 30.
The mathematical profit requirement is 333.333... additional units; orderable packs require 340. At 330, profit is still EUR 100 lower. At 340, profit is EUR 200 higher.
The engine does not infer that 340 filter sales are available, accepted, or economically caused by the discount.

LIMITS
This is a conditional calculation, not measured demand or a recovery forecast.
The unchanged offer replays the reference quantities using the same forward cost basis as the proposal; it is not historical reported profit.
ISZE-to-distributor purchasing does not establish end-customer demand.
The bridge reprices starting quantities before losses, transfers and recoveries; it is an arithmetic decomposition, not causal evidence.
Recovery and loss quantities are supplied assumptions, not inferred from competitor offers or service-demand gaps.
Declared source references and service equivalence are not verified by this module.
Support is deducted once at range level, not allocated to product lines.
Internal output includes costs and profit. Do not send it to distributors or connect it to a shared export without an approved audience-specific implementation.
The source references in these examples are fabricated. Live records and documented service equivalence would need separate validation.
This prototype is separate from the current component. No screen, existing calculation, saved case or distributor output was changed.
