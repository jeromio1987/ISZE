Financial scale

Not computable, and stated rather than papered over. The dealer-level purchasing file carries quantity only — no value column, no price, no discount. There is no price in the file to multiply by, so this page does not give the gap a euro figure. A number here would be invented.

For context only, and not this account's loss: the same five part numbers carry €26,170.09 of billed value across the whole booked network over the last twelve months (parts master, candidate release). That is a network figure. It is not a gap, not a leak and not a recoverable amount, and it must not be put in a business case.

Diagnosis

Something changed in how this account sources wheel fasteners, in or around August 2025, and it is specific to this account rather than to the part or the supply chain.

Two explanations survive, and the data cannot choose between them:

  • A stocking cycle that has been allowed to run down and has not been rebuilt. The five numbers are high-consumption fasteners; a workshop that holds a stock of them buys in batches, and a batch that was over-bought in May would suppress buying into the second half.
  • The requirement moved elsewhere — another supplier, or another workshop whose purchases are recorded against a different account name.

One reading is explicitly excluded, and it is the one somebody will reach for. This is not evidence that the dealer under-services its trucks. The account carries one of the higher self-assessment scores in its country field, and no link exists between a servicing workshop and a buying account for these part numbers — so the fitted-against-bought comparison cannot be made at all. The difference between the two is not a gap, not a leak and not a shortfall: they are two files with different populations and different units of account.

Confidence

Not a score. Seven named dimensions, each with its state and its reason. A single confidence number would hide which of these is missing, and in this case three of them are missing entirely.

Recommended action

Raise one scoped question, with a named owner and a review date. Not a price decision.

The question to ITUK's parts function: for Warrington Vehicle Centre, five wheel and hub fastener part numbers fall from 452 units in January–June 2025 to 40 units in July–December, with no activity after August, while the same five numbers continued across the rest of the network. Is this a stocking position that has not been rebuilt, or has the requirement moved to another supplier or another workshop? Nothing else is needed — no prices, no margins, no stock value, no customer detail.

What this page is, and what it is not

It is

  • Built entirely from files ISZE already holds. No new data collection, no distributor request, no new system.
  • Reproducible: every figure traces to one named file, one stated period and one population.
  • A real finding that nobody had asked for, found by looking at one account's shape rather than a network total.
  • An honest stop: it declines to conclude the interesting-sounding thing, and says what would settle it.

It is not

  • A statement that this dealer under-services its vehicles. That comparison cannot be made.
  • A euro opportunity, a leak, or a recoverable amount. No euro figure is possible from this file.
  • A price case. No price evidence exists for these numbers.
  • Publishable outside ISZE: the family assignment comes from a classification release that is a candidate, not a pinned authority, so no family label goes outside.
