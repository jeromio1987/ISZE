# 08 — Review brief: what to return, in what shape

Four blocks, worked in this order, returned under these exact headings. Keep each block to the point;
the value is in what you decide, not in how much you write.

---

## Block 1 — The page, one screen

Propose the first Imotors page: what a reader sees first, what three or four tiles carry, and what sits
below the fold. Answer the one question the page exists to answer, in a single sentence, before you
describe the layout.

Constraints you must design within, from `00_START_HERE.md`: sixteen dealers is the whole population;
the scores are self-assessed; there is no per-dealer purchase figure and no per-dealer fleet; Portugal's
own capture is 48.9%.

Say explicitly what you deliberately left off the first screen and why.

## Block 2 — How the sixteen dealers are read

The scores alone reward whoever marked themselves generously. Propose the reading that does not. Options
you may draw on, or replace with better ones:

- read a dealer against the **Portuguese market figure**, never against the other dealers;
- read the **section spread within a dealer** — a site scoring 96% on vehicle sales and 54% on premises
  is a different conversation from one that is uniformly average;
- show **which checklist items are weak across the network** so the reader sees a shared blocker rather
  than sixteen personal failures;
- put the market's 48.9% capture beside the parts section score and say plainly that the two disagree.

For the reading you choose, say what a reader would do next after seeing it.

## Block 3 — The gaps, named properly

For each of these, write **not in the pack** and name the smallest thing that would settle it. Do not
substitute a market figure for a dealer figure anywhere.

1. What a given dealer bought in a year.
2. How many vehicles a given dealer has in service.
3. Whether a dealer's parts performance is genuinely good or generously self-scored.
4. Whether a low parts score means lost sales or a low-mileage fleet.

Then make one recommendation: which single item is worth asking Imotors for first, and what exactly the
ask should say in one sentence.

## Block 4 — The one-line verdict

One paragraph, written for Jerome to read and act on. Lead with the conclusion. Say what the page can
honestly claim the day it is published, and what it cannot.

## Rules you must hold to

- **No invented number.** Anything not in the pack is written as *not in the pack*.
- **No league table as the headline**, for the reason in Block 2.
- **No per-dealer fleet, purchase, market share or sell-through** — none exists for Portugal.
- **Label the score as self-assessed** every time it appears as a number.
- **Plain business English.** No internal codes without a plain gloss the first time.
- **Do not propose rebuilding an existing dashboard.** The Portugal IDS comparison page and the dealer
  map already exist; this work sits beside them.

## The one discrepancy you may notice, and the rule for it

Two official Portugal files print slightly different scores for four dealers. The national ranking file
`Portugal_dealer_ranking.xlsx` gives 96.43% to the three Auto Reno Minho/Paços sites and 92.86% to
Amatoscar Portalegre; the dealer rollup gives them 96.07% and 92.50%. The order is the same and the gap
is under half a point, from the two files dividing by a slightly different maximum. **Use the rollup**
(the figures in `02_DEALER_DIMENSION.md` and `09_dealer_dimension.csv`), because its total and its five
sections come from one table and add up for a reader. Do not average the two files and do not switch
between them.
