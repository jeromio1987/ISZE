# ACCEPT-08 — Hand the running of it to someone else

**Task:** Transfer operation and decide the next justified scope.
**Participants:** one operator who has not run the tool before.
**Time:** one week of light use, plus a 30-minute review at the end.
**Where:** the internal build, on this machine.

## What this proves

That the tool can be run by someone other than the person who built it, and that the next
piece of work to do can be chosen from what actually happened in the week, not from a plan.

## Before you start

1. Write down, in one page, what the operator is to do during the week. Keep it to the two
   things people actually do: take a decision on a case they are given, and add a later answer
   when something comes back.
2. Hand over the running build and the two scripts for ACCEPT-05 and ACCEPT-07. Nothing else.
3. Read `CONSENT_AND_INTERNAL_ONLY.md`.

Do not sit with the operator during the week. The point is that it runs without you.

## What the operator does during the week

- Takes at least two decisions using the tool, on cases they are given.
- Records, each time, one line: what they were asked, what they decided, and anything that got
  in the way.
- Notes anything they had to ask you about, and anything they could not do at all.

## The review at the end (30 minutes)

> "Walk me through the week. What did you run, what worked, and what did you have to ask me
> about? And: if we were going to do one more thing to this tool, what should it be, based on
> what you actually hit?"

## What counts as a pass

- The operator took both decisions without you present, **and**
- the cases and their records are correct when you open them afterwards on your own, **and**
- the operator can name one next thing to do, and it is something the week showed them, not
  something they were told.

If you had to do any part of it for them, it is not a pass.

## The next justified scope

The operator's answer is the input. Write it down in their words, and mark it clearly as a
proposal, not a decision. Jerome decides what is next. Do not start the next piece of work from
this task; this task ends with a proposal.

## Record

`RECORDING_SHEET.md`, plus the operator's one line per decision (two or more lines).

## After

- Keep the operator's notes. They are the evidence for "transfer operation".
- The proposed next scope goes to Jerome on its own, in one paragraph, with what in the week
  produced it.

## What this does not prove

One operator, one week, on test data. It does not prove the tool is ready for the whole team,
and it does not give anyone authority to spend money, approve a price or send anything to a
customer.
