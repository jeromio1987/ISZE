# Consent and internal-only note

One page. The participant reads this before the task, and keeps it. No signature is collected
for the task itself; the observer records that it was read.

---

## What this is

You are looking at an early working version of the pricing decision tool (the AIM Universe
component). It is being tested before it is used for real work. You are helping find out
whether it is clear and whether it is safe to show to other people.

## What is in it, and what is not

- The **tender notices** and the **fleet and spend figures** are real, as of the dates shown
  on the screen.
- Two **dealer standards (IDS) answers** are real and named on screen: Ferndown (Isuzu Truck UK)
  and Astara Lisboa (Portugal I-Motors). Treat only those two as real IDS answers.
- The **parts, prices, stock and availability replies** on the cases are still test data.
  They are not real parts or real prices. Do not treat them as a fact about a customer.
  Other IDS answers, if any appear without those two dealer names, are also test data.

## What we ask of you

- Do the task as you would normally do it. Do not try to be helpful to the tool.
- Say out loud when something is confusing, missing, or looks wrong. That is the useful part.
- If you cannot find something, say so; do not ask the observer to look for you.

## What we record

- Your answers to the task, and any comments you make.
- What you clicked or opened, if the observer is watching you use it.
- Nothing is recorded about you personally beyond your name and role, which you give us.

## What we do not do

- We do not send anything to a customer or a distributor from this test.
- We do not change prices, lists or the live system.
- We do not record your screen or audio unless you are told first, on this page, which task.

## Internal only

This is not a public product. The version you are seeing must not be forwarded, copied to a
customer, or shown outside ISZE. Screenshots are for internal review only.
