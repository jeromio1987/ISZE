# Acceptance — how to run the five person-dependent tasks

Written for Jerome. Internal only. Nothing here has been run yet; these are the scripts and
sheets to hand to real participants.

**Read this first.** The tool today is proven on made-up test data. Two real feeds are now
connected in the internal build (the tender feed and national fleet figures), but the parts,
prices, IDS evidence, stock and availability replies on the cases are still the supplied
test pack. A participant must be told that before they start.

## Order

Run them in this order. Each one assumes the one before it.

| # | Task | What it is | Participants |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ACCEPT-04 | Look at the real participant-facing brief and say whether anything private is in it | 1 reviewer |
| 2 | ACCEPT-05 | Take one real internal decision from frame to call, end to end | 1 analyst |
| 3 | ACCEPT-06 | Two different participant tasks, observed | 2 people, one task each |
| 4 | ACCEPT-07 | A later exchange on a decision already taken, and its outcome reviewed | 2 people, already briefed |
| 5 | ACCEPT-08 | Hand the running of it to someone else for a week | 1 operator |

Do not start a later one to "save time". ACCEPT-06 depends on the two tasks in ACCEPT-05
having worked; ACCEPT-07 depends on 06; ACCEPT-08 depends on 07.

## Who picks the people

Jerome. The name fields are left blank on purpose. The only constraint: the ACCEPT-04
reviewer must not be the person who built or briefed the case, or the check proves nothing.

## What a pass is not

A pass is not "they liked it". Every task states, in one line, what is observed and what
counts as a pass. A task where the participant was helped through it, or where the observer
answered a question for them, is not a pass — record it as such.
