# ACCEPT-06 — Two different participant tasks, observed

**Task:** Prepare and observe the two different participant tasks.
**Participants:** two people, one task each. They are two different roles, not two tries at the
same job.
**Time:** about 40 minutes each, run separately.
**Where:** the internal build, on this machine.

## What this proves

That the tool works for the two kinds of person it is meant to serve, not only for the analyst
who built the case: someone who **makes the call**, and someone who **prepares the evidence**
for the call.

## The two tasks

Give each participant only their own sheet. Do not read the other person's sheet to them.

**Person 1 — the decision maker.** They are handed a finished case and asked to take the call.
They do not open the underlying evidence; they judge from what is on the decision view.

> "Here is a case someone else prepared. Take the decision. Tell me what you would sign off,
> and what you would send back if anything."

**Person 2 — the evidence preparer.** They are given the raw question and asked to build the
case so that Person 1 could decide.

> "Build this case so that someone else could take the decision from it. Tell me what you
> needed, where you got it, and anything you could not get."

Run Person 2 first if you can, so that Person 1 has a case they did not build. If Person 1
sees a case they built themselves, record that on the sheet — it weakens the check.

## The two questions to keep separate

These are the two participant tasks the roadmap means, and they must be judged apart:

1. Can a decision maker take a call from a prepared case, unaided?
2. Can a preparer build a case someone else can decide from, and say honestly what is missing?

## What counts as a pass

- Person 1 reaches a call without the observer's help, **and** names at least one thing they
  would send back, **and**
- Person 2 produces a case that Person 1 could use, **and** names at least one thing they
  could not get.

Both must pass. One pass and one fail is a fail of this task, and a finding about which of the
two roles the tool does not serve.

## Record

One `RECORDING_SHEET.md` per person. On each, note whether the other person's sheet was read to
them (it must not be).

## After

- Keep both cases. ACCEPT-07 uses them: a later exchange on the decision, and a review of what
  actually happened.
- If Person 1 needed the observer, do not start ACCEPT-07.

## What this does not prove

Two people, one case each. It does not prove the tool holds up across the whole team, and the
cases still carry test parts and prices.
