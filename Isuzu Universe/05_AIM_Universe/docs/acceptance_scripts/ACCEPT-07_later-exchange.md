# ACCEPT-07 — A later exchange, and the outcome reviewed

**Task:** Prove a later exchange and review evidenced outcomes.
**Participants:** the same two people as ACCEPT-06, already briefed on the decision.
**Time:** about 30 minutes, at least a day after ACCEPT-06.
**Where:** the internal build, on this machine.

## What this proves

That the tool carries a decision past the moment it was made: the case can be reopened, what
was agreed is still there, a later answer can be added, and the outcome can be looked at
against what was decided — without rewriting what was originally recorded.

## Before you start

Have the ACCEPT-06 decision to hand: the one-sentence question, the call, and the reason. Do
not run this on the same day; a later exchange should be later.

## The task, in the participants' words

> "This is the decision you took [yesterday / last week]. Something has happened since — either
> an answer came back from the dealer side, or the price moved, or nothing happened at all.
> Open the case and tell me: what did we decide, is that still what it says, what is different
> now, and what would you do about it? If nothing happened, say that too."

Then:

> "Look back at the call you made. Knowing what you know now, was it a good call? Say so
> plainly."

## What the observer watches for

- Is the original decision still exactly as it was? (It must be. A later note is added, it does
  not overwrite the record.)
- Can they add the later answer without losing the original?
- Do they say "nothing happened" honestly when nothing did, rather than inventing a change?
- When they review the call, is the judgement about the *decision*, not about the tool?

## What counts as a pass

- The original decision is intact and readable after reopening, **and**
- the later answer is recorded as a separate thing, not a rewrite, **and**
- the participants can say what happened and whether the call still holds.

If the original record changed when the later answer was added, that is a fail: history was
rewritten.

## Record

`RECORDING_SHEET.md`. Write the original call and the later answer side by side on the sheet,
so a reader can see they are two separate entries.

## After

- Keep the updated case. ACCEPT-08 hands the running of it to someone else and will look at
  whether the record is clear enough for a stranger to operate.
- If nothing changed at all in the exchange, that is a valid result — record it as such, and
  say in the notes that the exchange was empty.

## What this does not prove

One exchange on one decision. It does not prove the case stays correct over months, and the
"outcome" is whatever the participants say happened — the tool does not measure the market.
