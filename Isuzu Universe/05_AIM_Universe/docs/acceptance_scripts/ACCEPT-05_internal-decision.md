# ACCEPT-05 — Take one internal decision, end to end

**Task:** Demonstrate a useful end-to-end internal decision.
**Participants:** one analyst who does this kind of work today.
**Time:** about 45 minutes.
**Where:** the internal build, on this machine.

## What this proves

That the tool can carry a real internal pricing decision from "where do we stand" through the
options to a call the analyst would stand behind — not that it renders, but that it is useful.

## Before you start

1. Pick a case and a real question about it (a part, a market, a price move). Write the
   question down **before** the participant starts, in one sentence.
2. Confirm which parts of the case are test data, and tell the participant.
3. Read `CONSENT_AND_INTERNAL_ONLY.md`.

## The task, in the participant's words

> "Take this question and answer it using the tool: *[the one-sentence question]*. Work in the
> order that makes sense to you. Tell me as you go: where do you stand, what the options are,
> what each one does to the money, which one you would take, and why. When you have made the
> call, tell me what should happen next."

## What the observer watches for

- Did they reach a call, or did they stall?
- Did they use the position, candidates, economics, decision and action views, or skip some
  and say why?
- Did they find a number they could not explain, or a panel that said "not available" and
  blocked them?
- Did they ask you to interpret a label for them?

## What counts as a pass

- A decision is reached, **and**
- the participant can say why, in terms of the money shown, **and**
- they can say what happens next, **and**
- they did not need the observer to interpret the tool for them.

A decision that is reached only after the observer explains a view is a fail of this task,
and a finding: the view is not self-explanatory enough for the person who would use it.

## Record

`RECORDING_SHEET.md`. On the sheet write the original question, the call the participant made,
and their reason, in their words.

## After

- If the participant could not reach a call, do not start ACCEPT-06. The two tasks in 06
  assume 05 worked.
- Keep their one-sentence question and their call. ACCEPT-07 will come back to this decision
  later and ask what actually happened.

## What this does not prove

One analyst on one case. It does not prove the tool is reliable across people, and it does not
prove any number in the case is real — the parts and prices are still test data.
