## The ten challenges: answers that change the plan

**Give stronger than 'one screen'.** The first demonstrator must turn one selected AIM family into a scoped dealer purchasing picture, the exact source rows/coverage explanation, relevant PRISM context and an owned follow-up without re-keying the review. Compare that task with the existing workflow. If it only adds navigation, the slice fails the usefulness gate.

**Ask lighter than four new returns.** Require one recurring dealer/part/month extract in the agreed format, plus notification of coverage changes. Capture a single agreed action during the meeting. DLOD cases and tender feedback stay optional until participants establish their usefulness.

**Geographic fairness.** Map observed activity and validated changes, not estimated dealer performance. No euro-per-dealer-truck, no implied catchment share, no ranking of 'missing' family purchases without the necessary denominator. Unmapped accounts stay in the list and totals.

**DLOD conflict.** Separate observed evidence, requested ISZE support and actual support decisions. Do not collect dealer margins as an entry condition or produce recommended resale-price compliance. Historical case access requires an authorised audience.

**Kaizen surveillance.** Default to the distributor's own actions; propose that ISZE sees jointly agreed support actions and an agreed progress summary, not unrestricted internal personnel notes. The owner must approve access before external use. Do not create a network compliance league table.

**Cash without more discount.** Test one range/stock availability action at unchanged support terms, or stop an unsupported blanket discount after reviewing evidence. More actual purchases can improve ISZE gross profit if they are not just timing, displacement or price effects. Count only measured, finance-reconciled contribution after relevant costs; no guaranteed gain.

**Tender dilution.** Home opens the monthly review; tenders are a secondary panel and notification, not the landing hero. No tender leaderboard, pipeline euro from notice counts or feedback condition for core access.

**Dealer phase should not disappear.** After two completed monthly review cycles, inspect unresolved questions. Open a bounded workshop-extract experiment only if a repeated, decision-relevant question genuinely needs service evidence, a willing data holder exists and the access/use case is approved. Continue the distributor product without that experiment if it solves the questions already. Review commercial usefulness again after two pilot quarters; no causal claims are automatic at either checkpoint.

**AIM authority.** No live-data distributor demonstration until one dated release is pinned and relevant categories are checked. Synthetic interaction work can proceed now, visibly labelled. A classification source disagreement becomes an explicit exception, not a silently overwritten label.

**Closest-to-dealer claim.** Say 'better visibility into distributor-to-dealer purchasing', not direct dealer behaviour. The earliest dealer-visible artefact is a distributor-approved review brief with evidence and agreed actions. No bypass of the distributor and no claim of workshop understanding from parts purchases.

## Newly identified release risks

- The reported dealer context schema includes `uio_units` and `sellout_spu_eur` despite no verified dealer-fleet denominator. Quarantine those fields until their lineage is proved; a market fleet copied or allocated to a dealer is not a valid denominator.
- `contested_bvp_capture` requires a defined eligible product population and price basis. It must not appear as market capture, GPS or proven replacement of competitor purchases.
- The AIM export includes `l12m_billed_eur`. A classification join must not propagate this potentially wider commercial value into an ITUK dealer result or customer payload. Use a classification-only projection unless a separately authorised, scoped measure is required.
- Grain fields include `grain` and `period_grain`. Inspect them: do not assume all 101,341 reported rows are mutually additive monthly facts. Exclude annual control rows from monthly sums and reconcile annual controls separately where present.
- One dealer commercial account is not automatically one physical site. A name match, coordinate or country label does not approve a sales-to-site/IDS responsibility link.
- The historical verifier predates the proposed AIM export. A checksum pin and consumer-specific tests are required; a green older report cannot be inherited.
