## What Universe should do

*PRODUCT PURPOSE*

Universe should turn connected evidence into a better distributor conversation, without pretending that every source describes the same activity.

Isuzu Motors Europe (ISZE) supplies distributors, which supply dealers. The product should support that commercial relationship. It is not an end-customer shop, a complete-repair ordering service or a mechanism for treating every purchase as an observed workshop event. [B1]

### A useful return for participating distributors

| Review need | What the distributor receives |
| --- | --- |
| Understand the change | Recorded purchasing by relevant part family and period, with the source level and completeness visible. |
| Compare the range | Genuine/BVP mix and documented alternative-part comparisons, with application and price basis stated. |
| Review a dealer | Commercial activity, eligible dated dealer assessments and agreed follow-ups in one connected context. |
| Know what remains uncertain | Explicit missing data, unresolved identities, unpriced parts and model assumptions rather than false zeroes. |
| Act on the review | A named follow-up, accountable owner and next decision, tied to the evidence actually discussed. |

A monthly review should answer what changed, which comparison warrants discussion, what the assessment evidence says, what is still unknown and who will act next. A modelled difference can support investigation; it is not proof that a distributor or dealer lost that amount of business. [R1]

### Protected scope

Retain the portfolio overview and Genuine/BVP comparisons. Keep the sales cube removed and product photography deferred. Preserve corrected tonnage-v2 decisions and useful existing code. A dealer pilot adds a supported level of detail; it must not turn Universe into a dealer-only product. [R1]

> A roadmap task can close because the current product already meets the requirement. New code is not the default evidence of progress.

The product direction is a proposal drawn from the working roadmap. Current implementation is unverified. [R1, R2]

## Make every figure interpretable

*MEASUREMENT*

The label, period, population and calculation basis are part of the result, not optional footnotes.

| Evidence type | Permitted meaning | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recorded quantity | Signed purchasing quantity at a declared source level and unit. | Not proof of installation, servicing or end-customer consumption. |
| Reference-price valuation | Eligible quantity multiplied by a dated reference price, with currency and pack adjustment. | Not actual billing, realized savings or a value for unpriced records. |
| Actual transaction value | Recorded financial value under an explicit credit, tax and currency policy. | Do not add distributor billing to downstream sales as one sales total. |
| Spend per truck | Recorded parts billed divided by the eligible historical fleet on a stated basis. | Do not substitute a different fleet denominator without explanation. |
| Target spend / expected demand | Modelled expectation under stated fleet, service and price assumptions. | Not measured customer demand or a measured loss. |
| Genuine/BVP mix | Share of a declared eligible recorded population. | Not market share or evidence that all category parts are interchangeable. |
| No usable observation | A missing source, unmatched identity, unpriced record or other limitation. | Not automatically zero activity or an opportunity. |

For each measure, retain the distributor, commercial level, reporting clock, exact period, unit, currency, price basis, source version, eligible population and exclusions. Essential labels remain beside the figure. Further detail belongs in a consistent evidence panel. [R1]

The contextual billing data ends on 30 June 2026; its 2026 figures cover January-June only. The paper date does not extend that coverage. Each candidate pilot source must establish its own cutoff, and a latest-twelve-month view requires twelve eligible complete months. [B2, R1]

> Present any quantified shortfall with its euro or per-vehicle basis, part family or service category, and eligible fleet. When one is missing, record an investigation question rather than a quantified finding. [B3]

Warranty is evidence of claims, not scheduled-maintenance activity. Historical fleet evidence can be used within its stated limits; the unsupported forecast beyond 2025 cannot be used. [R1, B2]

## Connect evidence without inventing relationships

*IDENTITY AND APPLICABILITY*

A commercial customer, dealer organization, physical site and assessment record are not automatically the same entity.

### Dealer and IDS identity

Use a documented relationship from distributor to commercial customer, dealer, site and assessment. Retain source identifiers, relationship type, validity dates, evidence and approval status. The designated business owner validates responsibility; ChatGPT reviews the policy and supporting evidence, not a guessed relationship. [R1]

Country, submission cohort or a similar name can help identify a record to investigate. None is an approved production join. When an account serves several sites, its purchasing stays at the measured account level unless the source supports a site-level split. [R1]

IDS remains dated dealer self-assessment. A distributor view stays commercial; a dealer-assessment summary names its assessed population, scoring version, dates, section denominator and exclusions. It is not a distributor audit or a combined commercial-performance score. [R1]

### A commercial pilot does not need a fabricated IDS connection

The ITUK commercial review may proceed for a verified customer/dealer population while site-to-IDS links remain unresolved. That is a limited commercial-pilot decision, not approval of the full connected release. Linked IDS functions remain subject to their own mapping, comparison and navigation evidence. This rewrite makes that separation explicit in the task dependencies. [R2; revision R1-01]

### Tonnage and applicability

Keep three views separate: recorded purchasing of parts with validated exclusive applicability, modelled expected service demand by band, and actual market purchasing. Shared applicability stays pooled. A part fitment relationship does not prove which vehicle used the purchased part. [R1]

> Never allocate shared parts by fleet share, add market actuals repeated across band rows, or divide market purchasing by one band's expectation to claim band service cover. Preserve v2 corrections unless a reproducible regression is demonstrated. [R1]

Keep ITUK separate from IUK, and UMI separate from UTI. Exclude UTI from any comparator tied to the 2026 IDS round in which it did not participate. These are distinct populations, not country-level substitutes. [B1, B3]

## Make the experience distinctive and useful

*EXPERIENCE*

Visual ambition should make the commercial evidence easier to explore and harder to misread.

### Portfolio composition

Keep a stable, labelled overview of the parts portfolio. Let users move from a family to a readable detail table without losing the selected distributor, period or measure. Show Genuine, BVP and unknown classification coverage. If area represents value, it must use one stated additive value basis; it cannot quietly switch to counts. [R1]

### Purchasing time trace

Place Genuine and BVP on the same eligible time basis, with quantity or financial basis explicit. Missing-source periods break the trace rather than becoming zero. Selection opens the recorded parts behind that period. This is a purchasing history, not an inferred end-customer journey. [R1]

### Connected dealer review

Keep commercial evidence and assessment evidence in linked but distinct panels. Selecting a dealer retains the sales period. The assessment shows its own date and version. An unavailable identity link produces a useful explanation, not a blank chart disguised as a zero or a synthetic score. [R1]

### Reusable evidence detail

Use one consistent panel for source level, period, version, pricing basis, population and exclusions. Important qualifications remain visible without opening it. Provide keyboard access, reduced-motion behaviour and a table alternative; animation should clarify the selection, not carry hidden analytical meaning. [R1]

### A restrained visual system

Use the available roadmap board's dark-charcoal and Isuzu-red direction as a design reference, with clear hierarchy and readable supporting detail. This is not a claim that the unavailable original presentation theme has been matched. Evaluate signature views against actual user tasks before retaining them. Photography remains deferred. [R1, R3]

> No technology replacement is prescribed. Inspect the current architecture, components and measured performance before proposing technical changes. PRISM is a reuse decision to establish from its code and purpose, not an assumed new module. [R1]

## Judge evidence and usefulness, not activity

*ACCEPTANCE AND VALUE*

The product is useful when someone can reach the right answer, understand its limits and act on an agreed follow-up.

### Release conditions that cannot be hidden

| Risk | Required response |
| --- | --- |
| Wrong commercial level or value basis | Reconcile each level separately and state whether the result is quantity, reference valuation, billing or a model. |
| Uncertain identity or repeated rows | Keep unsupported links outside linked results, show exclusions and prove joins do not inflate values. |
| Incomplete time or source coverage | Separate unknown from zero, state partial periods and do not imply full-year growth from a half year. |
| Unsupported price or service conclusion | Require documented eligibility and assumptions; do not turn a reference difference into realized savings. |
| Cross-distributor disclosure | Test delivered data and exports. Hiding a control does not remove data already included in a file. |
| Refresh or release failure | Keep the prior approved data available and prove recovery before authorizing the intended deployment. |

### Measure the pilot on comparable tasks

Observe whether users correctly answer the defined portfolio, BVP and dealer-review questions, including their limitations. Compare preparation effort for the same review scope. Sample displayed and exported results for traceability. Count approved and unresolved identity links against a declared population. Record actual action follow-through. Set performance targets only after measuring the baseline. [R1]

Later purchasing changes can be monitored alongside price, stock, availability and other interventions. A simple before/after comparison is not proof of causality, recovered business or realized savings. Do not promise a commercial uplift percentage in the absence of supporting observation. [R1]

### Choose expansion deliberately

Select the next distributor through usable source coverage, validated identities, permitted access and a clear review purpose. Keep PRISM as an evidence-based reuse decision. Keep the product narrowly useful before increasing scope. [R1]

> Success is not the number of tasks marked complete. It is an accepted review capability, supported by reproducible evidence and used within its approved population.
