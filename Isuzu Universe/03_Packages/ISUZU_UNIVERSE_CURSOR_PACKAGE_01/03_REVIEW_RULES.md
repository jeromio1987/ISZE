# Universe review rules for Cursor

These are the applicable rules for this bounded review. They are not evidence that the local application has implemented them.

## Preserve the product

Keep the portfolio overview and Genuine/BVP comparisons. Keep the sales cube removed and photography deferred. Reuse existing architecture and processes where they satisfy the requirements. Do not reopen closed work or repeat obsolete tonnage-v1 feedback.

## Keep the commercial levels distinct

ISZE-to-distributor billing measures distributor purchasing. Distributor-to-dealer sales measure the next transaction level. Neither proves dealer-to-end-customer consumption. Identify the actual source direction before interpreting it. Conflicting uses of 'dealer sales' in older notes remain an ambiguity to resolve against source documentation, not permission to choose a convenient meaning.

Keep ITUK and IUK separate, and UMI and UTI separate. Do not include UTI in comparisons tied to the 2026 IDS assessment round in which it did not participate. Do not infer site responsibility from country, names or cohort labels.

## Distinguish recorded, valued and modelled measures

A recorded quantity is not euro. Quantity multiplied by a reference price is a dated indicative valuation, not actual billing or realized savings. Actual recorded amounts require an explicit credit, returns, tax and currency policy. Modelled expected demand and target spend are not observed demand.

Missing sources, unmatched identities, unpriced records, filtered records and confirmed zeroes are different states. An absent purchase is not automatically lost business. Warranty does not establish scheduled maintenance or service-demand cover.

Give any quantified shortfall its euro/per-vehicle basis, part family or service category, and eligible fleet. Do not export the network service-demand euro total or other figures marked restricted. Do not reproduce background-only company targets or internal-only action-register amounts.

## Respect time and source versions

The contextual billing horizon is 30 June 2026. January-June is not a full year. Establish each inspected source's own cutoff; do not assume all packs share one. Keep calendar and fiscal periods separate. A latest-twelve-month measure needs twelve complete eligible months.

The historical fleet may be used on its documented basis. The unsupported forecast beyond 2025 must not be used. Do not average deliberately different external and internal service-demand bases. Preserve their labels and purposes.

Do not reuse stale figures when a superseding source exists. A dated verification report supports only its source version and scope, not a different pack or current repository by implication.

## Corrected tonnage v2

Read the correction log and proof first. Billing contains no observed vehicle end-use unless the inspected source establishes it. Attribute only through validated exclusive part applicability; retain shared applicability in a pooled population.

Keep exclusive-applicability purchasing, modelled band demand and market actual purchasing separate. Repeated market actuals are not additive and cannot be divided by one band's expectation to create band service cover. Do not allocate by fleet share. A modelled band share of a gap is not measured loss or a performance ranking.

Where the corrected pack specifies them, apply `sums_to_n_series_total == "yes"`, use `single_band_only` as the exclusive numerator and keep `also_fits_band` non-additive. These are review requirements to verify against the actual pack, not a claim that the files were read here.

## IDS and entity mapping

IDS is dated dealer self-assessment, not a distributor audit. Keep customer account, dealer organization, site and assessment separate. Source/canonical IDs, relationship type, valid dates, evidence, accountable approval and unresolved cases remain traceable.

A multi-site commercial account stays at its measured level unless the source supports a site split. Similar names may create candidates for review, never approved joins. Commercial-only review is allowed for a verified commercial population while IDS links remain unresolved; do not call that a fully linked release.

## Genuine/BVP

Use BVP as the user-facing tier name. Part-level comparison requires documented applicability, relationship type, pack/unit alignment, price basis and effective date. Category mix does not establish technical interchangeability. Price presence does not establish stock availability. Reference-price differences are not realized savings or total-cost-of-ownership results.

## Confidentiality and local safety

Do not copy raw line-level billing or a full price list into this package. Do not return secrets, credentials, unnecessary personal information, another distributor's unauthorized data or bulk source code. Prefer source manifests, limited aggregate evidence and synthetic examples.

Preserve originals locally. If an original paper contains restricted data, do not return it unchanged merely because this package asks for the original: provide its identity/hash, permitted excerpts and an explicit restriction. A redacted copy must not be described as the original.

No public links, external sends or commercial commitments. Any email remains a draft for the user. A client-side filter is not a data-access control. Report conflicts and limitations without silently rewriting the source.
