Internal · 23 September 2026 · what is in this folder, which document to open, and where the source text sits.

What this folder is

Isuzu Universe\ holds one programme, written up in more than one way. It is not a set of competing plans: it is a single commercial idea, put in front of different readers, plus the working software and the evidence behind it.

The idea is that ISZE should give its distributors and dealers tools worth using — a parts classification, a dealer self-assessment, market and fleet context, a defence for jobs that are being lost to competitors, and an early view of tenders — and in return earn the information that makes those tools better. The papers argue that case. The annex carries the design detail. The deck is for talking from. The showcase is the working screen.

Why there are several papers rather than one:

  • The white paper is the five-pillar evidence case: AIM, IDS, PRISM, DLOD and Tenders, each with its own worked example on real measured numbers. It is the long read, and it is the one to start with if the reader wants the evidence rather than the summary.
  • Universe 3.0 is the shorter commercial paper: the scope boundary, the five-tool portfolio, the money chapter, the participation assessment and the decision it asks for.
  • The annex exists so that nothing was lost when the 80-page edition was withdrawn. The tender framework, the IDS chapter in full and the reference rules live there.
  • The Yellow Paper is the operating paper underneath all of it: how the product is built, what surfaces a finding, how it is priced, and the stop condition.
  • The deck and the showcase are the same argument in two other forms. The deck is for a room; the showcase is the screen a distributor would actually see.

The current editions are the ones dated 23 September 2026, together with the Yellow Paper v0.5 of 22 September. Two earlier papers have been withdrawn and stay on disk for reference only; they are named in the footnotes at the end of this index.

Opening them

Every document has a launcher sitting beside it in the same folder, and the launcher takes its name from the document it opens. So the way to open a paper is to look for the launcher whose name matches it, and double-click that. The showcase opens in a browser; the rest open in the reader for their file type. This index has a launcher beside it in the same way.

Nothing needs to be installed and no server needs to be started. If a launcher is missing, the document itself can simply be opened directly from the folder.

How these documents are made

Each document is generated from its own source text rather than assembled by hand, and the charts in a paper are drawn from the same measured files the paper quotes. That is deliberate: it means a chart cannot drift away from the data behind it, and it means a rebuild after a correction cannot silently leave an old figure in place. Each finished document also carries a short record of what was checked before it was issued.

A reader does not need any of this to use the folder. It is worth knowing only because it explains why the documents in here are dated, and why an undated copy of a paper should be treated as older rather than newer.

Footnotes

The older showcase. One earlier showcase file is kept on disk on purpose: ISUZU_UNIVERSE_3_0_SHOWCASE_SUPERSEDED_2026-09-22.html. It is not the current showcase and should not be opened as one. It is kept only because it still carries the withdrawn 80-page paper inside it, which is the last place that edition survives in a form that can be opened.

The withdrawn papers. ISUZU_UNIVERSE_COMMERCIAL_VISION_3_0.pdf (80 pages) and ISUZU_UNIVERSE_COMMERCIAL_TOOLS_WHITE_PAPER.pdf (33 pages) are the two editions of 22 September. Both are superseded by Universe 3.0 and must not be quoted as current. The first omitted the scope boundary; the second stated it but offered a different portfolio. What the review cut from the first, and is still worth keeping, now sits in the annex.
