# Independent review of ZKK versus AIM for ISZE

Prepared 20 September 2026. Internal commercial information: use only in an ISZE-approved environment.

## The actual assignment

Review ZKK and AIM as alternative or complementary systems for parts sales analysis and commercial decision-making at Isuzu Motors Europe (ISZE). Apply the same framework to both and give a clear recommendation: which is better for which decisions, whether one is preferable overall, and whether replacement or complementary use is justified.

The user has already asked for this comparison repeatedly. Do not substitute a generic sales report, another proposed framework, an apology, or a request to restate the task. Deliver the strongest review the evidence supports now. Do not invent a capability or a result merely to choose a winner.

## Required answer

Begin with exactly 10 short executive-summary lines in ordinary English. Give the overall recommendation first. No numbered sections, file cards, file previews, or citations interrupting those 10 lines. Put essential source references after the summary and naturally at the end of the detailed sections.

Then provide a criterion-by-criterion comparison, strengths and weaknesses of each system, concrete examples from the same sales population, risks, and a final decision. Distinguish demonstrated results, documented design capabilities, your own inference, and unknowns. A conditional recommendation is acceptable when the condition is precise; do not disguise an incomplete empirical test as a completed comparison.

Do not assume AIM must win. Do not assume ZKK is limited to price classes, catalogue keys, or a broad technical hierarchy unless its own documentation establishes that. Do not assume ZKK and AIM are mutually exclusive. A more detailed report is not automatically a better system.

## Fixed commercial scope

Review all mapped ISZE distributors, presenting material findings by distributor. Keep ITUK and IUK separate, and keep UMI and UTI separate. UTI did not take part in the 2026 dealer-standards round.

Current L12: 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026. Previous L12: 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025. Billing available in this package stops at 30 June 2026. Calendar-year 2025 and partial-2026 context are not replacements for these periods.

Test classification alone first; then assess any additional application features with the same supplementary data supplied to both. Do not credit AIM with the benefit of data that ZKK was denied. ## Evidence discipline

Keep measured purchases, modelled expectations and inferred causes separate. Lower distributor billing does not prove competitor purchasing. Missing sales-to-dealer data means unknown downstream sales, not zero. Warranty is not evidence of scheduled-maintenance demand or dealer service activity. Original-versus-value share is product mix, not aftermarket share.

No historical gross-profit or SPA-effect conclusion is supported without appropriate dated costs and agreement identification. An average billed-price change is not necessarily a list-price change or a causal price effect. Do not use the one-date price list retrospectively to label price leakage. Do not use fleet forecasts beyond 2025. Do not divide a dealer's sales by the entire national fleet and describe it as that dealer's effectiveness.

Every monetary finding needs a date, population and basis. Every demand gap needs its euro valuation, part family and relevant fleet, with assumptions identified. Do not externally sum euro service-demand gaps. The screened service file intentionally blanks non-quotable euro cells. Do not reconstruct restricted totals.

Conclude with the recommendation, the most important supporting reasons, and any conditions that could change it.
