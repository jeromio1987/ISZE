# Supplied source summaries

Read the manifest for the original source name, modifications and checksum. Most source files are unchanged; original legacy column names are retained. Filenames containing 'sellout' do not resolve the commercial channel on their own. Use the source's grain, scope and explanatory fields, and write 'sales to dealer' where that is the actual stage.

Annual and partial-year tables retain their original periods. Do not call these L12 data. Do not automatically compare partial 2026 with full 2025 as growth or decline.

Family/mix rows can represent selected product populations rather than the entire sales book. Dealer euro figures may be valuations rather than observed invoiced revenue. The nationwide fleet denominator in a dealer table is not that dealer's serviced fleet.

The dealer geographic file is context, not a comprehensive sales account list; its Israel population relates to UMI/dealer standards, not UTI. Warranty is secondary context, never a substitute for scheduled service demand. Fleet forecasts and non-quotable service-gap euro values have been screened as recorded in the manifest.
