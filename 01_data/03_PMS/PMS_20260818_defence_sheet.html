The eight questions you will get

1. "Why is fuel only 63%? We sell far more fuel filters than that." Because this is scoped to your N75 list. Your slide 17 puts 8981596930 (4,898/yr) on N35.125, and 1876100934 (1,544/yr) is on none of your four model slides. Strip those and N75 fuel is 7,415 to dealers. The earlier 95% counted N35 volume against an N75 parc.
2. "Where does 9,359 come from?" N75 units in operation, DVLA 2024. Not the 14,308 N75 VINs in your July list — those are cumulative registrations back to 2008 including scrapped. Not the 22,389 full UK parc either. 9,359 is the only one of the three that is a live parc.
3. "Why 1.25 services a year?" Your 40,000 km interval (your slide 3) divided into 50,000 km a year. The interval is yours; the annual mileage is our planning assumption. If your R&M data says lower, our gaps get smaller, not bigger — see sensitivity below.
4. "Your ISZE-to-ITUK fuel is half your to-dealer fuel. How?" Because you sell fuel filters we do not supply. 8975425390: 1,620 to dealers in CY2025, zero ever billed by ISZE. That is the ask, not an error.
5. "Where is the 47% clutch manual share from?" A DVLA-fleet convention, not VIN-verified. Your VIN list has no transmission column and neither does the CBU master. It is labelled a planning share on the slide. Weakest number in the pack — say so before they do.
6. "Why is the +1% smaller than the last version?" Scope narrowed to your 37 part numbers on request: 18,211 units instead of 36,843. Same method, tighter and defensible boundary.
7. "Pressure plate — 54 from ISZE, 9 to dealers?" Correct. You are holding stock on 8970388314. Both figures verified.
8. "Air is only 20%? That seems low." On the same 1.25 used for oil and fuel. If the air element is changed every second service the denominator halves and it reads 39%. Footnoted on slide 6. We need your interval to close it.
