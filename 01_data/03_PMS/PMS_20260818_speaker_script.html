Parts support for scheduled PMS

Thank you Duncan. What follows is the ISZE side of exactly the pack you have just seen — the same N75 fleet, the same forty thousand kilometre interval, the same four part families.

We are not proposing a second campaign. We are showing what ISZE will put behind the one ITUK has just presented — on parts, on price, and on supply. Ten minutes.

2
Contents

Five sections. Where this sits alongside the ITUK pack. What one percent actually means. How we build the coverage numbers, and what they say. The price route between genuine, BVP and SPA. And then the three items that sit outside the filter pack — urea, brakes and clutch.

3
The actions we will take

Four actions, and I want to be clear these are actions, not part families.

The filter pack is the primary one. Oil, fuel and air go out as one set on a service that is already booked, already in the workshop, already being paid for. We are not asking anyone to create a visit. We are asking that when the vehicle is on the ramp, all three filters go on it.

Second, BVP where genuine demand is quiet — a lower-priced Isuzu part rather than losing the line to the aftermarket. Third, SPA where price is the barrier, which in practice means the brake pad. And fourth, the clutch as a kit, manual transmissions only, no broad campaign.

4
One percent uplift on all PMS parts

This is the size of the ask, and it is deliberately modest. One percent on the parts the network already moves.

Not a change to the mix. Not additional services. Not manufactured visits — and I want to underline that, because Duncan's own principle says do not manufacture demand and we agree with it completely. This is better capture of scheduled work that is already happening.

One percent is not a stretch target. It is the floor. And it comes from the three actions on the previous slide, nothing else.

5
How the cover percentages are built

Before I show you any percentage, I want to show you exactly how it is built, so you can push back on the method rather than argue about the answer.

Three steps. Nine thousand three hundred and fifty-nine N75 units in operation — that is the DVLA twenty twenty-four figure, and it is a live parc, not a registration history. Duncan's forty thousand kilometre interval against fifty thousand kilometres a year gives one point two five services. Multiply through and you get eleven thousand six hundred and ninety-nine expected filters. That is the denominator for everything that follows.

Say this out loud — it protects you: the interval is ITUK's. The fifty thousand kilometres a year is our planning assumption. If your R&M data says the real figure is lower, our gaps get smaller, not bigger. We have deliberately taken the conservative end.
6
N75 filter coverage, calendar 2025

Now the answer. Oil at forty-six percent to dealers. Fuel at sixty-three. And air at twenty.

Air is the gap, and it is the gap on a visit we already have. The vehicle is in, the oil filter is being changed, the fuel filter is being changed — and four times out of five the air filter is not.

One note on scope. This is scoped strictly to the thirty-seven N75 part numbers in your own pack. Several filter part numbers are shared between the N35 and N75 ranges; where there was any doubt, we left them out. That is why fuel reads sixty-three here rather than the higher number you may have seen in an earlier draft.

If challenged on fuel: "An earlier version counted shared N35 and N75 filters together. We stripped them out. If the shared part numbers do count as N75, fuel is higher than sixty-three — so this is the conservative figure, not the flattering one."

ITUK filter sales, genuine and BVP

This slide is entirely your data. Year-to-date demand from slides fifteen and sixteen of your own pack. We have simply split it genuine against BVP.

Look at oil. The BVP already outsells genuine — sixteen hundred and ninety-three against thirteen hundred and thirty-eight. Fifty-six percent of your oil filter business is already a BVP part. So the BVP route is not a theory at ITUK. It is the majority of what you do on oil, and it works.

Fuel is eleven percent, and that eleven percent is one single part number carrying it.

And air is zero. Not low. Zero. The air BVP has never sold a single unit.

So put the two slides together. Air is the family with the biggest coverage gap, and it is also the only family where we have never once offered the customer a lower-priced alternative. That is not a coincidence, and it is the most fixable thing in this entire pack.

8
Price route: the lower of genuine or BVP

One rule, applied across the whole set, and it is simple. Genuine first — unless the BVP or an SPA on genuine comes in lower. We are not defending a price. We are defending the line.

Fuel: genuine already moves, keep it. Air: this is where the BVP matters most, for the reason you just saw. Oil: the BVP is established, leave it alone, it is working. Pad: there is no BVP, so an SPA on genuine is the only lever we have.

Here is the pack itself, with the part numbers and our prices to you.

10
Urea filter kit is retained

Short one. ITUK asked us to keep this item and we are keeping it. It sits outside the thirty-seven part numbers, as a separate line.

To be precise about what it is: this is the DEF pump filter kit, not bulk fluid. And it is worth knowing that this same filter runs across the whole Isuzu Euro-six range — N35, N75, and the heavy C and E trucks — and it carries over to P700. So the quantities shown are total ITUK, not N75 only. The opportunity is wider than this campaign.

11
Brakes remain condition-based

No promotional trigger, no volume pressure. On this we and ITUK are saying exactly the same thing.

But there is a touchpoint here worth naming. The brake pad is inspected every twenty thousand kilometres — that is from the IMG maintenance schedule. At the mileage we are using, that means brakes get looked at twice a year, against one filter service. The inspection already happens. We are not asking for a new one.

Both N75 pad numbers are now included. There is no BVP for the R90 pad, so an SPA on genuine is the only lever available.

Two things to land here: first, the rotors sell steadily, they come only from us, and they are not on the PMS list — we would like them added to the R90 brake pack. Second, your slide nine asks us to confirm the R90 pad so you can place the initial order. Consider that confirmed.
12
Clutch supplied as a kit, manual transmissions only

Deliberately narrow. No broad campaign, VIN and manual transmission only, and only on identified units until we have transmission data we can actually rely on.

The one commercial point: the kit is thirty-one percent below buying the three pieces separately. Where the workshop is doing the job anyway, the kit is the better line for everyone.
