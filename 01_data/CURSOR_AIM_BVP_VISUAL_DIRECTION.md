# AIM Parts Universe - BVP comparisons and authentic product imagery

Prepared: 20 September 2026.
Purpose: design feedback for Cursor while the tonnage reconciliation is being resolved.
Status: proposed next increment; no dashboard code or commercial data has been changed for this brief.

## Direction

Use BVP throughout the interface instead of "value parts". Expand it once as Best Value Parts in help or the introductory explanation. Use Genuine and BVP as the two comparison labels, consistent with the user's terminology. Remove the sales cube, its guided-tour steps and its navigation entry. Do not replace it with another decorative 3D data chart.

Retain the existing atlas, clickable comparison, period controls, evidence panel and permanent overview. The next improvement should connect authentic product images to the Genuine/BVP business, not produce another dashboard or a new colour theme. Preserve red, blue and white. Extract approved colour tokens from the presentation if available; do not guess official colour codes from website screenshots.

The recommended journey is:

Real product -> product family -> Genuine/BVP purchasing -> change over time -> exact distributor comparison.

The same distributor selection, date, category, part selection and source scope must survive each transition. At least one compact whole-portfolio navigator stays visible at every depth.

## Signature interaction: the Genuine/BVP ribbon comparison

Replace the detached two-tier totals with one clear composition: distributor -> family or category -> Genuine and BVP. It is a regrouping of the same billing, not a physical movement of goods and not evidence that customers switched products.

Use red for BVP and blue for Genuine as a proposed interface convention, subject to the presentation's approved tokens. Label the branches directly. Do not reuse those colours to mean good/bad performance in another view.

Give the diagram two explicit modes:

- Recorded euros: ribbon width represents euros, using the same absolute scale when comparing distributors.
- Purchasing mix: each distributor starts at the same total width; the split represents Genuine/BVP shares of the selected two-tier total. Show the actual euro total alongside each distributor so a small business is not mistaken for a large one.

In comparison mode, show two aligned, synchronised ribbon diagrams with a common family/category order. Select a family in either and highlight it on both. Clicking drills both views into the same category; Back returns both to their common parent. Keep the overall atlas visible.

If a large distributor dominates the absolute view, offer an explicit switch to purchasing mix. Do not silently rescale one distributor. Do not use a logarithmic width that breaks additive flow interpretation.

Ribbons should have sufficient hit areas, readable labels, restrained animation, and an exact-values table. Preserve original category ordering while the viewer is reading; reordering is an explicit action, not an uncontrolled side effect of every selection.

The share definition is:

BVP billed euros / (Genuine billed euros + BVP billed euros), for the selected scope and period.

This is a purchasing mix, not market share or service-demand cover. Label it accordingly. Unclassified or out-of-scope billing must be reported separately rather than forced into a tier. Do not quietly exclude a residual to imply that two mapped tiers cover the entire portfolio.

If the selected signed-billing basis includes negative amounts or a non-positive combined total, do not turn signed amounts into positive ribbon widths. Explain the limitation and provide a signed-value table or diverging bars. State the basis and reconcile any alternative gross-purchases view explicitly.

## Product comparison bench

This is the preferred visual centrepiece, using real photographs rather than generic geometry.

Place a Genuine part and its validated BVP alternative side by side with matched photographic treatment. Display exact part numbers, category, relationship source and any applicability qualifications. One product may relate to more than one alternative; do not force one-to-one pairs. A supersession relationship is not automatically a Genuine/BVP alternative.

Behind or beneath the images, reveal the selected distributor's billing history and Genuine/BVP contribution. Moving the date selector changes the charts while the product identity stays fixed. Switching distributor changes the evidence without discarding the pair or losing the portfolio position.

Where only category totals or part examples exist, label them as such. Do not allocate category sales to the photographed example. Clearly separate a family-level representative photo from an exact-part photograph.

For an approved same-part image sequence, support drag-to-rotate, a whole-part view and a true macro crop. A photographed pair should not imply identical dimensions, internals, performance or fitment unless those statements are independently supported. Do not generate replacement markings or redraw engineering details.

Potential initial subjects: an oil-filter pair, an air-filter pair, a brake-pad pair and a clutch example, chosen only from approved relationships and available physical samples. This is a suggested production shortlist, not a statement that those exact pairs or assets have been supplied.

## BVP movement map

Use a two-dimensional comparison of changes, not the sales cube:

- Horizontal position: change in Genuine billed euros between two comparable periods.
- Vertical position: change in BVP billed euros between the same periods.
- Marker area: selected-period combined billed euros where non-negative and suitable for an area encoding.
- Marker identity: the selected family or category, with persistent labels for highlighted items.

Use common, visible euro scales with zero lines. The four regions distinguish both up, Genuine up/BVP down, Genuine down/BVP up, and both down. Do not call these regions winners, cannibalisation, recovered business or aftermarket conversion: the chart shows purchasing patterns, not causation.

A rising BVP share does not by itself establish increasing BVP sales, increasing total sales or improved profit. Show the two euro changes and their combined change beside the percentage-point movement.

Click a marker to open that category in the ribbon comparison or product bench while retaining the current distributors and period. Any animated trail uses actual comparable periods; animation between endpoints is a visual interpolation, not extra measured observations.

Do not compare January-June 2026 with a full year. Offer an H1 comparison only when both six-month periods are supplied on a compatible basis. Otherwise show the half-year observation without a growth claim.

## Exact change explanation

Add a compact contribution bridge within the comparison, not a separate dashboard:

Earlier combined billing + change in Genuine billing + change in BVP billing = later combined billing.

Use the same distributor, period definitions, classification version and inclusion rules on both ends. Report residual/unclassified changes if the scope includes them. If classification or coverage differs, reconcile the difference or suppress the explanation rather than portraying it as commercial movement.

Each contribution opens the categories responsible. Each category opens the validated part-level aggregates where available. The bridge is an arithmetic decomposition, not evidence that a promotion or price revision caused growth. Gross profit stays unavailable until a matching approved cost basis exists.

## BVP purchasing breadth

Within a selected family, show a distributor-by-category matrix with distinguishable states:

- BVP purchases recorded.
- No BVP purchases recorded in a confirmed-complete observation scope.
- A BVP alternative or applicable range has not been confirmed.
- Source data is unavailable or incomplete.

Selecting a cell opens its Genuine/BVP comparison and the applicable relationship evidence. Keep the distributor as the commercial customer. No purchasing observation, by itself, establishes end-customer demand, distributor stock levels or missed sales.

Offer a clearly labelled eligible-range view once availability/applicability is confirmed. Keep it distinct from the all-family BVP share. Do not rank markets by an eligible-range rate before the denominator is comparable.

## Monthly purchasing pattern, if monthly aggregates are available

A compact monthly strip below the ribbon can show whether BVP purchasing is sustained, intermittent or concentrated in a particular order period. Clicking a month narrows the evidence in both distributor views.

Distinguish a confirmed zero from a missing month. Do not fabricate monthly values from the annual totals in the existing extract. This is an optional enhancement dependent on an approved distributor x month x category x tier aggregate.

## Product imagery: recommended route

Preferred route: approved existing Isuzu photography first, then a small, consistent studio shoot to fill the important gaps. Retire the schematic truck and generic component model if they cannot meet the quality threshold. A clear product photograph with a useful commercial interaction is preferable to an inaccurate 3D model.

For a proposed studio shoot, commission a consistent white or neutral background, matching three-quarter angles, neutral lighting, whole-part images, macro details and a photograph of the packaging/part label for identity verification. Consider an optional turntable sequence for a few selected products, rather than producing a full 3D portfolio. Agree reuse rights and permitted crops/cut-outs as part of the brief.

Keep archival originals outside the HTML. Prepare appropriately sized browser derivatives after approval, preloading only what the next interaction needs. The main view must remain usable without waiting for every photograph or rotation frame. Test the actual offline package on the presentation computer; do not claim smoothness from the choice of rendering library alone.

Use three explicit identity levels:

- Verified exact part: the photograph is linked to the displayed part number by an approved source or a checked physical sample.
- Representative family image: a real part illustrates the category but is not claimed to be the selected number.
- Unavailable: use a tidy textual or line-icon placeholder, not a plausible invented product.

Any AI-generated mood imagery would be a concept visual, never proof of a part's appearance, marking, construction or fitment. Do not create a truck X-ray from exterior photography. Photograph sequences also do not reveal unphotographed internal systems.

## Verified public source leads

Checked through public web pages on 20 September 2026. These are sources to request or inspect, not a cleared asset bundle. Individual file resolution, exact European applicability and reuse permissions have not been approved. Some direct image/category downloads could not be fetched in this session.

### Isuzu Commercial Truck of America - Media Gallery

https://www.isuzucv.com/en/app/mediaGallery

The official gallery lists Parts Images, truck/chassis images, logos and graphic standards, and documents high-resolution JPG downloads. Its published notice prohibits alterations and requires written consent for commercial use. Obtain the relevant marketing approval before embedding or modifying imagery; employment within the Isuzu group is not treated in this brief as automatic permission.

The US site also has a FleetValue range. Do not relabel FleetValue assets as BVP, or use a US vehicle/part image as exact evidence of the European product without checking identity.

### Isuzu Motors International FZE - Genuine and Best Value Parts

https://isuzu-intl.com/parts/

The official page contains Genuine and BVP product imagery and branded material. Use it to locate the original artwork and product photographs with the owner. Regional product positioning and warranty claims should not be imported into an ISZE comparison without validation.

The official page contains BVP imagery and identifies product categories including filters, clutch parts, bearings and water pumps. It is a useful source lead for brand artwork and photography; it does not validate availability or a Genuine/BVP pairing in the European catalogue.

### Isuzu Truck UK - Parts and E-Commerce

The official indexed page distinguishes Genuine parts and BVP and lists BVP product categories. It is useful for European-facing terminology and an internal request for original photography. A full current page/image fetch was not available here.

## Work that can proceed while tonnage is being reconciled

Terminology replacement, cube removal, linked comparison behaviour, ribbon design, asset selection, studio specification and layout improvements need not wait for weight-band data. Purchasing-mix and change views can proceed only on the already-approved aggregate scope; they must not bypass the current verification rules.

Tonnage attribution, fleet-normalised band rankings, band service-demand cover, and any additional profit view remain dependent on their respective validated inputs. This brief does not reopen or settle those questions. It does not copy disputed tonnage figures into a new design.

## Acceptance criteria

- User-visible tier labels, tooltips, legends, exports and accessibility text say BVP, not value parts. Internal source-field names may stay unchanged with an explicit label mapping.
- Sales-cube navigation, scenes and tour steps are removed, with a safe destination for older cube links.
- Both distributors and the same hierarchy position remain selected during all comparison drills and back actions.
- A fixed whole-portfolio navigator and Show everything control remain available, including beside product photography.
- Opening or closing evidence triggers a fit-to-available-space adjustment without clipped families or the earlier off-centre empty region.
- Euro ribbons reconcile to the displayed totals. Normalised diagrams clearly state their denominator and never imply equal absolute business sizes.
- Missing, zero, negative and unclassified observations have defined behaviour; none are hidden to improve the picture.
- Real product assets have recorded source, exact-part/representative status, owner and reuse approval. Unknown images are not published as exact-part photographs.
- Product imagery does not add invented fitment, internal construction, relative size or performance claims.
- Full-year/H1 boundaries and per-vehicle denominator labels already added to version 2.1 remain intact.
- Keyboard navigation, reduced motion, exact values and offline use remain supported.

## Recommended delivery order

First: terminology cleanup, remove the cube, source/approve one convincing product pair and build its comparison bench.

Second: connect that pair to synchronised Genuine/BVP ribbons and the exact change explanation, retaining the atlas overview.

Third: add the movement map and purchasing-breadth view when the approved inputs support them.

Do not start by commissioning a full truck digital twin. Prove the visual and commercial quality with one real product pair before expanding.
