# Wrong AIM labels on billed parts - proposal for the next release

In the AIM release of 11 October, 1020 billed parts look misfiled against their own description (EUR 2,077,222 in FY2026). I propose moving them to an existing AIM leaf. Another 1472 parts (EUR 2,071,180) are wrong or unclear but have no existing leaf that fits, so they need a name or a no from you before any release. The 30 pad kits stay in the separate pad-kit proposal and are not in this file. Nothing has been applied.

Oil and water pumps are the largest clean block: 50 oil pumps (EUR 151,920) and 25 water pumps (EUR 60,726) sit under Fuel Pump or Gasket; they move to Lubrication > Oil Pump > Oil Pump and Cooling > Water Pump > Water Pump. Six vacuum pumps (EUR 7,696) stay on decide - there is no vacuum-pump leaf.
