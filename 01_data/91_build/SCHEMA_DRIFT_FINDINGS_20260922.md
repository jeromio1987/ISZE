## Part 2 — the restored 2026 numbers (assessment only; NOT changed)

**Verdict: yes, they are misleading, and they now contradict their own row note.**

What the columns say today (18 of 22 rows in 2026; the four CIS rows carry no ceiling):

- `period_basis = partial_year` on all 22.
- `coverage_note` = "PARTIAL YEAR … do not read a capture % or traffic light off this row."
- yet `gps_capture_vs_max` carries 0.2321–0.6803 and `traffic_light_v9` reads red on 16, amber on 2.

The numerator is genuinely a part year. The 21 Sep regeneration did change the basis — the sales
column now **ties to `sales_fact` Jan–Aug 2026** (EUR 22,961,211 of EUR 23,004,094 across the 22
rows, +0.19%, seven months not eight), so the reading is not the pre-15-Sep quarter-year debris.
But the denominator `max_spend_eur` is still a full-year target (the 2025 target repeated), so the
comparison remains part-year over full-year. It was not made legitimate.

Size of the false reading:

- **Gap implied in euro:** EUR 36,848,548 (18 rows). Normalised to the same months it is
  EUR 25,836,917. Overstated by **EUR 11.0M**.
- **Per market, the gap is exaggerated by 12% to 330%** — Belgium reads a EUR 832,912 shortfall
  where the same-months number is EUR −53,291 (already past target).
- **Before the regeneration, the same 18 rows with the same ceilings** read 0.0684–0.2948, mean
  0.1729. Now they read 0.2321–0.6803, mean 0.4009 — a **2.3× increase in the apparent capture on
  the same rows**, with no sales basis change other than the two extra billing months.
- **Spend per unit:** the file implies −35.8% network year-on-year. `sales_fact` like-for-like
  Jan–Aug is **+4.9%** (EUR 22.96M vs EUR 21.90M, +EUR 1.06M). Mean false reading −39.5 pp across
  the 18 markets; worst ITUK (−16.2% implied against +36.0% actual) and Sweden (−29.0% against
  +21.0%).

## Surprises / unresolved

1. **The file's `coverage_note` and its values disagree on the same row.** The note says "do not
   read a capture % off this row" while the capture column is populated. Any reader who trusts the
   note is safe; any reader who trusts the column is not. This is worse than either alone.
2. **`gps_capture_ex_warranty_vs_max` is blank on every 2026 row** even though the 2025 rows carry
   it. The regeneration did not restore that one. So the containment is half-restored: one derived
   capture column blank, the other populated.
3. **`traffic_light_v9` counts 20 non-red on 2026 rows across years** — the red count is the
   reliable signal: 16 of 18 ceiling rows are red on 2026 versus 4 on 2025.
4. **The three distributed copies are stale and uncontained** — they carry the *old* quarter-year
   numbers (0.0684–0.2948) with no `period_basis` column, so they are misleading for a *different*
   reason than the live file. The BVDB pack copy for the 2026-09-23 meeting carries the *new*
   numbers.
5. **`enrich_gps_spu_v9.py` has no partial-year guard**, so the next pipeline run reinstates the
   same defect. A containment on the file alone will not hold.
