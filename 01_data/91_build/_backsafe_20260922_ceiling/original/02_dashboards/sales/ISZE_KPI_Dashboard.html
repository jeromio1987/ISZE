Sources. Parts billed and annual distance: the billing fact. Vehicles in service, spend per truck and warranty: the distributor spend file and the model-bucket fleet file. Capture and the uncaptured value: the capture-by-distributor file and the opportunity matrix. Growth split: the revenue waterfall. Dormancy: the defection alert file. Basket breadth: the billed-part file plus the goods classification. Exposure and price: the exposure file and the price-premium file. Competitive parts: the genuine-and-competitive mix file, the mapped-pair substitution file, the addressable-range file, the exposure-by-family file and the parts-mix classification. Sales to dealer: the six distributor sell-to-dealer files.

Definitions. UIO, vehicles in service: the vehicles in operation that parts demand comes from. GPS: parts spend per vehicle per year, in euro. Capture: units billed divided by units the maintenance model expects, on the same fleet. Uncaptured GPS value: modelled parts demand never billed, in euro. PMS: the parts model schedule, the maintenance interval behind the expected units. BVP, Competitive parts: the lower-priced Isuzu line that competes with the independent aftermarket; Genuine is the original-brand line. DB: distributor, the national importer we bill. Contested euro: euro on part numbers where a Competitive twin exists.
