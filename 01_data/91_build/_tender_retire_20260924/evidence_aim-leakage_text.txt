PRISM — AIM & BVP Leakage

Where genuine loses — by distributor, family and order type. Fifteen boards off the AIM classifier, the genuine↔BVP crossref, and the order-type (saty) split. ← back to Offset Insights · BI Gallery

BVP is Isuzu's own "Best Value Parts" line — a budget OEM range (Isuzu-format PNs), not third-party aftermarket. So "genuine vs BVP" below is internal cannibalisation of premium genuine by our own value brand, not loss to a rival. (The named external aftermarket comparison lives in Section D, the Eucon/AutoDoc probe.) Read the leakage honestly. The AIM scraper flags only ~1.0% (€313K) of 2025 revenue as direct aftermarket "high-risk", and it matches ~100% of revenue — so the scraper alone is not the leak story. The real battleground is the contested arena: €3.55M of revenue where a genuine and a BVP equivalent both sell, of which BVP already takes 36% network-wide — up to 92% at some distributors. "High-risk" and "contested" are derived from invoicing + the genuine↔BVP crossref; nothing here is attributed to a named competitor.

C+ · The down-trade truth — BVP is retained margin, not lost €
Two different threats, never to be added together. BVP capture is INTERNAL down-trade — the customer still buys from ISZE, just one tier down, so we keep the sale at BVP margin. The only real cost is the GP delta per unit (genuine GP/unit − BVP GP/unit). The EXTERNAL walk-out (Section D, named aftermarket brands undercutting) is the genuine-€-out-the-door risk. Sections A–C count BVP revenue; this section converts it to profit actually eroded.

On the contested PMS pairs that match into CY25 sell-in (genuine↔BVP crossref, SOrg IE40), BVP earns healthy margin — so the down-trade is far less painful than the revenue-share headline implies, and on oil filters it is margin-accretive (BVP GP/unit > genuine).

D · The price layer — Eucon / AutoDoc aftermarket benchmark

Sections A–C show where genuine loses share. This section adds the missing dimension: the actual price gap to named aftermarket brands, from the Eucon + AutoDoc scrape (eucon_pricing.xlsx → eucon_price_gap.csv).

Coverage — read it straight. Eucon benchmarks only 44 Isuzu PNs. But those 44 are real volume: €12.9M = 13.3% of 3-yr network revenue, all sold. Of them — 25 PNs (€6.2M) the aftermarket sells cheaper than ISZE genuine; 10 are the genuine side of a contested (BVP-equivalent) pair; and 8 PNs (€2.33M) are both contested and price-undercut. Everything below is limited to these 44 benchmarked PNs; it is a price probe, not full network coverage. Delta is the aftermarket avg vs ISZE IE40 list: negative = competitor cheaper. Two margin layers. ISZE GP% is actual CY25 sell-in margin (EUR Amount − EUR Cost, Sales CY25.XLSX, SOrg IE40 = ISZE→distributor, 42/44 PNs matched). Dealer GP is a flat 27% assumption (your input, not measured) for the downstream dealer margin — the profit the dealer loses when they switch to aftermarket. Loss is measured in gross profit at both levels, not just revenue.
