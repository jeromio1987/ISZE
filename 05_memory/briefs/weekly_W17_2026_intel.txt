EXECUTIVE SYNTHESIS  (≤400 words, actionable)
================================================================
This week's signal is convergent, not noisy. Three things matter.

First, the D-Max EV is now a market reality in the UK, not a
slide. Customer deliveries have started. That does two things for
ISZE at once: it confirms the Golden Window framing for ICE parts
(2025–2032 is now the upper-bounded window, not a forecast), and
it opens a second-order problem — none of our core tools (UIO,
PMS model, AIM, Competitiveness Dashboard) are yet tooled for an
EV parc. The size of the EV parc through FY2027 will still be
small enough to ignore for revenue planning, but large enough to
become embarrassing if ITUK asks us "what's your EV parts plan"
at Hillhead. We should have a one-page answer ready.

Second, the pricing competitive picture has sharpened in two
places. On lubricants, Texaco Delo UK pricing remains opaque —
which helps and hurts us: it gives cover for BESCO positioning,
but it also means the Lubricants BC v2 margin line is still built
on an assumption, and IML will eventually notice. This week's
deliverable: a current UK dealer quote for Delo Gold Ultra E and
Delo 400 SLK, fed into BC v2 before the next IML touchpoint. On
emissions parts, UK aftermarket NOx sensor suppliers (DPF Parts
Direct / Tranzparts / Redline) are actively substituting into
ITUK's Class F/J SKUs. This is a direct, testable challenge to
the high-margin tail of the basket that we are currently
defending on methodology. We should quantify it — not discuss it
— before the next ITUK exchange.

Third, Hillhead 2026 (23–25 June, Buxton) is now a fixed
calendar anchor for the ITUK relationship. The pricing discussion
with James Marsh will be read through Hillhead. Decide this week
whether the written methodology response goes out before
Hillhead (preferred — owns the narrative) or whether we let a
live conversation at the show carry it (acceptable — reads
confident). Drift is the worst outcome.

No change to the Kosha honest read. Nothing this week moves
FY2031 €52.1M from structurally unreachable to reachable. Keep
executing the reviewable deliverables: PMS, lubricants pilot,
AIM-to-pricing, fleet-age revenue model.
