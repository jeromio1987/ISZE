# Why €52.1M by FY2031 Is Structurally Unreachable

**Author:** Jerome Van der Pluym, VP Sales ISZE
**Date:** May 2026
**Grounding:** ISZE_Kosha_Execution_Record_v1.pptx · ITUK KOSHA 27_01_26 · PRISM pms_capture_by_db_year.csv
**Audience:** IGS / IML · internal use only

---

## The ask

IML Kosha / IX plan: €29.6M (FY2024) → **€52.1M (FY2031)**.

That is:
- €22.5M of additional revenue in 7 years
- **+76%** on FY2024 base
- **+8.6% CAGR** compounded, sustained for 7 consecutive years
- Step in FY2027 alone: €32.3M → €35.3M (+€3.0M, +9.3% in one fiscal year)

For context: ISZE has never grown 9% in a single year. The plan requires it repeatedly.

---

## Three structural limits

### 1. The Golden Window closes mid-plan

The ICE D-Max / N-series European parc **peaks between 2025 and 2030**, then declines as:
- New registrations fall (ITUK 2025 registrations: 987 units, down from 1,259 in 2024 = **−21.6%**)
- Emissions legislation (R51, DTU, VK05/VK11 homologation delays) constrains product availability
- EV / alternative drivetrain take rate accelerates past 2028

Parts revenue is a lagging function of parc age — it peaks 3–6 years after vehicle SOP. The revenue window is real and valuable. But the plan targets **€52.1M at the exact moment the parc stops growing.** Any plan that relies on parc growth for the back half (FY2029–FY2031) is building on a declining base.

**Consequence:** the FY2031 target assumes parc-driven volume that will not exist in the numbers required.

---

### 2. IML's own plan bakes in a 23% absolute GP decline

IML's plan projects:
- GP% decline: **−11 percentage points, FY2025 → FY2028**
- GP € trajectory: **€19.1M (FY2025) → €14.7M (FY2028)** = −23% in three years

This is IML's own trajectory — built into the Kosha model. The implication:

> **€52.1M at ~28% GP = €14.6M absolute gross profit.**
> ISZE currently earns ~€19M GP on €32.5M revenue at ~58% GP rate (parts margin).

Wait — these GP figures are IML's consolidated VP Sales GP, not ISZE's distributor margin. The GP compression is driven by FX (JPY/EUR), pricing headroom, and transfer pricing mechanics. None of those are controlled by ISZE commercially.

**Consequence:** even if ISZE delivers €52.1M in revenue, IML's own plan projects the business to be structurally less profitable at that point. The revenue target is not a proxy for a healthy business outcome.

---

### 3. Network leakage cannot be fixed by commercial action alone

PRISM data on ITUK (largest European distributor, UIO 22,389 N75, FY2023–2025):

| Category | Captured demand | Leak | Leak destination |
|---|---|---|---|
| Air filter | 20–28% | **70–80%** | Outside the dealer network |
| Clutch disc | 7–8% | **92–93%** | Outside the dealer network |
| Clutch pressure plate | 0% genuine | **100%** | Catalog gap — no genuine PN moves |
| Oil filter | 64–103% | Mix issue | BVP share 0–105% depending on year |

The air filter and clutch categories represent **~€200K–€500K/yr of addressable TAM at ITUK alone.** Even perfect capture of both = < €1M/yr incremental. That is a rounding error against a €12M+ gap.

The ITUK Connected project — which was ITUK's own plan to drive PMS through digital odometer triggers — **was killed** (ITUK KOSHA Jan 2026, slides 12–13): GATEX odometer data is unusable because it pulls MMU values, not IPC or Digital Tachograph data. The PMS digitization path at the anchor distributor is closed until hardware is updated fleet-wide.

**Consequence:** the PMS lever — the clearest commercial action ISZE can take — cannot close more than €1–2M of the gap network-wide. The €12M+ is not addressable through actions within ISZE's commercial control.

## What closes the gap (none of which ISZE controls)

| Action | € impact | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| BP_JPY stabilisation / FX tailwind | +€3–5M revenue equivalent | IML / macro |
| New geography expansion (RoW markets absorbing IE40) | +€3–5M | IML market development |
| Pricing architecture reform (class factor rebasing) | +€2–3M | IML / ISZE jointly |
| Network capacity addition (new distributors, fleet contracts) | +€1–2M | Distributor market |
| PMS digitization (dependent on Connected fix) | +€1–2M | IML product (GATEX) |
| ISZE execution (pricing discipline, SPA, lubricants, AIM) | +€3–4M | **ISZE** |

**ISZE's credible execution ceiling: ~€3–4M of incremental revenue over 5 years.**
Realistic FY2031 landing on current trajectory: **~€38–40M.**
Gap to Kosha: **€12–14M.**

---

## The honest position

ISZE is executing. The FY2026 pricing revision was implemented at 100% rate across 40+ distributors (+5.3% net). Lubricants are live (IML signed off May 2026, ~€442K revenue). AIM is at 93.5% classification. PRISM provides distributor-level transparency that didn't exist two years ago.

But a business can be executing well and still be structurally unable to reach a target set on different assumptions.

The Kosha number needs:
1. A revised parc trajectory that accounts for ICE plateau
2. FX and GP margin assumptions from IML that are consistent with the revenue target
3. An honest accounting of what ISZE controls vs what IML/macro controls

This document is not an argument against ambition. It is an argument against presenting €52.1M as an ISZE delivery commitment when the structural inputs are outside ISZE's mandate.
